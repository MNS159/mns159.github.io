Ukraine Strikes Russian Black Sea Naval Stronghold in Overnight Drone Blitz, Zelenskyy Says

International & World desk · anchored on AP

Ukrainian anti-ship missiles and drones struck the Russian Black Sea naval base at Novorossiysk in a “unique” nighttime operation, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Wednesday, as Russia reported downing more than 500 Ukrainian drones across the region. The attack targeted the last major stronghold of Russia’s Black Sea fleet, hitting air defenses, piers and other port infrastructure, according to Zelenskyy.

Krasnodar region Gov. Veniamin Kondratyev said hundreds of Ukrainian drones attacked Novorossiysk, Anapa, Gelendzhik and the Temryuk district overnight. An 8-year-old child was among three people killed in the region, Kondratyev said. Dozens of residential buildings were damaged, and debris from downed drones fell on the grounds of four industrial plants. The Russian business daily Vedomosti reported that the strikes damaged two of Novorossiysk’s three grain terminals, with one halting operations.

Novorossiysk also hosts the Grushovaya oil terminal, one of the largest international hubs in southern Russia for petroleum products, though no reports indicated it had been hit. The Caspian Pipeline Consortium, which handles crude exports from Kazakh fields in which U.S. energy companies Chevron and ExxonMobil have stakes, operates a pipeline to the port. In February, the U.S. State Department expressed displeasure about Ukrainian attacks on Novorossiysk, saying they affected U.S. oil interests in Kazakhstan.

On the illegally annexed Crimean Peninsula, air defenses downed 115 Ukrainian drones over Sevastopol overnight, said the city’s Russian-installed head, Mikhail Razvozhayev, adding that 10 apartment buildings and 25 private houses were damaged.

Ukraine’s air force said Russia fired 138 long-range drones and an unspecified number of missiles overnight, with damage recorded at 16 locations. In Ukraine’s southern Kherson region, a large-scale Russian drone attack killed two people and wounded two others, regional head Oleksandr Prokudin said. The attacks also destroyed a shopping mall in the city of Zaporizhzhia, according to authorities. Russia’s Defense Ministry said its missiles and long-range drones hit fuel reservoirs and infrastructure in the port of Chornomorsk in the Odesa region, as well as a bulk cargo vessel carrying military equipment and a tanker loaded with oil for the Ukrainian armed forces.

Zelenskyy said Tuesday that Ukrainian intelligence reports indicate Russian President Vladimir Putin is planning “an additional rapid mobilization of several hundred thousand Russians by the end of the year.” Russian lawmaker Andrei Gurulyov dismissed such reports as enemy disinformation aimed at destabilizing Russia. Putin, speaking Wednesday during a visit to a warship in the Pacific, threatened retaliation for Western seizures of Russian oil tankers, calling such actions “piracy and robbery” and warning that Russia would “be forced to respond in kind.”
