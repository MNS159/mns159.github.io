Hassabis Pitched AI Oversight Body Before DeepMind Leadership Reshuffle

Tech / AI desk · anchored on Reuters

Demis Hassabis, the co-founder of DeepMind, proposed the creation of a US-led coalition to act as an AI watchdog in the weeks before stepping down as chief executive of Google DeepMind on August 5, according to reporting by the Wall Street Journal. Hassabis, who sold DeepMind to Google for an estimated $650 million in 2014, warned on Substack that humanity needs to do more to prepare for highly powerful artificial general intelligence (AGI). "What we collectively do now will determine how the next phase of civilisation unfolds," he wrote. "By safely stewarding AGI into the world, we can enter a new golden age of scientific discovery and progress."
