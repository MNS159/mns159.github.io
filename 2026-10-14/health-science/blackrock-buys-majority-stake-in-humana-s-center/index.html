BlackRock buys majority stake in Humana's CenterWell clinics for $2.8B

Health & Science desk

Humana announced Tuesday that BlackRock will acquire Welsh, Carson, Anderson & Stowe’s majority stake in CenterWell Senior Primary Care, the insurer’s network of 150 Medicare Advantage-focused clinics, in a transaction valued at $2.8 billion.

The deal marks the exit of private equity firm Welsh Carson, which made its initial investment in CenterWell more than six years ago and has poured at least $1.8 billion into the venture since 2020. BlackRock will take over as the majority owner.

Over the next two years, Humana said the transaction will help fund existing clinic operations, support the addition of new clinics, and enable other strategic acquisitions. The clinics are part of Humana’s CenterWell brand, which focuses on primary care for Medicare Advantage beneficiaries.

The move reflects a typical private equity timeline, with Welsh Carson exiting after a six-year-plus holding period.
