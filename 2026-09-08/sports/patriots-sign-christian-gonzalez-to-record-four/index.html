Patriots sign Christian Gonzalez to record four-year, $135 million extension

Sports desk

The New England Patriots and star cornerback Christian Gonzalez have agreed to a four-year, $135 million contract extension, resetting the market for his position less than two days before the start of the NFL season, according to multiple reports. The deal includes $102 million in guaranteed money and makes Gonzalez the highest-paid cornerback in league history by average annual value, as well as the highest-paid player ever to wear a Patriots uniform.

Gonzalez, 25, was selected 17th overall in the 2023 NFL Draft and has become an All-Pro cornerstone for New England. He was a major factor in the Patriots' playoff run last season, which included a game-clinching interception in the AFC Championship. The extension resolves months of looming contract negotiations that threatened to distract a team preparing for a highly anticipated Super Bowl LX rematch against the Seattle Seahawks on Wednesday night.

Gonzalez did not participate in the Patriots' voluntary OTA sessions during the offseason but did attend the team's minicamp in June. Team owner Robert Kraft had publicly expressed his desire to keep the Pro Bowl talent in New England for years to come. On July 25, Kraft told reporters, 'We love Christian Gonzalez. We love him as a player, as a person. That's why we made an offer to him that makes him the highest-paid player ever to play that position in the NFL, and coincidentally the highest contract in the Patriots franchise history.'

Early Tuesday morning, the Patriots' official X account appeared to acknowledge the new deal with a photo of Gonzalez. The extension officially makes Gonzalez the highest-paid cornerback in league history and resets the market for the position.

Sources: CBS Sports
