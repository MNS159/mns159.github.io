Antonio Rüdiger announces end of international career with Germany

Sports desk

Antonio Rüdiger, the 33-year-old Real Madrid defender, announced on Tuesday the end of his international career with the German national team, weeks after the 2026 World Cup, which served as his final tournament with the Mannschaft.

Rüdiger made his international debut in 2014 and went on to earn 86 caps for Germany, scoring three goals. Over his 12-year tenure, he played at six major tournaments and helped Germany win the 2017 Confederations Cup, though he did not secure a senior title.

In a statement, Rüdiger said: 'After some thought, the time has come for me to end my national team career and clear the way for the young generation. When I wore the DFB jersey for the first time in 2014, I could not have imagined it in my dreams that it would turn into 86 national games.'

He added: 'For me, a new chapter starts now, in which I will concentrate completely on my club. Thanks for travelling with me.'

The announcement, first reported by Sky Germany, marks the end of a journey that began 12 years ago. Rüdiger will now focus entirely on his club career with Real Madrid.
