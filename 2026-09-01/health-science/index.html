Measles deaths in Pennsylvania spark feud among politicians and health officials

Health & Science desk

Pennsylvania officials this week reported that two unvaccinated residents died after being infected with measles, with Gov. Josh Shapiro stressing that vaccinations could have averted the deaths. The announcement quickly came under fire from U.S. Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., a Trump administration appointee, who said in a social post the deaths "may even have been altogether fabricated." The dispute has ignited a public feud among politicians and health officials over the role of the contagious disease in the fatalities.
