Israel says senior Hamas member captured as Gaza raid and strikes kill three children, woman

International & World desk

Israel's defence minister says a senior member of the Palestinian armed group Hamas has been captured, after reports of a raid by Israeli special forces and an Israel-backed Palestinian militia in Gaza City. Israel Katz, speaking after meeting Israelis living in Netiv HaAsara next to northern Gaza, said the activity of Israeli forces in the territory 'continues at all times'. 'A short time ago, a senior Hamas terrorist was arrested – and this is our policy: we do not wait for threats, but pursue Hamas terrorists, thwart them and destroy their terror infrastructures,' he added. He did not name the commander or say who had captured him.

Palestinians in a crowded part of western Gaza City had earlier reported a heavy Israeli bombardment, with two vehicles hit and turned to wreckage. Videos captured chaotic scenes, with the sound of gunfire and the aftermath of strikes. Reports say Israeli special forces and pro-Israeli Palestinian militiamen had entered the area, but their presence was discovered. Some Israeli media suggest that only a local armed militia operated on the ground.

A Palestinian security source told the BBC the target of the Israeli operation was a senior official in the Hamas internal security service. Ismail al-Thawabta, a spokesman for Gaza's Hamas-run government media office, said: 'An Israeli special forces unit attempted to carry out a security operation in Gaza City, but the unit was exposed while carrying out the mission.' He added that the ensuing Israeli strikes 'resulted in a number of people being killed and others injured, in addition to the deaths of several members of the special forces unit'.

During the same incident, Israeli air strikes killed at least two children and a woman, local medics say. A third child was killed in a separate incident in central Gaza. The Israeli military has only confirmed striking several targets in Gaza 'in order to remove threats' to its troops, adding that none of its troops were injured.

The Gaza war was triggered by the Hamas-led attack on southern Israel on 7 October 2023, when about 1,200 people were killed and 251 others were taken hostage. Since then, more than 73,460 people have been killed in Gaza, according to the territory's Hamas-run health ministry, whose figures are considered reliable by the UN.
