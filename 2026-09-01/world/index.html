'I've never been to Afghanistan': Six million deportees forced to start over under the Taliban

International & World desk

More than six million Afghans have been forced to return to Afghanistan over the past three years, the largest cross-border movement of people anywhere in the world in recent history, according to the UN Refugee Agency (UNHCR). The deportations from neighbouring Pakistan and Iran have accelerated in 2025, with an additional one million people expelled since January as the two countries crack down on undocumented migrants. Many returnees have never set foot in Afghanistan before, including 35-year-old Nazia, who was born in Pakistan and crossed the Torkham border with her two children. 'But I've never been to Afghanistan before and many people here don't even have enough bread to eat,' she said.

Nepal death toll crosses 1,000 as rescuers race to find workers trapped in mud-filled tunnels

International & World desk

The death toll from devastating floods in Nepal has crossed 1,000, the country's disaster agency said, as rescuers race to find more than 600 workers believed to be trapped in a vast network of mud-filled tunnels at hydropower sites along the Trishuli River. More than 3,900 people remain missing overall. Only 4% of the 900 bodies recovered so far have been identified, a spokesperson from the Ministry of Health told BBC Nepali.

Sexual assaults happening almost every day in Ceuta, prosecutors say

International & World desk

Spanish prosecutors have recorded 23 sexual assaults, including nine against children, in Ceuta since the mass influx of migrants into the Spanish exclave at the end of July, meaning there has been almost one case every day, chief prosecutor Silvia Rojas said. Rojas told El País that migrant girls were 'particularly vulnerable because of their irregular status in the territory.' She said the influx had led to an exponential increase in sexual assault and that many victims did not file a report, either out of fear or because of their 'current situation in Ceuta.'

International & World desk

Israel's defence minister says a senior member of the Palestinian armed group Hamas has been captured, after reports of a raid by Israeli special forces and an Israel-backed Palestinian militia in Gaza City.
