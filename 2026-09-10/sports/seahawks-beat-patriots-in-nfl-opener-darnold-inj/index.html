Seahawks rally past Patriots 13-10 in NFL opener; Darnold injured, Maye throws three fourth-quarter interceptions

Sports desk

The defending champion Seattle Seahawks overcame a 10-0 deficit and the loss of starting quarterback Sam Darnold to a hip injury on their opening drive to defeat the New England Patriots 13-10 in the 2026 NFL season opener Wednesday night at Lumen Field. Backup quarterback Drew Lock led the comeback, while Patriots quarterback Drake Maye threw three interceptions in the fourth quarter, including a game-sealing pick in the end zone with 21 seconds remaining.

Darnold was injured on Seattle's first possession and did not return. Head coach Mike Macdonald said initial tests indicated Darnold had not fractured his hip. Lock, who took over and struggled early, completed the key play of the game on a fourth-and-1 from the Patriots' 45-yard line with under 12 minutes to play. He found wide receiver Jaxon Smith-Njigba on a play-action pass, and Smith-Njigba — last season's offensive player of the year — raced 45 yards for a touchdown that tied the game at 10. Lock admitted after the game that he had never practiced that specific play.

The Patriots had taken a 10-0 lead in the second quarter on a touchdown catch by rookie tight end Eli Raridon, the first touchdown of the NFL season. New England's defense limited Seattle to three points through three quarters, and Maye completed 17 of 22 passes for a touchdown and no interceptions in the first three periods. But the game unraveled in the fourth quarter. Seahawks cornerback Nehemiah Pritchett intercepted a Maye pass deflected by rookie Rodney Thomas to set up Lock's touchdown pass. After Julian Love picked off Maye on the next possession, kicker Jason Myers hit a 26-yard field goal to give Seattle a 13-10 lead.

Maye's third interception came with the Patriots driving for a potential game-tying or winning score. From well within field-goal range with 21 seconds left, Maye threw a pass toward Mack Hollins that was intercepted in the end zone by Seahawks defensive back Josh Jobe. Maye finished 23 of 33 for 178 yards, one touchdown and three interceptions. According to OptaSTATS, the Patriots became the first NFL team in the last 30 years to record at least three interceptions, three sacks, three penalties and zero points in a single quarter. Maye called his final interception "unacceptable," adding, "We could have sent it to overtime at the worst, and the only thing I could do was turn the ball over." Patriots head coach Mike Vrabel said, "You can't throw three interceptions. Drake knows that."

The Patriots also lost star wide receiver A.J. Brown to an ankle injury during the game. New England's rushing attack was ineffective, and the team struggled to handle Seattle's blitzes. The Seahawks' defense, known as 'the Dark Side' during their Super Bowl run, recorded three sacks in the fourth quarter after allowing none in the first three. Seattle extended its winning streak to 11 games. Week 1 continues with the NFL's first regular-season game in Australia, as the Los Angeles Rams face the San Francisco 49ers in Melbourne on Thursday. The Patriots next host the Pittsburgh Steelers on Sept. 20, while the Seahawks travel to face the Arizona Cardinals on the same date.
