Misiorowski and Schlittler Unanimously Win Cy Young Awards

Sports desk

Milwaukee Brewers’ Jacob Misiorowski and New York Yankees’ Cam Schlittler were named unanimous Cy Young Award winners for the 2026 MLB season on Wednesday, each receiving all 30 first-place votes from the Baseball Writers’ Association of America. Misiorowski, who led the Brewers to their first World Series title, posted a league-best 1.80 ERA among starters with 252 strikeouts in 30 starts, becoming the first pitcher since Randy Johnson in 2001 to win both the Cy Young and a World Series in the same season. Schlittler, in his first full season with the Yankees, recorded an AL-best 1.95 ERA with 239 strikeouts across 33 starts, allowing one run or fewer in 21 of those outings — a Yankees record.

Schlittler, 25, is the Yankees’ youngest Cy Young winner and the team’s first since Gerrit Cole in 2023. He beat finalists Dylan Cease of the Toronto Blue Jays and Parker Messick of the Cleveland Guardians. A seventh-round draft pick out of Northeastern University in 2022, Schlittler transformed from a pitcher whose fastball hovered in the low 90s to one who regularly touches 100 mph, throwing a four-seamer (averaging 98 mph), a sinker (97.8 mph) and a cutter (94.6 mph). He threw fastballs 89.2 percent of the time, the highest rate of any starter. Yankees manager Aaron Boone called him "the best pitcher in the league, hands down," while teammate Cole noted, "He gets an idea of how to dominate, and he dominates."

Misiorowski, 24, became the fourth Brewers pitcher to win the Cy Young, following Corbin Burnes (2021), Pete Vuckovich (1982) and Rollie Fingers (1981). He averaged 100.6 mph on his fastballs and threw more than 1,000 pitches at 100 mph or faster during the season. His fastball against the Chicago Cubs’ Pete Crow-Armstrong in June clocked at 105.5 mph, the fastest pitch ever thrown by a starter in the Statcast era. Misiorowski also led all pitchers with a 6.6 fWAR and a 0.80 WHIP. Phillies left-hander Cristopher Sanchez finished second in NL voting, with Braves veteran Chris Sale third.

Schlittler’s season included a notable snub when Toronto manager John Schneider picked Cease over him to start the All-Star Game, despite Schlittler leading Cease in ERA, WAR and games started at the time. Schlittler opted not to pitch in the game, instead resting as he accumulated a career-high 193 2/3 innings. He also continued a feud with fans of his hometown Boston Red Sox, whom he dominated in Game 1 of the AL Wild Card Series, throwing 117 pitches in a victory. The Yankees sometimes show Schlittler clips of people dissing him as motivation.

It is only the third time in MLB history that both Cy Young winners were unanimous picks, following 2022 (Justin Verlander, Sandy Alcantara) and 1968 (Denny McLain, Bob Gibson). San Diego Padres closer Mason Miller and Blue Jays closer Louis Varland won the inaugural Reliever of the Year awards for their respective leagues. Both Misiorowski and Schlittler have bright futures, with Misiorowski already holding a World Series ring and Schlittler established as the Yankees’ ace in a rotation that also features Cole, Carlos Rodon and Max Fried.
