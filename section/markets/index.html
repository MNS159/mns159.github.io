Jobs

August jobs report crushes expectations with 162,000 payrolls added, stoking rate-hike fears

Financial & Markets desk

The U.S. economy added 162,000 nonfarm payrolls in August, far exceeding the 53,000 to 55,000 economists had expected, the Bureau of Labor Statistics reported Friday. The unemployment rate held steady at 4.1%, as forecast. The August gain was the strongest monthly increase since March, and prior months were revised upward: July swung from a loss of 23,000 to a gain of 21,000, and June was revised up to a gain of 31,000, an increase of 11,000.

Trump and Vance escalate calls for Fed rate cuts as inflation forecast signals further pressure

Financial & Markets desk · anchored on Reuters

President Donald Trump on Friday demanded that the Federal Reserve slash interest rates or else he will cut off trade with countries with which the U.S. maintains trade deficits, according to a Truth Social post reported by CNBC. The ultimatum came a day after Vice President JD Vance told reporters the Fed should be lowering rates, adding to pressure on the central bank as its own initial September inflation forecast shows a mixed picture: headline CPI is expected to ease to 3.3% but core PCE — a preferred Fed gauge — is projected to reaccelerate to 3.49%.
