US-Iran conflict

US and Iran pause strikes for second day amid diplomatic push

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

The United States and Iran have paused their military strikes for a second consecutive day, raising hopes of a return to diplomacy after nearly two weeks of escalating conflict. The US halted its bombing campaign against Iran on Saturday, and Tehran subsequently suspended its retaliatory attacks across the region. The relative calm comes as mediators, including Oman and Qatar, work to revive negotiations on an interim ceasefire deal that collapsed amid a dispute over the Strait of Hormuz.

Ukraine-Iran

Ukraine Strikes Iranian-Linked Vessel in Caspian Sea; Iran Warns of Retaliation

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Ukraine has claimed responsibility for a long-range strike in the Caspian Sea that struck a Russian warship and several vessels allegedly involved in transporting military equipment linked to Iran, including an Iranian commercial vessel. The attack on Saturday killed one sailor and wounded another, according to Iran, which summoned Ukraine’s chargé d’affaires in Tehran to protest what it described as a “hostile and criminal” act. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky announced on X that Kyiv had achieved “very strong results” with long-range strikes in the Caspian Sea, hitting “vessels used in military cargo shipments involving Iran, as well as a warship.”

Terrorism / Europe

Berlin Pride attack suspect shot dead by police

International & World desk

German police shot and killed Abdul Ballout, the 21-year-old German citizen of Lebanese heritage suspected of carrying out a deadly attack on Berlin's Christopher Street Day Pride event, ending a massive manhunt that had lasted less than 24 hours. Officers opened fire in the Spandau district of western Berlin on Sunday evening after Ballout rushed at them with a knife, police said. Despite immediate resuscitation efforts, he died at the scene.

International & World desk
