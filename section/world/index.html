Ukraine War

US envoys Witkoff and Kushner arrive in Kyiv after Moscow talks in new Ukraine peace push

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

US envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner arrived in Kyiv on Sunday for talks with Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy, a day after holding more than three hours of discussions with Russian President Vladimir Putin in Moscow. The visit marks the envoys' first official trip to Ukraine as part of a renewed effort by US President Donald Trump to end the four-and-a-half-year conflict. Because Ukraine's airspace remains closed due to the war, the pair traveled by train from Rzeszow, Poland, after flying into that country. Putin declared a three-day ceasefire to ensure the envoys' safety, and both Russia and Ukraine pledged not to strike each other's capitals during the negotiations.

Middle East

US strikes three Iranian oil tankers after Iran fires missiles at warships

International & World desk

The United States struck three Iranian oil tankers on Saturday in retaliation for Iran's ballistic missile attacks on two US warships near the Strait of Hormuz, marking a sharp escalation in the months-long conflict. US Central Command said its forces targeted a tanker off Iran's Kharg Island, the hub through which Iran exported 90 percent of its crude before the war, a second tanker near Jask, and an unladen vessel in the Gulf of Oman whose crew was ordered to abandon ship. The strikes followed an Iranian ballistic missile launch against a US aircraft carrier and a navy destroyer, which CENTCOM said successfully evaded the attacks without injury to US personnel. 'If you shoot at two of our ships, we will impose an even higher economic cost — taking out three of yours,' CENTCOM commander Admiral Brad Cooper said.
