English FA set to join Welsh in withdrawing support for Infantino

Sports desk

The English Football Association is set to write to FIFA President Gianni Infantino withdrawing its support for him, following the collapse of his plan to sell minority stakes in FIFA competitions to private investors. The move comes as the Football Association of Wales has already publicly withdrawn its backing for Infantino's bid to continue as president. Infantino is expected to stand for re-election for a fourth and final term next March and would need 106 votes from FIFA's 211 member associations to win.

Pogacar to ride Vuelta a Espana for Grand Tour treble

Sports desk

Newly crowned five-time Tour de France champion Tadej Pogacar will race in this month's Vuelta a Espana in an attempt to become the ninth rider in history to win all three of cycling's Grand Tours. The 27-year-old Slovenian, who rides for UAE Team Emirates-XRG, is the runaway favourite for the three-week race after winning the Tour de France by more than six minutes in July and the Giro d'Italia by nearly 10 minutes in 2024.

Sports desk

The first big domino of the 2026 trade deadline fell Aug. 1 when the Los Angeles Dodgers acquired left-handed ace Tarik Skubal from the Detroit Tigers in exchange for outfield prospect Zyhir Hope and right-handed pitching prospects River Ryan and Brady Smith, according to CBS Sports.
