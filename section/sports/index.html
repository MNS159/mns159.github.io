Darts

Littler and Greaves win World Matchplay titles

Sports desk

Luke Littler defended his World Matchplay title in sensational style, smashing Phil Taylor's tournament and final average records in the process. The 19-year-old world number one beat seventh seed Gerwyn Price 18-9 in Blackpool to become just the fourth person to win back-to-back Matchplay titles. Littler finished the week with an average of 111.04, beating Taylor's record of 106.31 from 2010, and averaged 111.53 in the final, bettering Taylor's 111.23 against Adrian Lewis in 2013. He claimed £225,000 in prize money and now leads the PDC's two-year order of merit with £3,143,500, £2.13m ahead of second-placed Luke Humphries.

Formula 1

Norris wins Hungarian Grand Prix as Ferrari flounders

Sports desk

Lando Norris won the Hungarian Grand Prix on Sunday, securing his first victory of the Formula 1 season in a drive he called 'one of my best performances.' The McLaren driver started from pole position but lost the lead to teammate Oscar Piastri on the first lap after sliding wide at Turn Two. Norris stayed calm, kept pace with Piastri in dirty air, and regained the lead after McLaren pitted Piastri to cover Lewis Hamilton's Ferrari, giving Norris clean air and a blistering pace. Piastri later retired with a gearbox failure, denying McLaren a one-two finish. Norris crossed the line ahead of Red Bull's Max Verstappen and championship leader Kimi Antonelli of Mercedes.
