MLB Trade Deadline

Dodgers acquire two-time Cy Young winner Tarik Skubal in blockbuster trade with Tigers

Sports desk

The Los Angeles Dodgers acquired two-time reigning American League Cy Young winner Tarik Skubal from the Detroit Tigers late Saturday night in a blockbuster trade ahead of the Aug. 3 trade deadline. The back-to-back defending World Series champions added the top starting pitcher available on the market, a rental who is set to become a free agent after the season. The move solidifies the Dodgers' bid for a third consecutive title, a feat not accomplished since the 1998–2000 New York Yankees.

FIFA

Former IOC marketing chief: Infantino's leadership 'radioactive,' reign 'over' after FIFA investment plan backlash

Sports desk

FIFA president Gianni Infantino's leadership has become "radioactive" and his 10-year reign as head of world football "is over," according to Michael Payne, former head of marketing at the International Olympic Committee. Payne's comments to AFP come after a leaked FIFA plan to raise up to $4.2 billion through private investment — based on a $20 billion valuation of a new commercial subsidiary called the FIFA Forward Enterprise (FFE) — sparked a furious global reaction, leading Infantino to shelve the scheme on Saturday.

Commonwealth Games

Josh Kerr wins Commonwealth Games mile gold in Glasgow, then spends 45 minutes with fans

Sports desk

Josh Kerr won the men's mile at the Commonwealth Games in Glasgow on Saturday night, crossing the line in 3 minutes 54.12 seconds at Scotstoun Stadium. The 28-year-old Scot, who set a world record of 3:42.66 in London last month, held off Australian prodigy Cam Myers (silver) and former Olympic silver medallist Timothy Cheruiyot (bronze). His childhood friend and Scotland teammate Jake Wightman finished sixth.
