FIFA president Infantino's plans to sell competition stakes unravel

Sports desk

FIFA president Gianni Infantino's plan to sell stakes in the men's World Cup and other competitions to private investors collapsed on Saturday after five days of mounting opposition, with European football's governing body UEFA saying it had 'lost confidence' in his leadership. The proposal, which lacked a formal title, was withdrawn by FIFA after drawing condemnation from confederations, member associations, and senior FIFA staff. The plan would have offered countries an extra $40m for football development if they signed up by 19 September, but critics questioned why private investment was needed given FIFA's financial position: the not-for-profit body holds over $2bn in reserves and is expected to announce record revenues of about $15bn from this summer's World Cup.

MLB trade deadline: Skubal, Peralta headline active market as Aug. 3 cutoff nears

Sports desk

With the MLB trade deadline set for 6 p.m. ET on Monday, Aug. 3, the market is heating up around two-time defending AL Cy Young Award winner Tarik Skubal of the Detroit Tigers and New York Mets right-hander Freddy Peralta, who was scratched from his scheduled Friday start as trade talks intensified. The Mets cited protecting their asset as the prudent move, with interim manager Andy Green saying, “Once talks got to the level that they’re at, it just became the prudent path for us.” Peralta, a pending free agent, is expected to command a top-100 overall prospect in return, according to The Athletic, with the Braves, Cubs, Rays, and Phillies among potential suitors.
