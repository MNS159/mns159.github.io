FDA Approves Replimune Melanoma Therapy Tudriqev After Two Rejections

Health & Science desk · anchored on Reuters

The Food and Drug Administration on Aug. 6 granted accelerated approval to Replimune’s engineered viral immunotherapy Tudriqev (vusolimogene oderparepvec-wtpg, formerly RP1) in combination with Bristol Myers Squibb’s Opdivo (nivolumab) for adults with unresectable or metastatic cutaneous melanoma whose disease progressed on prior anti-PD-1 therapy. The list price is $450,000 per course of therapy before customary rebates and discounts, Replimune said. The approval marks a reversal for the Massachusetts-based biotech after two prior complete response letters from the FDA.

Senate panel votes to hold Anthony Fauci in contempt of Congress

Health & Science desk

A Senate committee voted along party lines Thursday to hold Anthony Fauci, the former director of the National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases, in contempt of Congress for refusing to answer questions during a hearing last week about the origins and federal response to the COVID-19 pandemic. The Homeland Security and Governmental Affairs Committee voted 8-7, with all Republicans in favor and all Democrats opposed. The contempt resolution now moves to the full Senate, where it would be subject to a filibuster; if passed, the Department of Justice would decide whether to prosecute Fauci on a federal misdemeanor charge that carries a penalty of up to $100,000 in fines and one to 12 months in prison.

FDA approves first mRNA flu shot for adults 50 and older

Health & Science desk

The Food and Drug Administration has approved Moderna's mFLUSIVA, the first mRNA-based vaccine for seasonal influenza, for adults 50 and older, the company announced Wednesday. The vaccine, which uses the same messenger RNA technology as the COVID-19 shots, is expected to be available in the United States within weeks.
