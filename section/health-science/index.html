FDA Approves Ionis Drug Zanvastro, First Therapy for Alexander Disease

Health & Science desk

The FDA has approved zilganersen, a treatment for Alexander disease that will be marketed as Zanvastro, making it the first authorized therapy for the ultra-rare neurodegenerative condition and Ionis Pharmaceuticals' first wholly owned neurology drug. The Thursday decision covers both children and adults. Ionis estimates about 300 patients in the United States have Alexander disease, which occurs in roughly one per one million to three million people worldwide.

Scientists show RNA polymerase can accurately read eight-letter genetic alphabet

Health & Science desk

Researchers at the University of California San Diego have demonstrated that RNA polymerase, a key cellular enzyme, can accurately read and transcribe an eight-letter genetic alphabet, doubling the four letters used by all known life on Earth. The finding, based on two studies published in August and September 2026, marks an important step toward building expanded genetic systems capable of performing entirely new biological functions.
