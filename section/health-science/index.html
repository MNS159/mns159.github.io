CDC's Nowcasting Model Helped South Carolina Navigate Worst Measles Outbreak in 35 Years

Health & Science desk

A new disease modeling system from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, called Nowcasting, provided South Carolina public health officials with real-time data during the state's historic measles outbreak from October through March, helping them anticipate a surge in cases and later decide when to scale back resources. The outbreak, which sickened 997 people, was the worst single outbreak in the United States since a 1991 outbreak in New York City, according to a Post and Courier analysis.

Alzheimer's trial to test drug on people without symptoms to see if it can be prevented

Health & Science desk

An experimental Alzheimer's drug will be offered to healthy middle-aged people deemed to have a higher risk of developing the condition, in a trial aiming to prevent dementia before symptoms emerge. The first European participants in the international PrevenTRON study will be screened on Monday by the Surrey and Borders Partnership NHS foundation trust, which is leading recruitment in the UK. The phase III trial is recruiting about 1,600 people aged 55 to 80 from around the world.
