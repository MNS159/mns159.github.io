Trump threatens trade war if Fed doesn't cut rates after strong jobs report

Financial & Markets desk · anchored on Reuters
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

President Trump threatened to stop trading with some nations if the Federal Reserve does not cut interest rates, according to Reuters, with the warning coming after a strong jobs report.

The Hill characterized the president's threat as a trade war if rates are not cut. CNBC's "Fast Money" traders reacted to the president's trade threat aimed at the Federal Reserve, with the segment airing on Friday.

The president's remarks add another layer of pressure on the Federal Reserve over its interest-rate policy, though the reports did not elaborate on which nations would be affected or the timing of any potential action.
