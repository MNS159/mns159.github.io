LSU excludes ex-NFL players from roster ahead of Clemson game amid SEC legal battle

Sports desk
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

LSU did not include former NFL players Dae'Quan Wright and Zxavian Harris on its roster for Saturday night's season opener against Clemson, multiple outlets confirmed, despite a Louisiana judge granting a preliminary injunction on Thursday that cleared the way for the Tigers to play them. The decision keeps the two former Ole Miss stars off the field as the SEC fights in federal court to enforce its ban on professional athletes returning to college football.

The SEC filed its own lawsuit against LSU and coach Lane Kiffin on Thursday, seeking legal authority to enforce its rules after league members voted 15-0 to prohibit former professional players from returning to college football. The league has threatened stiff penalties: a half-season suspension for Kiffin, a 50% cut to the team's annual operating budget, and the revocation of some SEC voting privileges. Game forfeitures are among other punishments discussed by league members.

Wright, a 22-year-old tight end, spent the 2026 NFL training camp and preseason with the Philadelphia Eagles and Cleveland Browns before rejoining Kiffin at LSU. Harris, a 22-year-old defensive tackle, signed with the New Orleans Saints as an undrafted free agent in May. Both players exhausted their college eligibility at Ole Miss under the previous rules.

LSU reportedly filled only 103 of its 105 roster spots, leaving the door open for Wright and Harris to join the squad later this season.

Clemson coach Dabo Swinney blasted the concept of former NFL players returning to college football, calling it "embarrassing" on Tuesday. With LSU's decision, Swinney's Tigers will not face any former professionals in the season opener.
