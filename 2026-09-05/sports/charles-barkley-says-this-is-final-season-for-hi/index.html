Charles Barkley says 2026-27 will be his and Ernie Johnson's final season on 'Inside the NBA'

Sports desk

Charles Barkley announced on “The Bill Simmons Podcast” released Thursday that the upcoming 2026-27 NBA season will be his last on “Inside the NBA,” and that host Ernie Johnson will also retire — unless Johnson changes his mind. “Me and Ernie are a package deal,” Barkley said. “So right now, unless Ernie changes his mind, next year is my last year, 100 percent.” Johnson, 70, has not publicly stated his retirement plans.

Barkley, 63, said he originally intended to retire at 60 but agreed to stay two more years at TNT’s request while the network negotiated a new NBA rights deal. After TNT lost the league’s broadcast rights and ESPN acquired the license to “Inside the NBA,” Barkley decided to return to the show so that the behind-the-scenes staff could keep their jobs. “The only reason I stayed is so everybody could keep their job behind the scenes,” he told Simmons. Barkley noted he has worked with many crew members for more than 25 years.

Barkley joined “Inside the NBA” in 2000; Johnson has been the host since 1990. The show debuted in 1989 and also features Shaquille O’Neal and Kenny Smith. The broadcast schedule for the coming season has not yet been announced. Before the show’s move to ESPN, Barkley had indicated the 2024-25 season would be his final one, when TNT’s NBA future was uncertain.

Barkley said Johnson asked him to stay for two more years before last season as the show transitioned from TNT to ESPN. “I said: ‘I love you, man. You’re the best. I’ll stay,’” Barkley recalled. He also met with NBC and Amazon after TNT lost the NBA, and said NBC offered him more money than he was making at Turner. Nevertheless, he chose to stay with “Inside the NBA” to preserve the employment of longtime staff.

This is the latest timeline Barkley has set for his departure from the iconic studio show. With Johnson apparently planning to retire as well, the network will likely face the challenge of replacing two of the show’s central figures if neither changes course. Until Johnson’s plans are confirmed, Barkley’s statement leaves some uncertainty — “unless Ernie changes his mind,” he said.
