Tadej Pogacar ends season after Vuelta crash with neck fracture

Sports desk

Five-time Tour de France champion Tadej Pogacar will not race again this season after suffering injuries including a neck fracture at the Vuelta a Espana, his team announced.

The 27-year-old Slovenian crashed out of the Vuelta on the race's eighth stage last month while in the race leader's position. He required surgery on a broken collarbone and also suffered a concussion, a 'stable' fracture in his neck, and multiple abrasions.

Pogacar had been aiming to add the Vuelta title to his collection, which includes a single Giro d'Italia victory and five Tour de France wins, completing the set of Grand Tour titles. He was also scheduled to defend his rainbow jersey at the World Championships in Montreal later this month and, in October, to defend his title at the European Championships in Ljubljana, Slovenia, as well as race in Il Lombardia. All of those events will now take place without him.

'Of course, this isn't the way I wanted my season to finish,' Pogacar said in a statement released by his team, UAE Team Emirates-XRG. 'Right now, the most important thing is to recover properly and give my body the time it needs. It has been a season with many special moments and I'm proud of what we achieved together. Now I'll focus on getting healthy, supporting the guys from home, and coming back next year ready for what's ahead.'

The team said the decision not to race again until 2027 would allow him to return to full health 'without placing a timeline on his return to competition'. UAE Team Emirates-XRG also announced that Pogacar had agreed to extend his contract by two years, keeping him with the team until 2032.
