Oil rises past $100 after Iran attacks vessels and US base

Politics desk
✓ Facts
  • Brent crude, the global benchmark oil price, rose nearly 3 percent to $100.72 a barrel in early trading Wednesday.
  • This marked the first time Brent crude has traded above $100 a barrel since late July.
  • Iran said it targeted 10 vessels near the Strait of Hormuz, including two U.S. vessels and eight oil tankers.
  • The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) said it struck the vessels as they attempted to pass through an area Iran had declared off-limits.
  • Iran's strikes followed a U.S. military attack Tuesday that destroyed five Iranian oil tankers.
