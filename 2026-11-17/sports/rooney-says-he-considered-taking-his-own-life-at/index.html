Wayne Rooney says he contemplated suicide during Manchester United career

Sports desk

Former Manchester United and England captain Wayne Rooney has revealed that he contemplated suicide during his playing days at the club, describing a period when he would lock himself in his house for two days drinking. Rooney, 41, made the disclosure on the BBC podcast Life at the Midpoint, hosted by Gabby Logan.

Rooney, who joined United from Everton in 2004 and became the club's record goalscorer with 253 goals in 559 appearances, said the lowest point of his life came while his eldest son Kai, now 17, was a baby. 'When I was drinking, I would lock myself in my house for two days and just drink,' he said. 'This is probably in my best part of my career playing wise.' He added that he 'got to a place where you're contemplating suicide' and 'just didn't want to be around anyone'.

The former forward said he struggled to deal with his feelings and initially tried to mask them. 'You don't want to show any weakness, so you try to mask it,' he explained. 'Even though I knew the position I was in, it was difficult to actually admit that I was in that position because I felt like you feel less of a man, if you like, for doing that.' He said he was speaking to a therapist to try to understand what was wrong, noting that at the time he was at the height of his career, had just had a young son, and had recently married. 'It should be the happiest point in my life and it wasn't,' he said.

Rooney credited his wife Coleen with helping him through the crisis. 'Coleen helped me massively. She could have walked away, it was that bad, but she stuck by me and helped me get through it,' he said. He also said that after opening up to Coleen and his mother, he knew he 'needed help to get through it'. He described hiding away from his son because he 'didn't want Kai seeing me like that'.

Rooney helped Manchester United win five Premier League titles, the Champions League, Europa League, FA Cup and three EFL Cups before returning to Everton in 2017. He scored 53 goals in 120 appearances for England. He was recently named pundit of the year at the Broadcast Sport Awards and is currently appearing on the reality TV show I'm a Celebrity... Get Me Out of Here.
