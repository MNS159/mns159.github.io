Ex-FBI officials charged with lying to Congress over Hunter Biden probe

Politics desk · anchored on Reuters
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum
✓ Facts
  • The Justice Department charged two former FBI officials, Bradley Benavides (56) and Nikki Floris (also referred to as Nikki Cole Floris, 46), each with one count of making false statements to Congress.
  • Both held senior roles on the FBI's now-defunct Foreign Influence Task Force (FITF): Floris was deputy assistant director and functional head, Benavides was section chief.
  • The charges stem from testimony both gave to the House Judiciary Committee in 2023 regarding the FBI's investigations into Hunter Biden and his laptop.
  • Both defendants surrendered to authorities and made their initial court appearance Friday morning in Washington, D.C.; a preliminary hearing is scheduled for the following Tuesday.
  • Prosecutors filed the case via criminal complaint rather than a grand jury indictment, meaning a grand jury has not yet formally charged them.
  • DOJ's statement of facts alleges Benavides testified he learned about the laptop story from Wikipedia, while emails reportedly show he was in contact with and met with the investigative team beforehand.
  • DOJ alleges Floris denied any FITF discussion or involvement regarding Hunter Biden, while email logs reportedly show she discussed him with FITF colleagues.
  • Magistrate Judge Zia Faruqui, at the Friday hearing, described both defendants as having 'sparkling backgrounds' in federal law enforcement and called it unusual to charge such old conduct by criminal complaint.
○ Analysis

The case revives the long-running controversy over the FBI's handling of Hunter Biden's laptop, which The New York Post reported had been abandoned at a Delaware repair shop and later obtained by Rudy Giuliani weeks before the 2020 election. Hunter Biden was later convicted on federal gun charges and pleaded guilty to tax offenses before receiving a full pardon from his father, then-President Joe Biden, in December 2024. The FBI later authenticated the laptop as evidence during Hunter Biden's 2024 gun trial. These new charges mark one of several politically charged prosecutions brought by the Justice Department since President Trump's return to office, and they center on whether two career FBI officials misled lawmakers investigating the bureau's conduct, rather than on the underlying laptop investigation itself.

Steelman

Officials and defense attorneys disagree sharply on whether the prosecution reflects legitimate accountability for lying under oath or a politically timed, retaliatory use of DOJ power against career officials with long service records.

Implications

The case now moves to a preliminary hearing. Prosecutors have charged Benavides and Floris by criminal complaint rather than grand jury indictment; in other recent high-profile cases under the current administration, grand juries in Washington, D.C., and elsewhere have declined to return indictments.
