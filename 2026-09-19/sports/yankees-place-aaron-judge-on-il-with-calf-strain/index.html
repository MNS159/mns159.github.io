Yankees place Aaron Judge on IL with calf strain; ESPN's Passan calls move 'devastating'

Sports desk

The New York Yankees placed outfielder Aaron Judge on the 10-day injured list with a calf strain. Judge left Wednesday’s game against the Minnesota Twins after limping off the field. The move can be backdated to Sept. 17 because Judge last played on Sept. 16, giving him a chance to return before the end of the regular season but leaving little room for a lengthy rehab assignment or extended buildup.

ESPN senior MLB insider Jeff Passan told SportsCenter that the injury "throws everything into flux" for the Yankees. "They've been banking on Aaron Judge's return for months now in hopes that it would wake up an offense that had slumbered since that initial injury toward the end of May," Passan said, "and to see him not just limping out of the game but needing to go on an injured list here, it's devastating, frankly."

Judge missed nearly four months earlier this season after suffering a stress fracture in his right rib in late May. Manager Aaron Boone has offered no firm timetable for Judge’s return, leaving the team waiting for more information while the regular season winds down.

Passan also raised concerns about what Judge will look like if he makes it back for October. "They wonder now what, if anything, are they going to get from Aaron Judge in October?" Passan said.
