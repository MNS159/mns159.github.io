Google gives Gemini the ability to control your Mac

Tech / AI desk

Google on Thursday announced that it is bringing computer use to its Gemini desktop application on macOS, allowing its AI to complete tasks on users’ behalf by navigating native Mac apps and browsing websites in the background. The feature is initially available in English in the U.S. and requires the Gemini app for Mac along with a Google AI Plus, Pro, or Ultra subscription. Google did not say when it would expand to other markets or platforms.

Computer use is a type of agentic offering that lets an AI agent take actions directly on the user’s computer. In Gemini’s case, the AI can complete multistep tasks that involve getting things done across local files, apps, and the web. After the user instructs the AI what they want to do, they will see the agent working in a live preview in their chat, while still keeping their screen and cursor free to continue working on other things. As with other AI agents offering computer use, users can pause, stop, and approve Gemini’s actions before they happen.

Google suggests the feature could be useful for tedious tasks and other workflows, such as filling out forms, repeating the same task over and over across a batch of files, organizing files into folders based on specific criteria, creating slideshows from files or information in online dashboards, and more.

Google is a little late to the game with this launch, as other leading AI model makers like OpenAI, Anthropic, and Meta have already been offering agentic features in their desktop applications for some time. Meanwhile, Google only this week announced the broader rollout of Gemini’s “Tasks,” the in-app feature that allows the AI to work more as an agent, helping users sign into websites, make online purchases, or book appointments. Google also only recently began admitting early testers to try its revamped “CC” AI agent, announced in September, which is designed to help families stay organized across email, calendars, chats, and tasks.
