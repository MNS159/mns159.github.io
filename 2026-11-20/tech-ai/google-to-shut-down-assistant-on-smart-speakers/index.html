Google to retire Google Assistant on smart speakers and shut down Nest app in 2027

Tech / AI desk

Google has announced significant changes to its smart home software, including the retirement of Google Assistant on smart speakers and smart displays and the shutdown of the Nest app, with specific dates set in 2027. The Nest app will be shut down on January 25, 2027, and Google Assistant will be replaced by the Gemini for Home voice assistant on August 2, 2027.

The Nest app shutdown means users will have to manage their Nest products—including Nest thermostats, cameras, and Nest Protect alarms—via the Google Home app. Google says it will automatically transfer Nest devices, cameras, and video history to the Home app on January 25, and will also move Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions to Google Home Premium. The company will begin deleting Nest account data on March 25, 2027.

For the Assistant transition, Google argues that Gemini for Home allows users to ask more complex questions and speak naturally rather than using dedicated commands or specific language. Gemini for Home was announced last year, and Google also killed Google Assistant on phones earlier this year, making the smart home move less surprising.

Google says it plans to share more updates about the changes ahead of the August 2027 transition.

These moves mark the end of an era for Google Assistant on smart home devices and consolidate Google's smart home ecosystem around the Google Home app and its new Gemini voice assistant.
