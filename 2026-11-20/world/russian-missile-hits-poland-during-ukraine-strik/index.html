Russian missile hits Poland during Ukraine strikes as NATO scrambles jets

International & World desk · anchored on AP, AFP

Poland said a Russian cruise missile was among three objects that violated its airspace early on Friday, with Prime Minister Donald Tusk saying it did not appear to be an accident when Western military exercises were taking place in the region. Nobody was hurt and no damage has been reported, but Warsaw described the incident as a "provocation" from Moscow. Russia has made no comment about the violations, which took place during one of its biggest aerial attacks on Ukraine since the start of the full-scale invasion in 2022.

Defence Minister Wladyslaw Kosiniak-Kamysz said the Kh-101 missile hit a rural area at Nowy Lipowiec in southeastern Poland about 50km (31 miles) from the Ukrainian border. The cruise missile, designed for precision targets, crashed at about 06:30 (5:30GMT) and Natalie Szponar, a local resident, said she was getting her children ready for school when she heard a "whistle and a dull impact". The moment of impact was caught by a camera on a farm, and officials later made clear there was no risk to residents. Another suspected missile went down near Rudnik nad Sanem to the west of Nowy Lipowiec, and a jet-powered drone crashed to the south near Mołodycz, said Kosiniak-Kamysz. He called the incursion into Polish airspace a "provocation" from Russia and said he would be asking Nato for additional support.

Nato Secretary General Mark Rutte condemned "grave violations of Polish airspace" and said Nato jets had been scrambled in response. "This incident is clearly dangerous - a consequence of Russia's ongoing war against Ukraine," said Rutte, who was due to visit Warsaw on Saturday. A UK- and France-led exercise called Koalicja 2026 is currently taking place in southeastern Poland involving 11 countries that are part of the West's Coalition of the Willing group in preparation for a potential peace deal in Ukraine. "It is no coincidence that these objects landed near the site where military manoeuvres by a 'coalition of the willing' supported by Polish troops are taking place in Nowa Dęba," Tusk told reporters on Friday. Hours later, five coalition defence ministers - from the UK, France, Poland, Estonia and Lithuania - visited the military training ground where the drill was taking place.

UK Defence Secretary Wes Streeting told reporters that what had happened earlier was "yet another example of Russian provocation that is designed to divide Europe and to deter Ukraine's allies from supporting their fight for freedom and democracy". In Brussels, UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham said the missile incident was "utterly unacceptable". European Council President António Costa expressed his "strong solidarity" with the Polish people and said Russia had to come to the negotiating table to bring the war to the end. The violations of Polish airspace came a day after 40 countries took part in a Coalition of the Willing summit in Berlin, where the allies pledged continued support for Kyiv, and Chancellor Friedrich Merz said peace talks with Russia should take place soon on neutral territory.

At least eight people were killed on Friday as Russian missiles and drones targeted three of Kyiv's bridges across the River Dnipro and a station in Lviv, days after Russia's Vladimir Putin threatened "painful" retaliation for repeated strikes on Russia's bridge across the Kerch Strait to occupied Crimea. This is not the first time the war in Ukraine has spilled over into Poland. In July a cruise missile also landed in Poland, leaving a 10m-wide (33ft) crater. That incident also took place during an attack on the western Ukrainian city of Lviv. Casualties in Poland have been rare, while Ukraine suffers deadly attacks on a daily basis. However, two Polish farmers were killed by a missile in November 2022, which was believed to have been a Ukrainian air-defence missile responding to a Russian attack. Moldovan officials said on Friday that their airspace had also been violated on Friday, when a drone crossed briefly into its territory during Russia's attacks on Ukraine. The Moldovan defence ministry has recorded 118 such violations since the start of Russia's full-scale invasion in February 2022.
