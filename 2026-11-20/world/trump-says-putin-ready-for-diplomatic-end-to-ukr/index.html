Trump says Putin ready for diplomatic end to Ukraine war; US floats NATO membership for Kyiv

International & World desk · anchored on AP
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

President Donald Trump said Russian President Vladimir Putin 'clearly expressed' a desire to end the war in Ukraine by diplomatic means, following a historic trilateral summit in Shenzhen, China, with Putin and Chinese President Xi Jinping. Trump described the talks as 'very good' and said he believes 'there’s a real good chance' an end to the war can be reached. The summit, held on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) forum, produced what Trump called a 'declaration of peace,' though European leaders and Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky expressed skepticism about the prospects for a lasting settlement.

Speaking at a press conference after the Shenzhen talks, Putin said the Americans were 'insistently' asking Russia to allow European participation in peace negotiations. Putin claimed Russia was not opposed but wanted to clarify 'what Europe will bring to these talks,' and said Moscow had invited European leaders to come to Moscow to determine their positions. He also said he had discussed a 'possible ceasefire in a number of areas' but decided against it after a Ukrainian strike on the Crimean Bridge. Putin insisted Russia is 'interested' in ending the war and ready to make compromises, but accused Ukraine and Europe of not being willing.

U.S. envoy Jared Kushner, who attended the Berlin summit of Ukraine’s allies known as the Coalition of the Willing, told reporters that Trump wanted to make a deal with Moscow 'with Europe by his side.' Kushner said the Russians were willing to trust the instinct of U.S. envoys that the Europeans should be at the table, and that Moscow was now 'comfortable' with Ukraine having security guarantees based on NATO’s Article Five collective defense clause. Reports indicate U.S. negotiators have mulled giving Kyiv such guarantees without full NATO membership, if Ukraine agrees to territorial concessions.

European leaders meeting in Berlin expressed readiness for talks with Russia but struck a cautious tone. UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham said Europe has 'a clear and consistent and united negotiating position,' while German Chancellor Friedrich Merz stressed that Moscow's 'maximalist demands are not realistic.' Merz said any attempts to divide Europe into spheres of influence were unacceptable, rejecting a 'Yalta format' for peace. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk took exception to Trump calling Putin a 'friend,' saying 'Mr Putin is not our friend and will never be.' French President Emmanuel Macron urged allies to do the 'maximum' to deliver air defense systems to Kyiv and to 'reopen the question' of Ukraine’s NATO membership.

Zelensky, speaking after the Berlin summit, said there is a 'huge chance' the war will end 'if the United States will really pressure Putin.' He called on global superpowers to 'stop trade with Russia,' pointing to China as Russia’s most vital economic partner and noting that the Trump administration has expanded business with Moscow despite sanctions. Zelensky said Ukraine faces 'a difficult winter' but hinted at possible changes in U.S. policy when Trump’s party loses its majority in Congress in January. He also said details of the next prisoner-of-war exchange between Kyiv and Moscow have not yet been finalized.

The trilateral summit in Shenzhen coincided with continued Russian attacks on Ukraine. As delegations met in China and Germany, several buildings in Kyiv’s historic center came under drone attack, killing one person and damaging the Dutch and Austrian embassies and Saint Volodymyr’s Cathedral. Russia continues to pound Ukraine’s energy infrastructure as winter sets in, and Kremlin officials have not budged from demands for Ukrainian sovereign territory. Putin’s aide Yury Ushakov said a 'great deal' would have to be done before the negotiating format could be expanded to include Europe, suggesting further rounds of discussions are needed.
