Thunder's Jalen Williams stretchered off with significant leg injury after awkward landing

Sports desk

Oklahoma City Thunder guard Jalen Williams was taken off the court on a stretcher after suffering a significant leg injury late in Sunday's 108-101 victory over the Los Angeles Clippers. Williams' left foot was bent unnaturally, and a Thunder staffer quickly covered it with a towel.

The Thunder did not provide an immediate update on Williams' condition or the specific nature of his injury. Head coach Mark Daigneault, speaking after the game, echoed the sense of concern. 'Those are tough, man. We're not doing great,' Daigneault said. 'That's tough on the guys.' Gilgeous-Alexander, fighting back tears during a postgame interview, added, 'I just feel bad for him. Williams missed a significant portion of last season due to various injuries, including a hamstring issue that sidelined him for multiple games during the Western Conference finals. He played in just 33 games last season. Gilgeous-Alexander expressed confidence in Williams' resilience: 'I have no doubt in my mind that he'll be back and be better than he was before.
