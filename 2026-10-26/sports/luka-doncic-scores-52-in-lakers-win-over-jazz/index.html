Luka Doncic scores NBA season-high 52 points with 10 assists in Lakers' win over Jazz

Sports desk

Luka Doncic scored 52 points and dished out 10 assists on Sunday, leading the Los Angeles Lakers to a victory over the Utah Jazz and improving the team's record to 3-0 to start the season.

Doncic's 52-point outing is the highest individual scoring total in the NBA so far this season, according to ESPN. The performance was described by NBA.com as a "magical 52-point performance" in the Lakers' win.

The Lakers, now 3-0, are off to an undefeated start behind Doncic's early-season excellence. The win over the Jazz continues a strong beginning for Los Angeles, which appears to be building momentum.

The game marked the latest showcase of Doncic's scoring and playmaking ability, as he combined his 52 points with 10 assists to power the Lakers.

Sources: NBA.com·ESPN
