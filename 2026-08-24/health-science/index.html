FDA approves Moderna's mRNA flu vaccine for adults 50 and older

Health & Science desk

The Food and Drug Administration has approved Moderna’s mFLUSIVA (mRNA-1010), the first mRNA-based seasonal influenza vaccine in the United States, for adults ages 50 and older. The vaccine is expected to be available for the 2026–2027 respiratory virus season. The FDA granted traditional approval for adults ages 50 to 64 and accelerated approval for those 65 and older, the latter contingent on additional post-approval evidence confirming clinical effectiveness.

Genetics

AI identifies DNA signature of genetic 'on switch' in human genes

Health & Science desk

Researchers at the University of California San Diego have used artificial intelligence to decode the DNA signature of a key genetic element known as the "initiator," which helps determine when human genes are switched on. The findings, published August 23, 2026, in the journal Genes, show that the initiator is present in roughly 60% of human genes. The team, led by graduate student researcher Torrey Rhyne-Carrigg in the laboratory of Professor James T. Kadonaga, analyzed approximately 500,000 different versions of the initiator sequence using high-throughput DNA sequencing and trained a machine learning model to identify its characteristic DNA pattern.

Neurology

APOE4 Alzheimer's risk gene shrinks neurons and hyperactivates memory circuits years before symptoms, study finds

Health & Science desk

Researchers at Gladstone Institutes have identified a molecular mechanism by which the APOE4 gene variant, the strongest known genetic risk factor for Alzheimer's disease, disrupts brain function long before memory problems appear. In a study published August 23, 2026, in Nature Aging, the team showed that APOE4 increases production of a protein called Nell2 in hippocampal neurons, causing the cells to shrink and become abnormally active. The early hyperactivity predicted later memory deficits in mice, and reducing Nell2 in adult animals reversed the changes, suggesting a potential therapeutic window.
