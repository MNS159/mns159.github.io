Saints trade LB Deion Jones to 49ers for RB Zamir White after Kamara injury

Sports desk

The New Orleans Saints have acquired running back Zamir White from the San Francisco 49ers in exchange for linebacker Deion Jones, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter. The trade, reported Sunday, addresses immediate depth concerns for the Saints after a series of injuries to their running backs, including Alvin Kamara's MCL sprain.

White, 26, signed a one-year, $1.2 million contract with the 49ers less than two weeks ago. He spent his first four NFL seasons with the Las Vegas Raiders, accumulating 736 rushing yards and two touchdowns over 45 games, along with 25 receptions for 152 yards. In two preseason appearances with San Francisco, he rushed 14 times for 30 yards. He was projected to be sixth on the 49ers' depth chart and was considered a potential cut candidate.

Jones, 31, signed a one-year, $1.3 million deal with the Saints on Aug. 7. A former second-round pick of the Atlanta Falcons in 2016, Jones has played 128 career games (91 starts) across stints with the Falcons, Cleveland Browns, Carolina Panthers, and Tampa Bay Buccaneers, recording 761 tackles, 12 sacks, and 13 interceptions. Last season with the Buccaneers, he appeared in all 17 games and made 29 tackles. He was listed as a fourth-team linebacker on the Saints' depth chart and was considered a cut candidate.

The Saints' backfield has been hit hard by injuries. Kamara is nursing an MCL sprain, Devin Neal aggravated a hamstring injury in Saturday's preseason game, and Audric Estime hurt his ankle in the preseason opener. That leaves Travis Etienne Jr. as the clear No. 1 option, with Kendre Miller among the few healthy reserves. White is expected to provide depth through the end of training camp and could see snaps early in the regular season if needed.

For the 49ers, the trade adds veteran linebacker depth behind starters Fred Warner and Tatum Bethune. Jones has familiarity with the system of 49ers defensive coordinator Raheem Morris, who coached him in Atlanta in 2020 and 2021. The 49ers also signed running back Khalil Herbert in a corresponding move after placing Patrick Taylor Jr. on injured reserve, leaving the team with Christian McCaffrey, Jordan James, Kaelon Black, Herbert, and Sincere McCormick at the position.
