Soccer

Watkins absent as Villa thrashed 4-0 by Brighton; transfer speculation intensifies

Sports desk

Brighton & Hove Albion began their Premier League season with a dominant 4-0 victory over Aston Villa at the Amex Stadium, a result that was overshadowed by the absence of Villa striker Ollie Watkins from the matchday squad. The 30-year-old England international was left out as speculation over a move to Saudi Pro League club Al-Hilal continues to grow. Villa manager Unai Emery, who had earlier in the week confirmed offers had been received for Watkins, declined to explain the decision, telling Match of the Day: "This question is for him. You can call him and try to get his answer. The players here wanted to play and we had two strikers with a small injury. The question on Watkins is for him."
