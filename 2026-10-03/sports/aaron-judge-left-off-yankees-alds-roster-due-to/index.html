Aaron Judge left off Yankees' ALDS roster due to calf strain

Sports desk

New York Yankees slugger Aaron Judge was not included on the team's 26-man roster for the American League Division Series against the Tampa Bay Rays, the club's network first reported, due to a right calf strain that may threaten his availability for the remainder of October. Judge, 34, suffered the injury on Sept. 16 at Minnesota, just seven games after returning from a rib fracture. He played in only 66 games this season after winning consecutive AL MVP awards.

Judge sat out the Yankees' two-game sweep of the Boston Red Sox in the AL Wild Card round. Before that series, he took simulated at-bats at the team's Class AAA facility in Somerset. After the Yankees flew to Tampa for the ALDS, he worked out at their spring training facility and also went through the off-day workout at Tropicana Field. Manager Aaron Boone did not specify whether Judge had run the bases in his rehab progression, often one of the final steps to a return, but noted he had tested the calf in other ways. Judge had vowed to "do all he can" to play against the Rays.

On Monday, Judge took live batting practice at Yankee Stadium and took more live at-bats at the team's Double-A affiliate in Somerset on Wednesday. He told reporters he had been limited in his activities. Boone said on Wednesday, "We feel like in a couple weeks since the injury, he's made real strides and significant improvements. Hopefully, we're in a position to make another decision in a couple of days."

New York's sweep of Boston marked its first playoff games contested without Judge since 2015. The Rays won the AL East in Judge's absence. The Yankees now open the ALDS in Tampa without their three-time MVP, whose calf strain may threaten his viability for the rest of the postseason.
