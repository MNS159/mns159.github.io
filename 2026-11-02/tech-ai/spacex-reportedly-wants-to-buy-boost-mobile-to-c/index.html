SpaceX reportedly seeks to buy Boost Mobile from EchoStar to compete with top US carriers

Tech / AI desk

SpaceX is looking to acquire Boost Mobile from EchoStar, according to a report from The Washington Post, a move that would help the Elon Musk-owned company build a hybrid cellular-satellite network and potentially become the fourth-largest wireless provider in the United States. EchoStar has about 7.5 million customers between Boost Mobile and Gen Mobile, its other mobile service offering.

The acquisition would complement SpaceX's existing satellite-cellular efforts. In 2024, the company began offering satellite connectivity to T-Mobile subscribers through its Starlink service. Last year, SpaceX also purchased $17 billion worth of wireless spectrum from EchoStar for cellular services, a move that Musk said at the time on X was 'the last critical piece of the spectrum puzzle needed for SpaceX to provide complete phone coverage in America.' The reported interest in buying Boost Mobile suggests that the spectrum acquisition was not the last piece.

Musk reportedly spoke directly with EchoStar chairman Charlie Ergen in October about acquiring Boost Mobile. Boost does not operate its own cell towers; instead, it leases capacity from AT&T and T-Mobile. Sources told The Post that network leases are usually nontransferable, and AT&T and T-Mobile might need to approve such a deal. Musk has previously approached AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon about buying capacity on their networks, but all three rejected him, according to the report.

The three largest U.S. wireless providers each have over 100 million subscribers, dwarfing Boost Mobile's base. However, the true value of the acquisition for SpaceX would be gaining access to terrestrial cellular networks. Boost currently covers most of the 48 contiguous states, allowing Starlink to cover devices when they’re outdoors and relying on cell towers to handle devices when Starlink signals can’t reach them.

The report said AT&T, T-Mobile, and Verizon may still have a say in a SpaceX acquisition of Boost Mobile.
