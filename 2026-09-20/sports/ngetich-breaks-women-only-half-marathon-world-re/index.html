Ngetich breaks women-only half-marathon world record

Sports desk

Agnes Ngetich broke the world record for a women-only half marathon on Sunday, winning gold at the World Road Running Championships in Copenhagen in a time of one hour, five minutes and 15 seconds.

The 25-year-old Kenyan shaved one second off the previous mark of 1:05:16, set by her compatriot Peres Jepchirchir when she won the World half-marathon gold in 2020. Ethiopia's Letesenbet Gidey holds the overall women's half-marathon world record with a time of 1:02:52.

"My coach believed in me and told me that I could do it, that I could win the gold medal," Ngetich said. "So, I am very happy and very grateful to now hold the world half marathon record, too."

Ngetich has a history of record-breaking performances. In April 2025, she set a new 10km world record by clocking 29:27, becoming the first woman to run the distance in under 30 minutes in a women-only race. She also broke the 10km world record in Valencia in 2024, finishing a mixed road race in 28:46 to become the first woman to run under 29 minutes.

Looking ahead, Ngetich said she would discuss her future plans with her coach but confirmed she intends to return to track racing in 2027 for the World Athletics Championships. "It is my dream to win a gold medal on the track. It is the only one I am missing," she added.

In the men's half marathon, Sweden's Andreas Almgren won gold and broke his own European record by 35 seconds, crossing the line in 58:06.
