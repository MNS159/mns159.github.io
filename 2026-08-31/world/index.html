Natural Disaster

Nepal-Tibet flood death toll nears 1,000 as rescuers race to reach trapped workers

International & World desk

The death toll from catastrophic floods along the Nepal-Tibet border has reached 939 in Nepal, with 16 additional deaths confirmed in Tibet, bringing the known regional total to 955, according to disaster authorities. Nepal’s disaster management agency reported 3,925 people missing nationwide, including 592 foreign nationals, as rescue operations entered their sixth day. In Tibet, Chinese state media listed 546 missing. The disaster has devastated hydropower projects along the Trishuli River, with at least 11 completed or under-construction plants damaged. Rescuers believe more than 600 workers remain trapped in mud-filled tunnels at the Trishuli-3A and Upper Trishuli-1 sites, while the number of unaccounted-for hydropower staff was revised down to 639 on Monday from an earlier count of 933.

Middle East

US and Iran exchange strikes, breaking monthlong pause in hostilities

International & World desk

The United States and Iran traded direct military strikes on Sunday, ending a nearly monthlong pause in hostilities. The U.S. military said it targeted two Iranian rocket launchers on Larak Island in the Strait of Hormuz that were preparing to fire sea mines into the strategic waterway, according to a statement sent to NPR. Iran retaliated by launching ballistic missiles at U.S. forces at Al-Minhad Air Base in the United Arab Emirates and at bases in Jordan, though no missiles reached U.S. positions. Jordan’s armed forces said it shot down eight missiles that entered its airspace. The UAE reported intercepting drones launched from Iran over its territorial waters.

Ukraine

Russia fires nearly 1,500 drones at Ukraine in four days, threatens 'massive' energy strikes

International & World desk

Russia has launched an intensified aerial campaign against Ukraine, firing nearly 1,500 drones in four days—more than half of them jet-powered—and threatening 'massive strikes' on Ukraine's energy infrastructure, according to Ukrainian and Russian officials. The attacks have pummeled Kyiv and surrounding regions for a fifth consecutive day, damaging warehouses, a retail outlet and residential buildings, and disrupting food logistics. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said between Thursday and Sunday, Russia deployed roughly 1,500 drones, with around 800 being jet-propelled, adding that Moscow's tactic is 'to wear down Ukraine and Ukrainians.'

International & World desk
