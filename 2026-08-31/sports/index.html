Lionel Messi announces retirement from Argentina national team after 20-year career

Sports desk

Lionel Messi officially announced his retirement from the Argentina national team on Monday, closing a two-decade international career that made him the country's all-time leader in appearances (207) and goals (125). The decision, posted on his social media accounts, comes roughly six weeks after Argentina's 1-0 extra-time loss to Spain in the 2026 World Cup final — his final match in a World Cup — and just over three weeks after his father and longtime agent, Jorge Messi, died on Aug. 8 at age 68.

Liverpool complete £123m signing of Barcola from PSG

Sports desk

Liverpool have completed the signing of France forward Bradley Barcola from Paris St-Germain for a fee worth up to £123m. The 23-year-old joins on a five-year contract, with Liverpool paying an initial £106m plus up to £17m in potential add-ons. PSG had valued the player at £145m. Barcola becomes the fifth new arrival at Anfield this summer, following winger Victor Munoz and defenders Jeremy Jacquet, Ronald Araujo and Ifeanyi Ndukwe.
