Liverpool complete £123m signing of Barcola from PSG

Sports desk

Liverpool have completed the signing of France forward Bradley Barcola from Paris St-Germain for a fee worth up to £123m. The 23-year-old joins on a five-year contract, with Liverpool paying an initial £106m plus up to £17m in potential add-ons. PSG had valued the player at £145m. Barcola becomes the fifth new arrival at Anfield this summer, following winger Victor Munoz and defenders Jeremy Jacquet, Ronald Araujo and Ifeanyi Ndukwe.

“It took a long time but I'm really happy today to be able to play for Liverpool and all I want is to be on the pitch and be able to play,” Barcola said. “I hope to achieve very big things. First of all, winning the Premier League - it's one of my biggest dreams in my life.” The France international, who will wear the number 29 shirt, added: “When I knew about Liverpool's interest in me, I didn't ask questions, and I knew it was a very big club for me.”

Barcola primarily plays on the left wing but can operate across the forward line. His arrival adds depth to Andoni Iraola's squad following the departure of Mohamed Salah, the long-term injury absence of Hugo Ekitike, and the potential sale of Cody Gakpo. Last season, he scored 13 goals and provided seven assists in 49 games for PSG in all competitions. He featured in all eight of France's games at the 2026 World Cup, scoring three goals as Les Bleus reached the third-place play-off.

The deal includes covering a solidarity payment PSG owed Lyon as part of the transfer that took Barcola to Paris three years ago. He joined PSG from Lyon in 2023 for a reported £38.5m and won three Ligue 1 titles, two Champions Leagues, the French Cup twice, the Uefa Super Cup and the Fifa Intercontinental Cup. Barcola was part of the PSG side that won back-to-back Champions League crowns in 2025 and 2026, featuring off the bench in both finals.

Barcola’s playing style reflects centre-forward instincts despite operating from wide areas. Last season, he averaged 3.5 shots per 90 minutes in Ligue 1 and posted a 9.83 expected goals (xG) total—the highest among Ligue 1 wingers. He was the only player across Europe’s top five leagues to average more than two shot-ending carries per 90 minutes (2.06). His pressing is also a strength: he ranked ninth across those leagues for possessions won in the final third. Liverpool continue their heavy investment of recent summers, having previously signed Alexander Isak and Florian Wirtz for more than £100m each.
