NPR investigation finds U.S. heat deaths are five times higher than official counts

Health & Science desk

A two-year investigation by NPR and Boston University has found that the official U.S. tally of heat-related deaths dramatically undercounts the true toll, with an estimated 9,000 deaths per year from 2018 to 2025 attributable to heat — roughly five times the average of about 1,700 reported annually by the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. In some years, the number may exceed 10,000, according to the analysis, which examined weekly mortality and temperature data for 530 U.S. counties representing about 73% of the population. The findings are scheduled for publication in a scientific journal.

Pennsylvania measles deaths spark political feud over vaccination

Health & Science desk

The recent announcement of Pennsylvania's first measles-related deaths in decades has ignited a public feud among politicians and health officials who disagree over the role of the contagious disease in the fatalities. Pennsylvania officials reported that two unvaccinated residents died after being infected with measles, with Gov. Josh Shapiro stressing that vaccinations could have averted the deaths. The announcement quickly came under fire, as a clash erupted between Shapiro, a vaccine-promoting Democrat, and U.S. Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., a Trump administration appointee with a history of raising alarms about established vaccines. Kennedy said in a social post the deaths "may even have been altogether fabricated."

FDA Approves Daraxonrasib as First RAS Inhibitor for Pancreatic Cancer; Stock Flat Despite Historic Approval

Health & Science desk

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has approved Revolution Medicines' oral therapy Rasonque (daraxonrasib) for adults with metastatic pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma (PDAC) who have received at least one prior systemic therapy or who are not candidates for multiagent systemic therapy. The approval is historic as daraxonrasib is the first RAS inhibitor authorized as a targeted treatment for the most common form of pancreatic cancer. Despite the milestone, Revolution's shares remained relatively flat in the days following the announcement, closing the week at $207.88 after a 6% drop on Friday, as investors weighed the drug's pricing and market potential.

NASA's Roman Space Telescope launches to probe dark energy and exoplanets

Health & Science desk

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope lifted off at 7:26 a.m. EDT Sunday, Aug. 31, 2026, aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket from Launch Complex 39A at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida, beginning a three-month journey of roughly one million miles to the second Sun-Earth Lagrange point, or L2. Once operational, the observatory will combine sharp infrared vision with an unusually wide field of view to survey enormous regions of the universe, with a primary focus on dark matter, dark energy, and exoplanets. NASA says the telescope is designed to survey the universe about 1,000 times faster than the Hubble Space Telescope.
