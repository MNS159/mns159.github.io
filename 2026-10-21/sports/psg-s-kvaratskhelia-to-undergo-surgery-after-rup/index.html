PSG's Kvaratskhelia to undergo surgery after ruptured ACL in win over Barcelona

Sports desk

Paris Saint-Germain winger Khvicha Kvaratskhelia will undergo surgery in the coming days after rupturing the anterior cruciate ligament (ACL) in his right knee during his side's 4-2 Champions League win over Barcelona on Tuesday. The 25-year-old Georgia international was substituted in the 59th minute at the Parc des Princes after landing awkwardly following a challenge with Barcelona defender Xavi Espart, unable to put weight on his right leg as he was carried off the pitch.

PSG have not set a timeline for Kvaratskhelia's return, but ACL ruptures typically require an absence of at least nine months, raising doubts about whether he will play again this season. In a statement on Instagram, Kvaratskhelia said: "Before every game I play I ask God to leave the field healthy, but in vain. I probably prepared for this game the best I have ever prepared for. I thought I was prepared for everything. I realise that God chose me and if I protected the players on another field, I'm ready to go through these months of hard work, pain, tears. I know I have to come back stronger for my people who always have my back. I won't let you down, I promise, I won't waste a second."

Kvaratskhelia joined PSG from Napoli in January 2025 and has helped the club win back-to-back Champions League titles, being named player of the tournament for the latter triumph. He has featured 11 times for PSG this season, tallying 29 goals and 20 assists in 91 games for the club overall. He finished 12th in the 2025 Ballon d'Or voting and is among the 30 finalists for the 2026 award.

The club also confirmed that forward Ousmane Dembele and center back Marquinhos will be absent for a "few weeks" after sustaining thigh injuries in the same match. Dembele limped off with a left thigh injury while Marquinhos injured his right thigh. PSG and Barcelona were level at 2-2 heading into the final 10 minutes before the hosts scored twice late on to maintain their winning start to their Champions League defence, sitting top of the table with three wins from three matches.

PSG have attacking options to fill the gap, including Maghnes Akliouche, Ferran Torres and Mika Godts, each acquired for around $50 million in the summer. Akliouche, who started over Kvaratskhelia against Manchester City earlier this season, has two goals and an assist in 11 appearances and has drawn praise from manager Luis Enrique for his versatility. Torres has started brightly with seven goals for PSG, while Godts has made only one start so far. The loss of Kvaratskhelia for a significant period is a major blow as PSG looks to repeat as Ligue 1 and Champions League champion, and the need to manage minutes for key players could make it tougher to keep the attack fresh through the season.
