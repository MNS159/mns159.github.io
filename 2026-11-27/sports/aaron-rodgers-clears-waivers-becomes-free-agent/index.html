Aaron Rodgers clears waivers, becomes free agent after accusing Mike McCarthy of sleeping in meetings

Sports desk
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

Former Pittsburgh Steelers quarterback Aaron Rodgers officially cleared waivers on Thursday and is now an unrestricted free agent, after the team released him following his public accusations against head coach Mike McCarthy. Rodgers, 42, had been benched earlier in the week and then aired his grievances during an appearance on The Pat McAfee Show. The team released him shortly after. In 10 games for the Steelers in 2026, he completed 60.9 percent of his passes for 2,241 yards, 16 touchdowns and six interceptions.

On Wednesday's McAfee Show, Rodgers accused McCarthy of sleeping during offensive meetings and arriving late to team meetings throughout the season, and said the behavior was present during their shared tenure with the Green Bay Packers as well as during McCarthy's run as head coach of the Dallas Cowboys. Rodgers claimed that Steelers tight end Pat Freiermuth informed him of the decision to make him inactive for Friday's game against the Denver Broncos. He gave the team an ultimatum to change play callers or release him.

Both Cowboys owner Jerry Jones and current Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer refuted Rodgers' claims. Jones told reporters he had never seen such behavior and that he had tried to extend McCarthy's contract when it expired after the 2024 season, calling him 'outstanding' and 'one of the finest people around football.' Schottenheimer, who served as McCarthy's offensive coordinator in Dallas, said, 'Mike McCarthy is an incredible man. He's a damn good football coach. My experience with him here was awesome.'

Former NFL quarterback Cam Newton suggested on his YouTube channel that Rodgers may have been the reason wide receiver George Pickens left the Steelers for the Dallas Cowboys in May 2025. Newton pointed out that before Rodgers signed with Pittsburgh, he trained with DK Metcalf in California while Pickens was still on the roster, saying, 'Who's to say Aaron Rodgers isn't the reason why George Pickens isn't there?' Newton added that Rodgers exhibited 'bad teamship' and was not a 'stand-up citizen.'

Despite the release, Rodgers' NFL future remains uncertain. According to Kalshi odds, there is an 88% chance he retires or does not sign with a team. The New England Patriots hold a 6% chance, and the Baltimore Ravens—who recently traded for Jacoby Brissett—are at 3%. Dianna Russini reported there is currently no market for Rodgers, though injuries could change the situation quickly. Rodgers signed a one-year deal with the Steelers after being released by the New York Jets following the 2024 season, having previously played for the Green Bay Packers.
