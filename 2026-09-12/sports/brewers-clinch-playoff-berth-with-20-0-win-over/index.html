Brewers clinch playoff berth with 20-0 win over Reds

Sports desk

The Milwaukee Brewers became the first National League team to clinch a postseason berth in 2026, pummeling the Cincinnati Reds 20-0 on Friday night at American Family Field. The Brewers (92-56) own the best record in baseball and assured themselves of no worse than the third wild-card spot, though they have loftier goals: clinching their fourth consecutive NL Central title and earning a Wild Card Series bye.

Milwaukee's 23-hit outburst included four home runs, the franchise's first grand slam of the season, and a 20-run shutout that made history. According to MLB.com's Sarah Langs, the Brewers became the first team in MLB history to record multiple shutout wins of 20-plus runs in a season, having also beaten the Seattle Mariners 22-0 on Aug. 18. It was also the largest victory by any team on the day it clinched a postseason spot.

Garrett Mitchell provided the grand slam in an eight-run fifth inning, finishing 3-for-5. William Contreras led the team with six RBI, hitting a three-run homer in the eighth inning. Brice Turang added a three-run homer and went 3-for-3 with four RBI and a walk. Jake Bauers launched a two-run homer in a six-run second inning. Jackson Chourio went 4-for-6 with two RBI. Starter Dustin May (7-9) allowed only two singles in six innings, and Garrett Stallings earned a save with three scoreless innings.

Cincinnati starter Andrew Abbott (6-11) lasted just two innings, allowing eight runs on nine hits in his shortest start of the season. Reds catcher and pitcher Jose Trevino pitched in the seventh and eighth innings, retiring the side in order in the seventh but giving up a three-run homer to Contreras in the eighth. Elly De La Cruz extended his on-base streak to 18 games with a walk in the sixth. The Reds fell to 69-78.

The Brewers have reached the playoffs eight times in the past nine seasons. They have not won a World Series; their only pennant came in 1982, when they lost to the St. Louis Cardinals in seven games while still part of the American League. Milwaukee has a 10-game lead over the Chicago Cubs in the NL Central, with its magic number to clinch the division at four. The right-handed duo of Jacob Misiorowski and Logan Henderson forms the top of the postseason rotation. The Brewers are top three in both runs scored and runs allowed per game.
