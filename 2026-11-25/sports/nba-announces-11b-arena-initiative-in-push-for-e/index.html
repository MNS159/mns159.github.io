NBA Announces $11B Arena Initiative to Develop European League with FIBA

Sports desk

The NBA on Wednesday announced an approximately $11 billion initiative to construct and renovate arenas and surrounding entertainment districts in Europe, a major step toward launching a new professional basketball league on the continent alongside the International Basketball Federation (FIBA) by 2027.

The funding, backed by investment firms including Apollo Sports Capital, Ares Management, Arctos, Blackstone, HPS Investment Partners and J.P. Morgan, will be used to develop modern facilities that include housing, workplaces, restaurants, retail and public spaces. The NBA said the initiative aims to provide prospective clubs and ownership groups with access to sustainable capital and the league's expertise to elevate the fan experience and attract more live events.

NBA Commissioner Adam Silver said in a statement, 'We've seen firsthand how state-of-the-art arenas and the related developments around them can transform basketball teams and their surrounding communities. As we prepare to launch a new league in Europe alongside FIBA, we see a significant opportunity to help clubs develop facilities which both build on the unique traditions that define European basketball while also elevating the fan experience and attracting many more live events.'

The league cited a study estimating the new league could drive more than $26 billion in gross value added to GDP across Europe, support nearly 15,000 full-time equivalent jobs annually, and attract nearly 70 million spectators over its first 10 years. The investors issued a joint statement expressing a commitment to honoring the passion and traditions of European basketball fans while supporting lasting economic growth.

The announcement follows a timeline confirmed in October by the NBA and FIBA targeting a 2027 launch. Previous plans for a merger with the existing EuroLeague were rejected by EuroLeague owners, who reportedly felt undervalued by the NBA and FIBA. The NBA said it will continue discussions with interested investors and arena developers as it finalizes the initiative.
