Aaron Rodgers benched by Steelers, accuses coach Mike McCarthy of sleeping in meetings and saying '1, 2, 3, Packers' at halftime

Sports desk

The Pittsburgh Steelers have benched four-time NFL MVP Aaron Rodgers, making Mason Rudolph the starting quarterback for Friday's home game against the Denver Broncos, the team announced late Tuesday. Rodgers, 42, will be inactive for the game and will not serve as backup. The decision was a coaching move, according to ESPN, and Rodgers was not listed on the team's injury report. The Steelers (6-4) have lost two straight games, including a 19-16 defeat at the Philadelphia Eagles on Sunday.

On Wednesday, Rodgers appeared on The Pat McAfee Show and unleashed a series of accusations against Steelers head coach Mike McCarthy, who previously coached Rodgers for most of his 18-year tenure with the Green Bay Packers. Rodgers claimed that McCarthy arrived late to a quarterback meeting last Wednesday and began snoring within 10 minutes, then left the room after doing so again. Rodgers also said McCarthy missed an offensive meeting Thursday, a walkthrough Friday, and a morning meeting Saturday. Rodgers alleged that McCarthy admitted to not watching film on the Broncos in preparation for Friday's game.

Rodgers said his breaking point came at halftime of Sunday's loss to the Eagles, when McCarthy told the team, '1, 2, 3, Packers!' — a chant from his Green Bay days. NFL insider Jordan Schultz reported that multiple sources confirmed the '1, 2, 3, Packers' comment was true. Rodgers said he confronted McCarthy on Tuesday, offering an ultimatum that if changes were not made — including McCarthy relinquishing play-calling duties — he would rather not be the team's quarterback. McCarthy denied the allegations, Rodgers said. Rodgers added that his time with the Steelers is over, saying, 'That ship has sailed.'

Rodgers joined the Steelers in 2025 after two seasons with the New York Jets. He had said he was '99% sure' he would retire after leading Pittsburgh to the playoffs last season but was persuaded to return by McCarthy's hiring. The Steelers rank 22nd in both points and passing yards per game. Wide receiver Michael Pittman is injured, and DK Metcalf was ejected during Sunday's loss. Rodgers, who turns 43 next week, has won the MVP award four times (2011, 2014, 2020, 2021), second only to Peyton Manning's five. The Steelers (6-4) hold the final AFC playoff spot, trailing the division-leading Cincinnati Bengals (7-3).

Rodgers said teammate Pat Freiermuth informed him of the benching. The Steelers have seven games remaining, and it remains unclear whether Rodgers would return to the lineup if Rudolph struggles.
