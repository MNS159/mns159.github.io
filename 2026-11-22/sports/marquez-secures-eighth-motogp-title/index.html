Marquez wins record-equalling eighth MotoGP title

Sports desk

Marc Marquez clinched a record-equalling eighth MotoGP championship with an eighth-placed finish in the Portuguese Grand Prix on Sunday. The Spaniard needed to finish in the top nine on his Ducati to secure a second consecutive title and move level with legendary Italian Giacomo Agostini on eight championships.

Marquez started second on the grid and rode cautiously after crashing out in the sprint race on Saturday. Aprilia's Italian rider Marco Bezzecchi, who held a 102-point advantage over Marquez at one stage this season, won Sunday's race, while Marquez's fellow Spaniards Jorge Martin and Raul Fernandez completed the top three.

The 33-year-old was hoisted in the air by his team after crossing the line. This season, Marquez won seven grands prix and 10 sprint races, adding to the 11 grands prix he won in 2025.

Sunday's title was Marquez's eighth in MotoGP and his 10th championship overall, following wins in the 125cc and Moto2 categories earlier in his career.
