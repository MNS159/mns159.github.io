Wembanyama scores 50 points in 18 minutes, makes history

Sports desk

Victor Wembanyama scored 50 points in a 114-91 victory over the New Orleans Pelicans on Saturday night at Frost Bank Center, tying Spurs legend David Robinson for the second-most 50-point games in franchise history with three. Wembanyama finished with 18 rebounds, five blocks and four steals, shooting 20 of 29 from the field. He had 32 points by halftime, outscoring the Pelicans' starters (26) by himself.

The 22-year-old superstar has now recorded three career 50-point outings, matching Robinson's total. Only George Gervin, with five, has more in Spurs history. Wembanyama's performance also marked his second 50-point game of the season; he scored 52 points in a Nov. 4 win over the Dallas Mavericks. Since an 0-2 start, San Antonio (14-3) has gone 14-1.

Wembanyama is the third player since 1973-74 to log at least 50 points, 15 rebounds and five blocks in a single game. After the game, he said in an on-court interview, 'It is very special.' He added with a smile, 'But unlike many of my peers, I'm not very good at stat padding. So I haven't reached it as many times as I should have.'

Actress Selena Gomez was sitting courtside, and Wembanyama acknowledged her presence. 'Of course, I had to show off for Selena, you know,' he said, laughing. Guard Stephon Castle added 19 points for the Spurs, and De'Aaron Fox contributed 10 assists.

The Pelicans (5-13) have lost nine of their last 10 games after a 4-4 start. Guard Jeremiah Fears, 20, led New Orleans with 29 points. Wembanyama is again an NBA MVP candidate, though he faces competition from Los Angeles Lakers guard Luka Dončić, who leads the league in scoring at 37.2 points per game and also has two 50-point games this season.
