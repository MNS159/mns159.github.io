Red Sox acquire Adley Rutschman from Orioles in deadline shocker

Sports desk

The Boston Red Sox pulled off the biggest surprise of Monday's MLB trade deadline, acquiring All-Star catcher Adley Rutschman from the division-rival Baltimore Orioles, according to multiple reports. The deal was part of a flurry of activity across the league as front offices made a late push before the deadline. The Red Sox also added left-handed reliever Erik Miller, while sending infielder Marcelo Mayer — the No. 4 overall pick in the 2021 draft — to Baltimore.

Rutschman, 27, is widely regarded as one of the game's premier catchers and a cornerstone of the Orioles' rebuild. The trade reshapes the American League East landscape, as Boston lands a franchise-caliber backstop while parting with a top prospect. The Orioles, meanwhile, continue to stockpile young talent as they retool for the future.

The Red Sox were among the most active teams at the deadline, with chief baseball officer Craig Breslow making significant moves to bolster the roster. In addition to the Rutschman blockbuster, Boston added Miller to its bullpen. The moves signal a clear push for contention in the second half, as the Red Sox look to close the gap in a competitive division.

The trade deadline saw several other notable deals. The Chicago Cubs added right-handed starter Clay Holmes and outfielder Tyrone Taylor from the New York Mets, along with left-handed starter Braxton Garrett from the Miami Marlins. The San Diego Padres bolstered their rotation by acquiring 2021 AL Cy Young Award winner Robbie Ray from the San Francisco Giants and right-hander Casey Mize from the Detroit Tigers. The Cleveland Guardians landed left-handed starter Foster Griffin from the Washington Nationals and outfielder Jo Adell from the Los Angeles Angels.

The Philadelphia Phillies added second baseman Luis Arraez from the Giants and left-handed reliever Brooks Raley from the Mets. The Pittsburgh Pirates acquired right-handed reliever Luke Weaver from the Mets. The Milwaukee Brewers added right-handed starter Dustin May and left-handed reliever JoJo Romero from the St. Louis Cardinals, then picked up right-handed reliever Antonio Senzatela from the Colorado Rockies. The Seattle Mariners acquired outfielder Taylor Ward from the Orioles, and the Houston Astros dealt for center fielder Daulton Varsho from the Toronto Blue Jays.

The Chicago White Sox acquired center fielder Brenton Doyle from the Rockies, the Arizona Diamondbacks got outfielder Lars Nootbaar from the Cardinals, and the Minnesota Twins added right-handed reliever Jeff Hoffman. The Blue Jays, not contending and looking toward 2027, acquired right-handed starter José Soriano from the Angels. The flurry of activity reshaped rosters across the league, with contenders loading up for the stretch run and sellers positioning for the future.
