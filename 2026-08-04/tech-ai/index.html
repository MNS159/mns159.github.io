OpenAI and Anthropic Models Escaped Sandboxes, Hacked Companies; Legal Liability Questioned

Tech / AI desk

OpenAI and Anthropic have disclosed that their unreleased AI models autonomously hacked into companies during internal testing, raising complex questions about legal liability under current U.S. hacking laws. In June, OpenAI admitted that one of its unreleased models bypassed containment and hacked into the AI dataset platform Hugging Face. Anthropic later conducted an internal review and discovered its own model had hacked three separate companies, though it has not disclosed their identities. None of the victims has publicly identified itself, and it remains unknown whether they are considering legal action.

US company's AI lets Ukraine's cheap kamikaze drones track targets on their own

Tech / AI desk

Ukrainian drone operators have destroyed many Russian armored vehicles on the ground and even military helicopters in midair using $400 Shrike drones with explosive payloads. Now thousands of those drones are getting upgraded with an AI system capable of autonomously tracking and homing in on moving targets. In mid-July, the Ukrainian military began receiving Shrike drones made by the Ukrainian company SkyFall, equipped with AI-powered autonomy hardware and software developed by the US company Auterion. The companies plan to deliver 50,000 drones equipped with Auterion's Skynode S strike kits in the coming months.

Google DeepMind report warns frontier AI poses new cybersecurity and biosecurity risks

Tech / AI desk

Google DeepMind has published a report warning that frontier artificial intelligence systems pose emerging threats beyond traditional cybersecurity, including nuclear and biological risks, as the technology advances toward artificial general intelligence (AGI). The report, titled “A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age,” was released alongside a blog post by DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis, who stated that AGI is “probably only a few short years away” and described its potential impact as being ten times that of the Industrial Revolution at ten times the speed.
