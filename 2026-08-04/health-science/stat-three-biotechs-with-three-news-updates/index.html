Eli Lilly opens early access applications for experimental obesity drug retatrutide

Health & Science desk

Eli Lilly will now accept applications from patients seeking early access to its next-generation obesity drug retatrutide, the company announced. The move comes more than a month after STAT reported that Lilly had granted a single 79-year-old patient special access to the experimental therapy.

Retatrutide is an investigational obesity treatment that Lilly is developing as a potential successor to its approved drugs. The company had previously made the therapy available to only one individual under a compassionate-use or special-access program.

By opening the application process to additional patients, Lilly is broadening the pool of people who may receive retatrutide before it receives regulatory approval. The company has not disclosed specific eligibility criteria or how many patients it will accommodate.

The decision follows scrutiny of Lilly’s earlier single-patient grant, which raised questions about equitable access to promising experimental medicines. The expanded program allows other patients to formally request the drug, though the company retains discretion over approvals.

Sources: STAT
