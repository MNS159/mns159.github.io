Oil Prices Fall as US Hints Hormuz Deal Near; Iran Focuses on Oman Talks

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Oil prices fell to a three-week low on Tuesday after senior U.S. officials expressed optimism that a deal to reopen the Strait of Hormuz could be reached within days. Brent crude, the global benchmark, dropped nearly 5% to under $80 a barrel, while U.S. West Texas Intermediate fell more than 5% to $76 a barrel, according to the BBC. The declines come as the market weighs the possibility of supply disruptions easing through the strategic waterway, which handles roughly one-fifth of the world's daily oil and liquefied natural gas supplies.

EU commends Spain's 'swift response' as 70,000 migrants returned from Ceuta

International & World desk

The European Union has commended Spain's "swift response" to the migrant crisis in Ceuta after about 72,000 people crossed into the Spanish North African territory from Morocco over 30-31 July, Spain's Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska said. An estimated 70,000 have since been returned to Morocco, he said, and EU interior ministers held emergency talks by video conference on Tuesday that were called after several member states expressed concern over Spain's handling of the influx, which prompted Madrid to deploy additional military and police forces.
