Dutch court halts Merck's new Keytruda formulation in Europe

Health & Science desk

A Dutch court has ruled that Merck infringed on a patent held by Halozyme Therapeutics related to a subcutaneous formulation of its blockbuster cancer drug Keytruda, ordering the company to stop manufacturing and marketing the medication in several European countries. The ruling applies to France, Italy, Sweden, Switzerland, and the Netherlands.

The decision is part of a widening patent infringement dispute between Merck and Halozyme over the best-selling cancer treatment. The court’s order does not affect the existing intravenous version of Keytruda, which remains available to patients.

Merck said in a statement that it “strongly” disagreed with the court’s decision.
