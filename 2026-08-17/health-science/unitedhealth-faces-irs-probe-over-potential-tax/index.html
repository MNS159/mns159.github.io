UnitedHealth faces IRS probe over potential tax avoidance

Health & Science desk

The Internal Revenue Service is investigating UnitedHealth Group, with an initial probe determining the health care conglomerate underpaid taxes during a four-year period by funneling money through a foreign subsidiary, the company disclosed in a recent regulatory filing.

UnitedHealth revealed that the IRS is “seeking to significantly increase taxable income” from 2017 through 2020 and may force UnitedHealth to pay more “for subsequent years after 2020.” The company received notices from the IRS in March, which have not been previously reported.

These types of audits from the IRS are exceedingly rare and focus on how big companies transfer profits within themselves. Given UnitedHealth’s size as one of the five largest companies in the world by revenue, this examination likely involves substantial sums of money.

The investigation centers on allegations that UnitedHealth avoided taxes by moving money through a foreign subsidiary. The outcome could result in significant additional tax payments for the years under review and potentially for later years as well.
