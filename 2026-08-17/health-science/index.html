RHIC data hint at hidden gluon structure in protons

Health & Science desk

New findings from the STAR detector at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) suggest that gluons, the particles that bind quarks together, may play a key role in carrying and conserving baryon number, a fundamental quantum property of protons. The results, published in Science, challenge the decades-old textbook assumption that baryon number belongs exclusively to a proton's three main 'valence' quarks. Instead, the data support the idea that baryon number is associated with a Y-shaped 'junction' of gluons connecting those quarks, a concept first proposed in the 1970s and refined in 1996 by theoretical physicist Dmitri Kharzeev.

Michigan cyclosporiasis outbreak approaches 14,000 cases

Health & Science desk

Michigan's cyclosporiasis outbreak has reached 13,909 documented cases, according to the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services, an increase from the 12,485 cases reported the previous week. The state is providing weekly updates, which now show 314 hospitalizations—up from 279—and two deaths, both reported earlier this month. The outbreak has been detected in 73 of Michigan's 83 counties, with Wayne, Oakland, Washtenaw, Ingham, and Genesee counties reporting the highest case numbers.
