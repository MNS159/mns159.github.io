Stripe Clinches Over $7 Billion Deal to Acquire AI Startup OpenRouter

Tech / AI desk

Stripe has finalized a deal to acquire OpenRouter, an AI gateway startup, for more than $7 billion, according to a report from Bloomberg. The acquisition, which follows earlier reports of talks between the two companies, marks a significant expansion for Stripe into the artificial intelligence infrastructure space.

OpenRouter provides a single access point that allows customers to select and switch between different AI models based on their specific needs and budget, helping to prevent vendor lock-in. The company announced in May that it had raised a $113 million Series B at a reported $1.3 billion valuation, with investors including Sequoia, Andreessen Horowitz, Menlo Ventures, and Alphabet’s Capital G.

At the time of the Series B, OpenRouter CEO Alex Atallah described the company as the equivalent of Stripe for AI, highlighting its role as a unified gateway for multiple AI systems. The startup also claimed to have 8 million global users and to provide access to more than 400 models.

The Wall Street Journal reported last month that Stripe and OpenRouter were in acquisition talks. Bloomberg now reports that those discussions have resulted in a deal price exceeding $7 billion. A Stripe spokesperson told TechCrunch that the company does not comment on rumors or speculation.
