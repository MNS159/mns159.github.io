Barcelona agree £65m deal to sign Rodri from Manchester City

Sports desk

Barcelona have agreed a deal to sign Spain captain Rodri from Manchester City for approximately £65m, according to multiple reports. The 30-year-old Ballon d'Or winner, who led Spain to the World Cup title this summer and was named player of the tournament, is set to sign a four-year contract with the Spanish champions. The transfer is expected to be completed in the coming days.

City had rejected Barcelona's opening offer of £38.5m last week, and an increased bid also fell short before the clubs reached an agreement on Sunday. Rodri, who entered the final year of his contract at the Etihad Stadium, had been the subject of interest from both Barcelona and Real Madrid, but Barcelona stepped up their efforts in recent days to secure the signing.

Rodri joined City from Atletico Madrid in 2019 for a then-club-record £62.8m and made 298 appearances across seven trophy-laden seasons. He won 12 major honors, including four Premier League titles, two FA Cups, three EFL Cups, and the Champions League in 2022-23, scoring the only goal in the final against Inter Milan. He missed much of the 2024-25 campaign after sustaining a serious knee injury, and parts of last season were disrupted by a hamstring issue, but returned to form at the World Cup.

Speaking after City's 3-0 Community Shield loss to Arsenal on Sunday, new manager Enzo Maresca said of Rodri: 'He's a top player, all the teams need a Rodri, so it's not about if we miss Rodri or not.' Maresca added that Rodri had only returned to the club on Friday after undergoing back surgery following the World Cup, and that he gave him a 'big hug' when they met.

City have already undergone major midfield changes this summer, spending £116m on Elliot Anderson and agreeing to sell Tijjani Reijnders to Al Qadisah. The club have been linked with Lille's 18-year-old Ayyoub Bouaddi, who would cost an estimated £85m, as well as Chelsea's Enzo Fernandez, Bournemouth's Alex Scott, and Liverpool's Alexis Mac Allister. Chelsea had set a Friday deadline for bids for Fernandez, seeking £120m, but no offers were received; City are understood to still believe a deal is possible.
