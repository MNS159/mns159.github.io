Ukraine War

Ukraine launches massive aerial attack on Russia, killing at least six

International & World desk

Ukraine launched one of its largest aerial attacks of the war against Russia overnight on Saturday, killing at least six people and injuring four others in the Belgorod region, according to Russian authorities. Acting Governor Alexander Shuvaev said a missile attack on a village in the Valuysky district killed six people and wounded four, including a 14-year-old child who was hospitalized. A separate drone strike in Russia's southern Astrakhan region killed a woman, local Governor Igor Babushkin reported, adding that the strike targeted an industrial facility but caused no damage.

Russia's Defense Ministry said it destroyed 822 Ukrainian drones overnight, with Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin reporting that about 600 drones were headed toward the capital and that more than a third were shot down over the Moscow region. Moscow region Governor Andrey Vorobyov described the assault as "one of the most massive drone attacks in recent memory" and said an 83-year-old man was killed when a drone hit a private home. In the Rostov region, five people died after more than 150 drones targeted three towns, damaging homes and a railway station and sparking a forest fire, according to Governor Yury Slyusar. A large fire also engulfed a 250,000-square-meter warehouse near Podolsk belonging to Russian online retailer Wildberries, which Ukraine's military said was disabled. Ukraine's Defense Ministry stated that seven of Wildberries' 10 largest logistics centers have been taken out of operation since July, though Wildberries and Moscow deny the warehouses are affiliated with the military.

The attacks came as Russia also launched intense missile and drone strikes across Ukraine, killing at least seven people and wounding more than 39 over the weekend. In the central city of Kryvyi Rih, a Russian missile attack killed two people and wounded 14, including at a steel plant where 13 employees were injured and production was partially halted. A ballistic missile attack on Kyiv injured six and sparked fires, including one that destroyed a large part of the Petrovka book market. In the Sumy region, two people were killed by Russian drone strikes, and in the Zaporizhzhia region, a man and woman died after a guided aerial bomb hit a private home. In Odesa, an overnight attack damaged a civilian vessel and injured four people, according to regional head Oleh Kiper. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said 13 regions had come under attack this week, with Russia launching more than 1,550 attack drones, nearly 1,560 guided aerial bombs, and 62 missiles.

The escalating air war reflects a significant expansion of Ukraine's long-range strike capabilities, using largely homegrown technologies such as the Flamingo cruise missile and the FP-1 long-range drone to hit targets up to 1,500 miles from the border. Both sides' air defenses are stretched, leading to rising civilian casualties. In July alone, 437 civilians were killed in Ukraine, the highest monthly toll since May 2022, according to a United Nations tally. Russian authorities reported 79 civilians killed in Ukrainian attacks in July, up from the previous month. An F-18 or aircraft of NATO shot down an unidentified drone that crossed into Romanian airspace, which a NATO military spokesperson said "appears to be Russian." The war, now in its third year since Russia's full-scale invasion in February 2022, has seen front-line fighting largely stalled, with both sides intensifying strikes deep into each other's territory.
