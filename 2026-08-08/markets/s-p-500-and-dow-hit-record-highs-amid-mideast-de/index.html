S&P 500 and Dow hit record highs amid Mideast deal hopes and AI earnings; Palantir surges on strong earnings

Financial & Markets desk

The S&P 500 and Dow Jones Industrial Average closed at record highs on Friday, buoyed by a weaker-than-expected July jobs report that sharply reduced the odds of a Federal Reserve interest rate hike in September, as well as a surge in AI-related stocks following strong earnings reports. The U.S. economy surprisingly shed jobs in July, sending Treasury yields lower and stocks higher as investors priced in a new outlook for the rate path.

According to prediction market platform Kalshi, the odds that the Fed holds rates steady at its September meeting jumped to 65% after the jobs report, up from about 50-50 before the release. On CME's FedWatch tool, the probability of the central bank maintaining rates rose to 60%, compared with 45% on Thursday and just one-in-three a week ago. The weaker labor market data may change how the Fed thinks about rate hikes, which some members have called for amid higher energy prices due to the U.S.-Iran war. At the Fed's July meeting, three members of the Federal Open Market Committee dissented, arguing the bank should have raised rates rather than held them steady.

"Today's weak payrolls print may ease the pressure on the Fed to raise rates at its September meeting, but next week's inflation data will still likely be the deciding factor," said Ellen Zentner, chief economic strategist at Morgan Stanley Wealth Management. "If those numbers come in hotter than expected, a cooler labor market may not be enough to quiet the calls for hikes inside the Fed." The Consumer Price Index for July is set to be released on Aug. 12. In June, prices posted their biggest month-over-month fall in six years as energy prices fell, though oil rose in July amid renewed tensions in the Middle East.

Despite the shift in September expectations, rate hikes this year are still not out of the question. Even after the report, CME's FedWatch tool still sees a 55% chance of a hike in October and an almost 75% chance in December. The jobs report showed the labor market weakening after a period of resiliency in 2026, which may make raising interest rates to slow the economy appear more risky.

In corporate news, Palantir Technologies surged on strong earnings, while other AI-related stocks also rallied. Atlassian Corporation shares rose more than 29% in premarket trading after the company beat FactSet consensus on revenue and earnings for its fourth quarter. Doximity more than doubled at one point in premarket trading after CEO Jeffrey Tangney said the company's new AI search tool brings in 10 times what it costs to run. Twilio shares surged more than 17% after the company boosted its full-year revenue growth forecast. Cloudflare jumped more than 16.5% after issuing solid guidance. Trade Desk shares tanked 27% after posting second-quarter earnings and revenue that fell below expectations. Solar stocks rose after President Donald Trump imposed tariffs on products imported to make solar panels, with First Solar jumping more than 5%. Airbnb shares surged nearly 7% after the company posted second-quarter earnings that outpaced analyst forecasts.
