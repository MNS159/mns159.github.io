OpenAI pauses Astra model development after it reaches critical cybersecurity threshold

Tech / AI desk

OpenAI said Friday it has paused internal activities around an in-development AI model, Astra, after internal evaluations found it reached the company’s “critical cybersecurity threshold” under its Preparedness Framework. The framework defines that threshold as a model that can independently identify and develop functional zero-day exploits of all severity levels in many hardened real-world critical systems without human intervention, or devise and execute end-to-end novel cyberattack strategies against hardened targets given only a high-level desired goal.
