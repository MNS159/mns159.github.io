Tesla cleared for Las Vegas robotaxi launch; stock jumps

Financial & Markets desk

Tesla shares jumped Friday after the company received regulatory clearance to launch its robotaxi service in Las Vegas, adding to a broader market rally that saw the Dow Jones Industrial Average gain 500 points. The automaker also plans to soon deploy its Cybercab in Austin, Texas, and has been given the go-ahead to operate thousands of vehicles in Nevada.

The stock move came amid a risk-on session that lifted bitcoin to a three-month high and boosted crypto-related names. SpaceX stock also rose, while Nvidia tested a key moving average. The broader market was working to recover from a sell-off in the previous session.

Tesla's robotaxi clearance in Nevada marks a significant step toward commercializing its autonomous driving technology. The company has been developing the Cybercab, a purpose-built autonomous vehicle, and plans to begin service in Austin as well. The Nevada approval allows for the deployment of thousands of vehicles in Las Vegas.

The news adds to a string of developments for Tesla as it pushes to expand its autonomous vehicle operations. The company has been testing its self-driving technology in various markets and now has regulatory approval to launch a commercial robotaxi service in a major U.S. city.
