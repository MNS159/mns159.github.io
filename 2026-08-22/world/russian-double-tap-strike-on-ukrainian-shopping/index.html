Russian double-tap drone strike on Ukrainian shopping mall kills 16; Zelensky vows response

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters
LiberalConservative
Coverage tilts slightly center-left

Rescuers in Ukraine worked through the night to search for survivors after a Russian double-tap drone strike on a busy shopping centre in Kryvyi Rih killed 16 people and wounded 130, local officials said. The attack, which President Volodymyr Zelensky condemned as “cynical and despicable,” occurred in two waves: the first drone hit the mall, and a second struck about half an hour later, targeting emergency responders at the scene. Nine people remain missing, including two children, according to regional military head Oleksandr Hanzha. Twenty-three children were among the injured, with 14 of them still hospitalized as of Saturday morning; three children—two boys aged 10 and 16 and an 11-year-old girl—were in serious or extremely serious condition after undergoing surgery, said Oleksandr Vilkul, head of the city’s defence council.

The EU’s foreign policy chief, Kaja Kallas, condemned the attack as “terror by design” and said she was putting forward “the most far-reaching Russia sanctions listings since the start of the war.” Zelensky, speaking in an evening video post, called the strike “an absolute Russian atrocity” and vowed: “We will definitely respond.” He urged Ukraine’s allies to impose “real pressure on the aggressor,” saying, “For peace to be possible, Russia must face real accountability.” Rescue teams from seven other Ukrainian regions were deployed to help search for survivors. Russia’s military has not commented on the strike.

Both sides continued to trade strikes overnight. Ukraine’s military said it hit the Novokuybyshevsk oil refinery in Russia’s Samara region, causing a fire, and also struck a logistics hub belonging to Russian online retailer Ozon. Zelensky said the refinery strike, 1,000 kilometers from the front line, deprived Russia of “millions of dollars in export revenues.” Russia’s Defence Ministry said it downed 457 Ukrainian drones overnight. In Russia, two children were killed and two adults injured in a strike on the port town of Yeysk in the Krasnodar region, according to Governor Veniamin Kondratyev. Local officials in Belgorod said a strike killed two and injured 13. In Ukraine, one person was killed by a ballistic missile strike on railway infrastructure in Kyiv, and another was killed by a drone strike in Zaporizhzhia, local authorities said. Four people—including three children—were killed in separate Russian attacks in Ukraine’s southern Mykolaiv region on Friday, and two more in the north-eastern Kharkiv region.

The attack on Kryvyi Rih, an industrial city about 60 km (37 miles) from the front line and Zelensky’s hometown, came as Russia has intensified its bombardments of Ukrainian cities in recent months. On Thursday, 17 people were killed and more than 40 injured in a major Russian missile and drone attack on Kyiv. Ukraine has in recent weeks intensified its own strikes on Russian energy facilities and warehouses, describing them as legitimate targets that help finance Russia’s war. Russian President Vladimir Putin launched a full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022, and Moscow currently controls about a fifth of Ukrainian territory in the south-east.

Ukraine’s allies are to meet on Monday, Ukraine’s independence day, to reaffirm support for Kyiv. The meeting of the so-called “Coalition of the Willing” will be co-chaired by French President Emmanuel Macron, UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham, and German Chancellor Friedrich Merz; about 10 leaders, including European Council chief António Costa, are expected in Kyiv. Zelensky held a call with UN Secretary-General António Guterres on Friday, updating him on the civilian casualties. The United Nations reported that the civilian death toll so far this year had risen to its highest level since the first months of the war.
