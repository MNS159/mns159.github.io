TikTok to pay $400 million to settle US child privacy lawsuit

Tech / AI desk

TikTok and its parent company, ByteDance, have agreed to pay $400 million to settle a U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) lawsuit alleging that the social media platform violated federal children's privacy laws. The DOJ announced the settlement on Friday, resolving a case first filed in 2024 under the Biden administration. The settlement is one of the largest recoveries ever obtained in a case involving the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

Apple lays off staff from Vision Pro and Siri teams

Tech / AI desk

Apple is cutting more than 200 jobs, with roughly 100 positions eliminated from the team behind the Vision Pro headset and another 100 from the Siri team and the Intelligent Systems Experience group, which focuses on integrating AI into devices, according to a Bloomberg report confirmed by the company. The cuts include the "largely shutting down" of a Vision Pro gaming team and a reduction in the size of the team that produces immersive content for the headset, Bloomberg reported.

Tesla Recalls Nearly 3 Million Vehicles in China Over Hidden Emergency Door Releases, Driver Monitoring

Tech / AI desk

Tesla is recalling nearly 3 million vehicles in China as part of a nationwide push by regulators to address the dangers of hidden emergency door releases. The recall covers approximately 2.98 million Model 3 and Model Y electric vehicles, which will receive warning labels and a software update that automatically lowers all windows in the event of a collision. A second, overlapping recall affecting about 2.74 million vehicles will update the driver monitoring system to use the interior camera to track driver gaze rather than relying solely on a steering wheel torque sensor.

Biotech

Outer Biosciences keeps human skin alive for weeks, trains AI on living tissue

Tech / AI desk

Michael Polansky, co-founder of Outer Biosciences and partner of Lady Gaga, is training an artificial intelligence model on human skin that remains alive outside the body for up to a month. The startup, founded in 2022, has raised roughly $23 million from backers including Wing Ventures and Initialized, and employs 19 people. The living tissue, sourced from discarded plastic surgery through nonprofit and commercial biobanks, is fed nutrients and cleared of waste by a proprietary support system, maintaining its original structure and molecular programs for weeks — far beyond the industry norm of a few days.
