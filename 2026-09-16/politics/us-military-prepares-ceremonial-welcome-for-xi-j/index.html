US military prepares ceremonial welcome for Xi Jinping at White House

Politics desk
✓ Facts
  • A US military honour guard held a rehearsal at the White House ahead of Chinese President Xi Jinping's expected visit to the United States next week.
  • Fox News correspondent Lucas Tomlinson posted a video of the rehearsal on social media on Tuesday afternoon Washington time.
  • The video showed service members from multiple US military branches in ceremonial uniform standing in formation.
  • The rehearsed drill mirrors the welcome ceremony China staged for US President Donald Trump during his May 14 visit to Beijing.
  • China's ceremony for Trump featured a People's Liberation Army honour guard in the square outside the east gate of the Great Hall of the People.
  • Trump, in an interview with Fox News, praised the Chinese military's marching during that ceremony as 'incredible'.
○ Analysis

The rehearsal reflects standard diplomatic choreography ahead of a high-profile state visit, in which host nations often design ceremonial welcomes that echo or reciprocate those extended to their own leader abroad. Here, the US appears to be preparing an honour guard ceremony for Xi's visit that consciously parallels the elaborate welcome China gave Trump in Beijing in May, suggesting an effort toward visual and protocol parity between the two governments. Such ceremonial exchanges are a routine, symbolic part of US-China diplomacy and do not themselves indicate the substance of policy discussions that may occur during the visit.

Implications

If the ceremonial welcome proceeds as rehearsed, it will set a formal, reciprocal tone for Xi's visit, though the substantive outcomes of any meetings between the two leaders will depend on discussions not covered in this report.

Sources: South China Morning Post
