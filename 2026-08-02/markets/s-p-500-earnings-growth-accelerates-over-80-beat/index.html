S&P 500 Earnings Growth Accelerates, Over 80% Beat Estimates

Financial & Markets desk

S&P 500 earnings growth accelerated this week as more than 155 companies reported across key sectors, with 84% of those firms topping earnings-per-share estimates and 72% posting year-over-year profit growth, according to Seeking Alpha’s Earnings Scoreboard. The strong results were headlined by several of the so-called 'Magnificent Seven' mega-cap technology stocks, including Microsoft (MSFT), Apple (AAPL), Meta Platforms (META), and Amazon (AMZN), alongside chip makers.

Amazon was the latest Big Tech company to report abnormally large earnings growth, driven in part by paper gains on its investments in Anthropic, according to MarketWatch. Microsoft and Amazon notably outperformed with strong revenue and EPS growth fueled by cloud and AI demand, while Apple and Meta disappointed on some growth drivers and profitability, Seeking Alpha noted.

Most S&P 500 companies beat earnings and revenue estimates, with strong year-over-year growth seen in both metrics across multiple sectors. Seeking Alpha’s Quick Insights highlighted that investors should focus on consumer discretionary, healthcare, technology, industrials, energy, and real estate sectors going forward.

Major reports still expected include McDonald's, Eli Lilly, Palantir, and Caterpillar, according to Seeking Alpha. The continued acceleration in profit growth and the high beat rate underscore the resilience of corporate earnings amid shifting economic conditions.
