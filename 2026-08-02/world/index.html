Migration

EU holds emergency meeting as Ceuta migrant crisis death toll rises to 72

International & World desk

The European Union has called an emergency video conference of interior ministers for Tuesday after an estimated 50,000 to 60,000 migrants entered Spain’s North African enclave of Ceuta over a three-day period, leaving at least 72 people dead. The bodies of the deceased were pulled from the sea, where they floated alongside abandoned rubber rings and flippers, according to Spanish authorities. Most of the migrants who crossed have since returned to Morocco, escorted by Spanish soldiers and police, and the situation in Ceuta has returned to near normal, Spanish Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska said.

Ukraine War

Ukrainian drones strike Russian oil refineries and Wildberries warehouse, killing eight

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Ukrainian drones struck multiple oil refineries and a Wildberries e-commerce warehouse across Russia overnight, killing at least eight people, regional governors said on Saturday. Ukraine’s General Staff confirmed it hit an oil refinery in Saratov, the Engels airbase, and an oil depot in Kaluga Oblast, part of a campaign to degrade Russia’s war-fighting capability. In Samara Oblast, a Wildberries warehouse in Novosemeykino was set ablaze; the company lists drone components and body armor on its website, and its banking arm was sanctioned by the European Union in July.
