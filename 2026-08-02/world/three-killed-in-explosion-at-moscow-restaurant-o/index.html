Three killed in Moscow restaurant blast; mayor calls it 'brutal terrorist act'

International & World desk

At least three people were killed and 21 others injured Saturday evening when a homemade explosive device detonated at the entrance of an upscale Italian restaurant in central Moscow, Russian authorities said. The National Anti-Terrorist Committee reported that a woman carrying the bomb tried to enter the Balzi Rossi restaurant on Kudrinskaya Square but was stopped by a security guard. The device exploded, killing the woman, the guard, and a restaurant patron.

The blast occurred at approximately 8:00 p.m. local time (17:00 GMT) near one of Moscow's seven Stalin-era skyscrapers. The restaurant's website said the venue had been closed for a private event. The Kommersant daily newspaper cited sources as saying the bomb was intended to harm guests on the restaurant's summer terrace. Heavily armed law enforcement officers sealed off the area, and roads around the square were briefly closed. A nearby evening cycling event was canceled.

Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin described the explosion as a 'brutal terrorist act' in an online post on Sunday, adding that 'those responsible for this crime will certainly be found and face the punishment they deserve.' He did not identify any suspects. The Moscow Investigative Committee said the attack was still under investigation, and no one had publicly claimed responsibility as of early Sunday afternoon.

Russian officials have not commented on any possible link to the war in Ukraine. In recent years, several senior Russian military figures have been assassinated in and around Moscow, with the government blaming Kyiv for those attacks. The explosion came amid a wave of Ukrainian drone strikes deep inside Russia overnight, including an attack on an apartment block in the city of Engels that killed two people and a strike on a Wildberries warehouse in Samara Oblast. Russia's Defense Ministry reported over 100 Ukrainian strikes in the Belgorod Oblast, leaving 14 people injured, and claimed 635 Ukrainian drones were shot down overall.

The Balzi Rossi restaurant apologized on social media for an initial post that said everything was safe on the premises, calling it 'highly inappropriate given the circumstances.' The restaurant said staff were focused on assisting guests and emergency services. State news agency RIA Novosti released video showing armed law enforcement officers at the cordoned-off scene.
