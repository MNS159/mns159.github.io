Warriors rally from 25-point deficit to rout Lakers; Curry drops 31 in NBA Cup win

Sports desk

The Golden State Warriors erased a 25-point first-half deficit Friday night to defeat the Los Angeles Lakers 112-95 at Chase Center, improving to 3-3 after an 0-3 start. Stephen Curry led the comeback with 31 points on 11-of-23 shooting (8-of-14 from 3-point range), six assists and four rebounds, passing Dominique Wilkins for 18th on the NBA's all-time scoring list. The 17-point margin of victory is the largest ever by a team that trailed by 25 points in the same game in the play-by-play era (since 1996).

Luka Dončić poured in 21 first-quarter points and finished with 37 points, seven rebounds and five assists, outscoring the entire Warriors team in the opening period as the Lakers raced to a 40-15 run. Dončić had been involved in a war of words with Warriors rookie Yaxel Lendeborg, who after his first career NBA game called the Lakers star a flopping crybaby for whom he 'lost all respect.' Friday night, Lendeborg struggled mightily, finishing with six points, one rebound, zero assists and five turnovers in 24 minutes before fouling out, posting a game-low minus-20.

The Warriors responded with a 38-13 run over 11 minutes and 39 seconds to tie the game by halftime. Curry provided the signature moment in the third quarter, dropping Dončić with a move before hitting a 3-pointer to cut the deficit to six. Brandin Podziemski added 21 points, six assists and five rebounds; De'Anthony Melton and Georges Niang combined for 27 points and seven 3-pointers; and Draymond Green took over defensive duties on Dončić while dishing out nine assists. Golden State's bench outscored the Lakers' bench 50-7, and the defense held Los Angeles to 57 points over the final three quarters.

After the Warriors' season-opening loss to these same Lakers, coach Steve Kerr told Lendeborg that individual defense on Dončić was meaningless if the team got torched. 'It's a team game,' Kerr said. Friday night, Lendeborg needed his teammates to cover for him, and they did. The win also gives the Warriors a significant early edge in NBA Cup group-stage play, where they share a group with the Lakers, San Antonio Spurs, Portland Trail Blazers and Sacramento Kings.
