Warsh Plan to Remake Fed: Fewer Rate Votes, More Deliberation; 2027 Calendar Confirmed

Financial & Markets desk
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

The Federal Reserve on Thursday released its 2027 meeting schedule, confirming that the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC) will hold eight meetings but vote on interest rates at only seven of them. The October 26-27 gathering will be dedicated to "broad monetary policy strategy issues," the Fed said, marking the first time a scheduled meeting will not include a rate decision [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/). The change, announced last month by Chair Kevin Warsh, reduces the number of rate-setting meetings from the long-standing eight per year to seven [nhk.or.jp](https://www3.nhk.or.jp/nhkworld/news/20261113de75679/).

Under the new calendar, the final rate-setting meeting of 2027 will be held November 16-17, replacing a previously scheduled December 7-8 meeting. After that, the FOMC will not meet again until January 25-26, 2028, creating a two-month gap that Warsh said would allow more economic data to accumulate between meetings [investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/fed-sets-new-meeting-schedule-for-2027-as-foretold-by-warsh-5023024). Warsh has said he expects the committee to hold two strategy-only meetings and six rate-setting meetings in 2028 [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/).

Warsh first floated the schedule change during the FOMC's July meeting, according to minutes, arguing that six meetings per year held every two months would "allow more information to accumulate between meetings" [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/). He formally announced the plan last month after the committee held interest rates steady at a range of 3.75% to 4%. "Getting policy right involves assessing near-term developments at timely, regular, and information-rich intervals," Warsh told reporters. "But it's equally important we reserve time and space, as an intellectually diverse group, for strategic deliberations — for thinking anew about big questions, looking out over longer time horizons, focusing, as I say, on the left side of the decimal point" [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/).

The schedule change is the latest example of Warsh putting his imprint on the central bank since succeeding Jerome Powell in May. He has also declined to submit his own "dot" projections in the FOMC's quarterly economic outlook, making him the only one of the 19 committee members not to do so. Warsh has said market participants "are learning to play the ball, not the referee" without such forward guidance [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/). He has established a task force to review the Fed's communications practices, including whether to continue publishing the dot plot. The Fed's updated meeting page no longer marks the March, June, and September 2027 meetings with an asterisk indicating they are gatherings for quarterly economic projections, a practice Warsh has been disdainful of since it began under former Chair Ben Bernanke [investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/economy-news/fed-sets-new-meeting-schedule-for-2027-as-foretold-by-warsh-5023024).

The move drew praise from Jason Furman, who chaired the Council of Economic Advisers under President Barack Obama. "I have long thought the Fed spends too much time on the immediate and not nearly enough time thinking about longer-term strategy," Furman wrote on X, calling the change a "great move" [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/). Under the Federal Reserve Act, the FOMC is required to meet at least four times per year [thehill.com](https://thehill.com/business/6219883-fed-announces-2027-meeting-schedule/).
