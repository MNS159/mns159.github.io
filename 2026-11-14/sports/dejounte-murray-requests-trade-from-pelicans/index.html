Dejounte Murray Requests Trade from Pelicans; Suns, Heat, Bucks Emerge as Potential Destinations

Sports desk

New Orleans Pelicans point guard Dejounte Murray has requested a trade, the team confirmed Friday, with agent Rich Paul working alongside team officials on a resolution. ESPN’s Shams Charania first reported the request. The Pelicans said in a statement that they "will evaluate potential trade opportunities that are in the best interest of our organization." Murray was ruled out for Friday night’s NBA Cup game against the Memphis Grizzlies, which the Pelicans lost 142-111 to fall to 4-9.

Murray, 30, is averaging 13.4 points, 4.6 rebounds, 4.4 assists and 1.6 steals this season. He started the season with averages of 17.8 points, 5.8 rebounds and 6.0 assists in four October games, but his numbers have dropped sharply; in his last five games he has averaged 10.0 points, 3.6 rebounds and 3.2 assists, missing the 10-point mark three times. Murray is trying to regain form after being limited to 14 games last season due to an Achilles injury, which he called his "worst experience in the NBA" during an appearance on The Pivot last year. He also recently defended himself from fans blaming him for the team’s struggles in deleted social media posts.

According to NBA insider Chris Haynes, the Phoenix Suns "have registered interest" in Murray. The Athletic’s Sam Amick reported that Murray has a "strong interest" in going to Phoenix and that his camp has been hopeful a deal would materialize in which he would be swapped for Suns guard Jalen Green. Amick added that team sources on both sides recently insisted there had not been any talks about that deal, but that "certainly could change." ClutchPoints’ Brett Siegel also reported that the Pelicans could call the Suns about a possible deal. The Athletic’s Sam Amick earlier reported that opposing clubs feel the Suns are "motivated" to trade Green for a proven point guard.

Other potential landing spots include the Miami Heat and the Milwaukee Bucks, per Haynes. ESPN’s Bobby Marks explained the salary cap implications of a Murray trade. The Heat could benefit from having another setup man for Giannis Antetokounmpo and Bam Adebayo, which would relieve Davion Mitchell of ball-handling duties and move veteran sharpshooter Klay Thompson to a sixth-man role. The Bucks recently benched Ryan Rollins to start rookie Brayden Burries alongside Tyler Herro, but the youngster has struggled to find his footing. The Pelicans are already prepared to hand the reins to second-year point guard Jeremiah Fears, making a trade likely sooner rather than later.

Green, in his second season in Phoenix, is averaging 11.8 points, 3.4 rebounds and 3.1 assists, with his numbers regressing from his rookie campaign. Both players could benefit from a change of scenery, and a potential swap between the Pelicans and Suns is a situation to monitor. Murray’s trade request comes after he posted on X following a loss to the Brooklyn Nets on Wednesday, making a bold allegation about the team that sources described as the last straw for the guard, who is unhappy with his role.
