Apple Launches HomeView Smart Display, Entering Smart Home Hub Market

Tech / AI desk

Apple on Tuesday announced the HomeView, its first smart home display, priced at $299 and available for preorder starting today, with shipping beginning November 13. The device features a 7-inch square Liquid Retina touchscreen, built-in Siri AI, and comes in two models at the same price: a countertop version with a speaker base and a wall-mounted version. However, the wall-mounted model requires a separate mount, starting at $59.95, which is not included in the $299 price.

The HomeView is designed as a smart home controller, personal assistant, and home hub. It can control smart home devices via the Apple Home app, display live feeds from HomeKit Secure Video cameras, and authenticate actions like unlocking a door using Face ID or an iPhone. The device runs on Siri AI and, when connected to an Apple Intelligence-enabled iPhone or iPad, can pull personal context from messages, calendar appointments, and emails to take actions, such as setting a coffee maker to turn on based on a contact's arrival time.

The display supports swipe gestures to navigate between customizable "faces" resembling Apple Watch faces, widgets, and on-board apps including Calendar, Messages, Photos, FaceTime, Notes, Reminders, Music, and Podcasts. It also features a guided cooking experience that can transfer recipes from an iPhone via Handoff and reformat them with in-line timers. Other capabilities include FaceTime calls with Center Stage, music playback, and a 12 MP camera that can function as a mirror.

The HomeView can recognize up to six individual household members, automatically showing personalized information such as messages, contacts, and calendar appointments when a person approaches. The wall-mounted version is available in two colors and requires a power solution; Apple offers three mounts from Schneider Home: a Power Mount USB-C Wall Connector starting at $59.95, a Power Mount AC Wired starting at $79.95, and a Power Mount PoE Wired available in early 2027 for $129.95. The countertop model includes a speaker base with a custom down-firing woofer and two passive radiators.

The launch positions Apple in direct competition with Amazon's Echo Show and Google's Nest Hub smart displays. The HomeView is part of a broader push into the home, alongside the announcement of new Apple TV 4K with built-in microphones for Siri, and a new Apple Home Collection including a video doorbell, smart lock, and security cameras. Apple has had a mixed history in the smart home space, with products like the HomePod struggling to match the popularity of competing devices.
