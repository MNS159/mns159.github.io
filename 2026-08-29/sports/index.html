Liverpool agree £123m deal for PSG's Barcola

Sports desk

Liverpool have agreed a deal worth up to £123 million for Paris St-Germain forward Bradley Barcola, according to multiple sources. The package includes an initial £106 million with £17 million in potential add-ons, and the 23-year-old France international is expected to undergo a medical this weekend before signing a long-term contract at Anfield. PSG initially valued Barcola at £145 million.
