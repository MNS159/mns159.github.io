Liverpool agree £123m deal for PSG's Barcola

Sports desk

Liverpool have agreed a deal worth up to £123 million for Paris St-Germain forward Bradley Barcola, according to multiple sources. The package includes an initial £106 million with £17 million in potential add-ons, and the 23-year-old France international is expected to undergo a medical this weekend before signing a long-term contract at Anfield. PSG initially valued Barcola at £145 million.

Barcola, who prefers to play on the left wing but can also operate on the right or through the middle, joined PSG from Lyon in 2023 for a reported £38.5 million. During his time in Paris, he has won three Ligue 1 titles, two Champions Leagues, the French Cup twice, the Uefa Super Cup and the Fifa Intercontinental Cup. He scored three goals for France in this summer's World Cup.

The fee Liverpool have agreed includes covering a solidarity payment PSG owe Lyon as part of the original transfer. Barcola's arrival is expected to increase doubts around the future of Liverpool forward Cody Gakpo, with both Tottenham Hotspur and Manchester City having shown interest in the Netherlands international, according to multiple sources. Liverpool are also in the market for a right-winger, having explored moves for Brighton's Yankuba Minteh and Crystal Palace's Ismaila Sarr.

Speaking before Saturday's game against Nottingham Forest, Liverpool boss Andoni Iraola said of Gakpo: 'He has been very good since he came back from the World Cup and his holiday, with a very good attitude. I hope we can keep him at that level because I think he has been very influential in our games.' Iraola added that he 'cannot guarantee anything' regarding Gakpo's future but wants to keep the 27-year-old at the club.

Barcola has become a main target for Liverpool as they look to bolster their attack following the departures of Luis Diaz and Mohamed Salah in the past two summers. The deal, which would be a record fee for a player leaving Ligue 1, is seen as making sense for both teams: PSG will more than triple their investment for a player entering the last two years of his contract who has shown no willingness to sign an extension, while Liverpool have secured one of Europe's most exciting forwards after holding firm earlier in the summer on PSG's £145 million valuation.
