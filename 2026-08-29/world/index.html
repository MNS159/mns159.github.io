Nepal-Tibet floods: missing number rises to 2,478 as rescue efforts continue

International & World desk

The number of people missing after catastrophic flash floods and mudslides struck the Nepal-Tibet border on Wednesday has risen to 2,478, according to authorities in both countries. Nepal’s disaster agency reported 1,924 missing on its side, while China’s state news agency Xinhua put the figure in Tibet at 554. The confirmed death toll stands at 579 in Nepal and seven in China, with Indian authorities reporting seven additional bodies recovered from rivers believed to be flood victims. The International Red Cross estimates that more than 93,000 people have been directly affected and are in urgent need of humanitarian assistance.

Iran War

Iran Leaders Acknowledge War’s Economic Toll as Pentagon Munitions Drain Raises Concerns

International & World desk

Iranian leaders have acknowledged the mounting economic damage of six months of war with the United States, with President Masoud Pezeshkian saying foreign trade has slumped by nearly 35 percent under U.S. sanctions and a naval blockade. The admissions came Saturday as Tehran signaled it would not back down, pledging to withstand U.S. pressure, pursue diplomacy, and maintain what it says is control over the Strait of Hormuz. The government said addressing the economic fallout was now a central focus, including curbing inflation, creating jobs, and gradually reducing dependence on the dollar.

Royalty

Haakon VIII ascends Norway's throne as nation mourns King Harald

International & World desk

Norway began a period of national mourning on Friday after the death of King Harald V, Europe’s oldest monarch, at the age of 89. Harald, who reigned for 35 years, died at 06:35 local time (04:35 GMT) at Oslo University Hospital, where he had been treated since August 17 for haemolytic anaemia, a rare blood condition. His son, 53-year-old Crown Prince Haakon, immediately ascended the throne as King Haakon VIII, holding an extraordinary council of state meeting with government ministers and adopting the royal family's motto 'All for Norway'.

International & World desk

President Donald Trump announced Friday a new US oil agreement with Venezuela’s interim president Delcy Rodríguez, saying the US will have majority control of more than 65 billion barrels of proven oil reserves at no cost to American taxpayers.
