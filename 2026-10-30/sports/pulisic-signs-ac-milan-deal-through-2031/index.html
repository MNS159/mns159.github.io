Pulisic signs AC Milan deal through 2031

Sports desk

AC Milan announced Friday that American forward Christian Pulisic has signed a contract extension keeping him at the club through June 30, 2031. The new deal adds four years to his previous contract, which was set to expire in the summer of 2027. Pulisic joined AC Milan in July 2023 from Chelsea for approximately $20 million.

In its announcement, AC Milan said Pulisic has established himself as a key member of the team since arriving, highlighting his technical ability, character, consistency and work ethic. The club noted that he has scored 44 goals in 141 appearances for the Rossoneri, underscoring his importance to the squad and his role in the club's future plans. The extension reflects a shared commitment to building on progress made together and achieving new milestones.

Pulisic's time in Serie A has included both highs and lows. He became an immediate key player under Stefano Pioli but faced challenges adapting to different tactical systems under a series of managers — Pioli, Paulo Fonseca, Sergio Conceicao, Massimiliano Allegri and current coach Ruben Amorim. Recurring injuries have forced him to miss significant playing time over the past two years, and the new deal is seen as a chance for greater stability on the pitch.

When fit, Pulisic has proven himself one of Serie A's best performers, and both his coach and teammates rely on his presence. That reliability was a major reason AC Milan, under American owner Gerry Cardinale, chose to extend his contract. In the summer of 2026, when the club underwent changes in management and coaching, Amorim and Cardinale agreed that Pulisic should be a key part of the new cycle, which they hope will bring trophies and lead into the 2031-32 season, when AC Milan plans to move into a new stadium unveiled jointly with city rivals Inter on Thursday. The announcement came ahead of Saturday's Derby della Madonnina against Inter at San Siro.
