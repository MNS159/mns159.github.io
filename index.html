Congo Ebola outbreak death toll surpasses 4,000 after treatment center burned

Health & Science desk · anchored on AP

Congo’s Ebola outbreak has killed more than 4,000 people since it was declared in eastern Congo on May 15, the Health Ministry announced Thursday, as the country continues to grapple with the fastest-growing Ebola epidemic in history. The latest figures show 4,018 deaths among 8,300 recorded cases. The outbreak is on track to exceed the deadliest on record — West Africa’s 2014-2016 epidemic, which killed 11,000 people — according to the World Health Organization.
