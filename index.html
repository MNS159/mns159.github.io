Anthropic surges 27% in record $81B IPO, topping $2 trillion market cap on Nasdaq debut

Financial & Markets desk · anchored on Reuters

Anthropic, the artificial-intelligence company behind chatbot Claude, made a historic stock-market debut Thursday, raising $81.3 billion in the largest initial public offering on record. Shares opened at $111.50 on the Nasdaq and surged 27% in early trading, pushing the company’s market capitalization above $2 trillion. The IPO eclipsed SpaceX’s $75 billion listing in June and marked the first time a pure-play AI firm has sold shares to the public.
