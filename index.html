Russian drone attack on Dnipro train station kills at least 14

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

At least 14 people were killed and 14 others wounded, including an 11-year-old boy, when a Russian jet-powered drone struck the main railway station in the central Ukrainian city of Dnipro on Thursday, local officials said. The attack hit a crowded platform where a passenger train headed for Zaporizhzhia and a commuter train were standing, according to Ukrzaliznytsia, Ukraine's state railway operator. Dnipropetrovsk regional head Oleksandr Hanzha confirmed the death toll and injuries.
