Pennsylvania reports third measles-related death amid outbreak

Health & Science desk

Pennsylvania officials have reported the state's third measles-related death this year, the death of a 40-year-old unvaccinated woman in Jefferson County on Saturday. Jefferson County Coroner Greg Furlong announced the death in a Facebook post, calling it "measles-related." The woman was not identified; Furlong said no more information about the case would be released. The first two measles-related deaths in Pennsylvania occurred in August and involved infants, according to state and local officials.
