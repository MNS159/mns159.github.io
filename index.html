International & World Europe Migration

Ceuta crisis: EU calls for stronger borders as death toll nears 100

International & World desk

The European Union has called for stronger border security after more than 50,000 people, mostly young Moroccan men, surged into the Spanish enclave of Ceuta last week, leaving at least 72 dead and triggering a political crisis across the bloc. European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen wrote to Spanish Prime Minister Pedro Sanchez on Monday, saying border protection is a "shared European responsibility" and offering enhanced technical and financial support to Morocco, Spain's neighbour. EU interior ministers are scheduled to hold an emergency video conference on Tuesday to discuss the response.

Ceuta, a Spanish city on the northern coast of Africa, and the nearby enclave of Melilla are the last European-held territories on the African mainland. They have been in Spanish possession since the 15th and 16th centuries, but Morocco does not recognise Spain's sovereignty and has demanded their return since independence in 1956. The enclaves form part of the EU's external border and are subject to special Schengen travel rules. Most of the roughly 84,000 residents of Ceuta wish to remain part of Spain, according to public opinion polls cited by analysts.

Russia and Ukraine trade heavy attacks; Kyiv strikes Russian radar systems in Crimea

International & World desk

At least 17 people were killed as Russia and Ukraine exchanged heavy attacks on Monday, with Ukrainian drone strikes killing at least 14 in Russia and Russian strikes killing three in Ukraine, according to local officials. In Russia’s southern Krasnodar region, six people, including three children, were killed and nearly 40 wounded when drone debris struck the resort village of Arkhipo-Osipovka near Gelendzhik, Governor Veniamin Kondratyev said. In occupied Crimea, the death toll rose to four, with two others injured, according to Moscow-installed head Sergey Aksyonov. Acting Belgorod Governor Alexander Shuvaev reported four civilian deaths and 17 injuries across border settlements. In Ukraine, three people—including an emergency response worker and his eight-year-old son—were killed in a Russian strike on a petrol station in the Kryvyi Rih district of the Dnipropetrovsk region, regional Governor Oleksandr Hanzha said.

International & World Ethiopia

Hundreds flee northern Ethiopia clashes as fears of renewed civil war mount

Hundreds of Ethiopians from the Tigray region have crossed into neighboring Sudan after intense fighting erupted over the weekend between Tigrayan forces and the Ethiopian federal army near the border town of Shererina, raising fears that a three-year-old peace deal could unravel. The clashes, which began Saturday morning and continued into Sunday, involved heavy artillery and drones, according to a member of the Tigray People's Liberation Front (TPLF). The death toll was not immediately clear, but Sudanese medical and local sources reported that civilians and wounded fighters fled across the border, and witnesses on the Sudanese side heard explosions, heavy gunfire, and aircraft throughout the fighting.
