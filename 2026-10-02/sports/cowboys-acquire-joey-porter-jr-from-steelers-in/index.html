Cowboys acquire Joey Porter Jr. from Steelers in blockbuster trade

Sports desk

The Dallas Cowboys acquired cornerback Joey Porter Jr. from the Pittsburgh Steelers on Tuesday, sending a 2027 sixth-round pick and a 2028 second-round pick to Pittsburgh, according to ESPN's Todd Archer and Brooke Pryor. Porter, who has been sidelined with a back injury amid a contract dispute since training camp, will reunite with former Steelers teammates Broderick Jones and George Pickens in Dallas. The Steelers had reportedly been unwilling to offer Porter the extension he sought—widely believed to be in the range of $30 million annually, comparable to the recent deals signed by fellow 2023 cornerbacks Christian Gonzalez ($33.75 million per year) and Devon Witherspoon ($33 million).

The trade comes at a critical juncture for the Cowboys' secondary. Dallas has allowed seven passing touchdowns through three weeks, and cornerback DaRon Bland has surrendered a passer rating of 108.8 in coverage. Cobie Durant missed last week's game with a hamstring injury. Porter recorded 31 pass breakups, three interceptions, and a 57.2 passer rating in coverage over three seasons in Pittsburgh. He is expected to become the Cowboys' clear No. 1 boundary corner once his back injury allows him to take the field.

According to NFL insider Jordan Schultz, the Cowboys are not expected to sign Porter to a new deal, leaving his contract situation unresolved. The cornerback had been seeking an extension averaging around $30 million per year, as reported by Chris Mack of the Kaboly and Mack podcast, but Pittsburgh declined to meet that figure, citing a long-standing organizational policy against negotiating contracts during the regular season and reluctance to guarantee large sums. The Athletic's Mike DeFabo noted that Steelers general manager Omar Khan has a history of letting negotiations linger, and the team had instead extended players such as Keeanu Benton, Nick Herbig, Michael Pittman Jr., Jaylen Warren, and Darnell Washington this offseason.

The trade leaves Pittsburgh with a cornerback duo of Jamel Dean and Asante Samuel Jr. Dean has missed 12 games since 2023, while Samuel has allowed a passer rating of 135.4 and two touchdowns this season. The Steelers, who entered the deal with $11.6 million in cap space and a projected $9.2 million over the limit in 2027, received only draft capital for Porter—no player in return. Bleacher Report gave the Cowboys an 'A' grade for the trade, calling it a 'slam dunk move,' while assigning the Steelers a 'D,' calling the short-term move a 'major blunder.' Porter was graded a 'C,' with the potential benefit of playing for a higher-scoring offense but the same uncertain contract outlook.
