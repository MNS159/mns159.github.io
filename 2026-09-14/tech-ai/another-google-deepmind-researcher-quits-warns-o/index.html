Another Google DeepMind researcher quits, warns of AI risks

Tech / AI desk

Alex Turner, a former researcher at Google DeepMind, has publicly resigned and issued a stark warning about the dangers of artificial intelligence, saying the field is running an "extremely dangerous race" toward superintelligent AI. Turner, who defended a PhD dissertation titled "On Avoiding Power-Seeking by Artificial Intelligence" before joining the company, said he left at "significant financial cost" after Google broke its ethical commitments against supplying AI for military use. He now estimates the chance of an AI takeover at roughly one in three, a risk he says is high enough to justify urgent government action.
