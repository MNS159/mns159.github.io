Manchester City found guilty on 114 of 115 Premier League financial charges, reports say

Sports desk

The charges were filed in February 2023 after a four-year investigation, as first reported by The Athletic, and cover alleged breaches of the league's spending regulations between 2009 and 2018. No penalties have been determined and the club is expected to appeal.
