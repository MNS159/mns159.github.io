Pharma

Novo Nordisk inflammation drug fails heart disease trial, shares plunge

Health & Science desk · anchored on Reuters

Novo Nordisk said Friday that its investigational anti-inflammatory drug ziltivekimab failed to reduce the risk of major heart events in a pivotal Phase 3 trial, a setback to the Danish drugmaker's efforts to expand beyond its blockbuster obesity and diabetes treatments. The company's shares plunged as much as 9.3% to around $46.70, erasing more than $30 billion in market value and marking the stock's largest intraday loss since February. Novo Nordisk's market capitalization fell from $229.2 billion to $198.5 billion, a nearly 69% decline from its all-time high of $635.7 billion in June 2024.

FDA Expands Pluvicto Approval to PSMA-Positive Metastatic Hormone-Sensitive Prostate Cancer

Health & Science desk

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has approved lutetium Lu 177 vipivotide tetraxetan (Pluvicto, Novartis) in combination with an androgen receptor pathway inhibitor (ARPI) for adults with PSMA-positive metastatic androgen pathway modulation-naïve or -sensitive (mAPMN/S) prostate cancer, previously referred to as metastatic hormone-sensitive prostate cancer (mHSPC). The decision, announced July 31, 2026, expands the radioligand therapy's use from later-stage metastatic castration-resistant prostate cancer (mCRPC) into the first-line setting, roughly doubling the eligible patient population, according to clinicians and the manufacturer.

FDA faces deadline on Moderna's mRNA flu shot amid skepticism from HHS Secretary RFK Jr.

Health & Science desk

The Food and Drug Administration faces a Wednesday deadline to decide whether to approve Moderna's experimental mRNA-based flu shot, a decision that carries major implications for the future of mRNA research. The vaccine would be the first mRNA flu shot to reach the market if approved, but the process has been tumultuous: the FDA initially rejected Moderna's application in February before reversing course a little over a week later after the company agreed to add a confirmatory study if the vaccine is approved. In June, an FDA advisory panel recommended approval.

Nebraska begins Medicaid terminations under Trump work rules

Health & Science desk

Nebraska on Saturday will become the first state to terminate Medicaid coverage for people who failed to meet new work requirements signed into law by President Trump. State Medicaid Director Drew Gonshorowski said roughly 200 Nebraskans will lose their coverage on Aug. 1. 'We've ensured that we have extra steps in place making sure we review any denial multiple times before it goes out the door,' he told the nonprofit news outlet Tradeoffs. 'We don't want folks falling through the cracks on this.'
