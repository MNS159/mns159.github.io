MLB trade deadline: Skubal, Peralta headline active market as Aug. 3 cutoff nears

Sports desk

With the MLB trade deadline set for 6 p.m. ET on Monday, Aug. 3, the market is heating up around two-time defending AL Cy Young Award winner Tarik Skubal of the Detroit Tigers and New York Mets right-hander Freddy Peralta, who was scratched from his scheduled Friday start as trade talks intensified. The Mets cited protecting their asset as the prudent move, with interim manager Andy Green saying, “Once talks got to the level that they’re at, it just became the prudent path for us.” Peralta, a pending free agent, is expected to command a top-100 overall prospect in return, according to The Athletic, with the Braves, Cubs, Rays, and Phillies among potential suitors.

Skubal, in his walk year and owed the balance of a $32 million salary, appears to be the most impactful player available. The Dodgers, Brewers, and Rays are seen as favorites heading into the weekend, per multiple outlets. The Brewers are reportedly willing to take on Skubal’s remaining salary, while the Dodgers’ deep resources make them a natural fit. The impending move has the league on edge. Teammate Jake Rogers, who was designated for assignment July 28, reflected on their relationship: “I love Skoob to death … It’s all a business and they had to do it.” Rogers was Skubal’s longtime personal catcher, and the two met at the alternate site during the COVID-19 pandemic in 2020.

The Baltimore Orioles are keeping an open mind about trading catcher Adley Rutschman, who is on the injured list with left wrist inflammation but expected to begin a rehab assignment early next week. GM Mike Elias said, “We love him … but we have to be pretty open-minded here as we go into these (deadline) talks. Adley is one of the best things we have going for us.” The Yankees are among teams known to have interest in the three-time All-Star. Outfielder Taylor Ward, a free agent after the season, could also be available if the Orioles sell, with a market including the Yankees, Phillies, Rangers, and Guardians. Ward is hitting .248/.383/.426 with 20 doubles and seven homers.

Other notable names on the market include infielder Luis Arraez, as well as Cincinnati Reds right-hander Hunter Greene, who said trade rumors have not been a distraction: “Because I can’t do anything about it … I’m not on the business side, so….” The Kansas City Royals, despite a 46-64 record, are telling teams they are not rebuilding and intend to hold onto veteran pitchers Michael Wacha and Seth Lugo. The San Diego Padres, who are a game above .500, are likely to keep closer Mason Miller, according to ESPN, given his importance to their playoff hopes.

In other market developments, the Houston Astros have interest in St. Louis Cardinals outfielder Lars Nootbaar, a left-handed bat who is under team control through 2027. The Yankees continue to seek catching help. The trade deadline is 6 p.m. ET Monday.
