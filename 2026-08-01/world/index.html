US-Iran War

Trump threatens more strikes on Iran; Iran warns against 'flames of war'

International & World desk · anchored on AP

President Donald Trump threatened further U.S. military action against Iran on Friday, saying the United States will hit Iran “very hard” until it can no longer take it, while Iranian authorities warned that Washington is “fanning the flames of a full-scale regional war.” Speaking to reporters during a Cabinet meeting at Camp David, Trump said “we just want to win” in Iran and signaled that U.S. operations could continue for some time. White House press secretary Karoline Leavitt later accused Tehran of signing a memorandum of understanding on a truce last month, then quickly “broke it, shot at commercial ships, and killed American soldiers,” adding: “Iran will continue to pay until they come to the table in, what President Trump deems, a meaningful way.”

Europe Migration

Spain Ceuta border crisis: death toll rises to 67 as EU tensions flare

International & World desk

Spain has condemned the “selfish, polarising and unlawful” reaction of some EU countries to the mass influx of about 60,000 migrants from Morocco into its North African enclave of Ceuta, as officials confirmed the death toll had risen to at least 67. Spain’s Interior Minister Fernando Grande-Marlaska said on Saturday that virtually all of those who entered have now returned to Morocco, and the situation in Ceuta had almost returned to normal, with businesses reopened. The deaths included drownings and people crushed while trying to climb the breakwater that holds the border fence; additional casualties on the Moroccan side are possible.

Ukraine War

Trump backtracks from promise to let Ukraine build Patriot missiles

International & World desk

President Donald Trump has reversed course on his offer to let Ukraine manufacture its own Patriot air-defense interceptors, telling reporters at a cabinet meeting on Friday that the US has "not agreed to that" and that sharing the underlying technology is "a hard thing to give away." The retreat, which occurred during Trump's first cabinet meeting of his second term at Camp David, contradicted both Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's readout of their White House meeting earlier this week and Trump's own public pledge at a NATO summit in Turkey in early July, where he told Zelenskyy, "We're going to give a license to you to make Patriots."

Ukraine War

Ukraine strikes Crimea rail bridges and naval depot as power cuts leave families queueing for hot meals

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Ukrainian forces on Aug. 1 struck a series of military and logistical sites across Russian-occupied Crimea, including a naval drone storage depot, two railway bridges, and a Black Sea Fleet electronic intelligence unit, the General Staff of the Armed Forces of Ukraine reported. The targets included a depot near Chornomorske used for storing and maintaining unmanned surface vessels; the Syvash railway bridge near Chonhar, which connects Crimea to the Kherson region; and a railway bridge near Vladyslavivka. A repair and recovery base near Pervomaiske was also hit. The military said the bridges serve as vital logistics routes for moving personnel, weapons, and supplies between the peninsula and southern Ukraine. Beyond Crimea, strikes hit a logistics depot near Podove in the Kherson region and a drone storage facility near Tokmak in the Zaporizhzhia region.
