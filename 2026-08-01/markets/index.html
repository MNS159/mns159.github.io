Hedge Funds

Situational Awareness hedge fund implodes after momentum reversal triggers margin calls

Financial & Markets desk

Leopold Aschenbrenner's Situational Awareness hedge fund, which managed roughly $45 billion at its peak earlier this month, was forced into a sweeping reduction of its listed-stock positions after a historic momentum reversal triggered losses on both sides of its portfolio and set off margin calls and compulsory sales. By Thursday, after offloading all leveraged stock bets to Ken Griffin's Citadel at a discount, the fund's holdings plunged to around $10 billion, according to people with knowledge of the situation. The near-collapse coincides with Aschenbrenner's wedding, set for this weekend.

Central Banks

Three Fed officials dissent from rate hold, push for hike to combat inflation

Financial & Markets desk

Three Federal Reserve officials dissented this week against the decision to hold interest rates steady, arguing that the central bank should hike now to prevent inflation from becoming entrenched. Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack, Minneapolis Fed President Neel Kashkari, and Dallas Fed President Lorie Logan voted against keeping the Fed's key overnight borrowing rate in a range between 3.5% and 3.75%, where it has remained all year following three cuts in late 2025. The other nine voting members of the Federal Open Market Committee voted to hold rates.

Fed Chair Warsh Highlights Inflation Measurement Confusion, Names Task Forces to Review Gauges

Financial & Markets desk

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh concluded the central bank’s July 2026 policy meeting with a contentious 9-3 vote to hold interest rates steady, marking the most dissents in nearly a decade as three members pushed for a rate hike. The following day, the Fed’s preferred inflation gauge—the personal consumption expenditures (PCE) price index—showed inflation at 3.7% year over year in June, down from 4.1% in May but still well above the 2% target. Warsh emphasized that inflation must continue to fall and declared there is “no soft inflation target,” while declining to signal future rate moves.
