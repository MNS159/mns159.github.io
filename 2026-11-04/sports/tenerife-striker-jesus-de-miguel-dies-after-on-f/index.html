Tenerife striker Jesus de Miguel dies after on-field cardiac arrest; funeral set for Thursday

Sports desk

Fans have gathered to pay their respects to CD Tenerife striker Jesús de Miguel, who died on Tuesday after suffering a cardiac arrest during a Spanish second-division match on Monday. The cardiac arrest followed a violent collision with Andorra goalkeeper Jesús Owono near the halfway line. The burial is set for Thursday in Navahermosa, the family’s home municipality in the province of Toledo, broadcaster Cadena SER reported on Wednesday.

Owono was sent off for the foul and is being investigated by police. However, Tenerife’s chief prosecutor José Luis Sánchez-Jáuregui believes criminal proceedings over the incident are very unlikely, according to Spanish media. Owono also faces a long ban from the Spanish football federation (RFEF).

The players, coaching staff and club management of Tenerife will attend Thursday’s funeral. The club will hold a memorial service at the Heliodoro Rodríguez López Stadium on the Canary Island on Saturday, with all fans invited. Tenerife has declared a six-day mourning period and suspended all activities of its men’s, women’s and youth teams until the end of the week. The league match against Granada scheduled for Saturday has been postponed. Andorra also requested a postponement of their next match.

In his first public comments since the incident, Owono issued a statement through his club. “These are the hardest days of my life,” the 25-year-old Equatorial Guinea international said. “It is hard for me to find words, and it is very difficult for me to comprehend what has happened. The loss of Jesús pains me infinitely.” He added that many questions still need to be answered and that he would face everything that comes his way “with the utmost respect,” but said, “At the moment, I honestly can think of nothing else but Jesús and the pain we are all experiencing. Rest in peace, Jesús.”

De Miguel’s family has thanked people for their condolences but, according to the club, has asked fans “to fully respect their privacy at this terrible time.” He leaves behind his partner María and their daughter Carla, born in June 2025.
