iPhone Duo preorders open at $1,999, deliveries slip to 2027

Tech / AI desk

Apple opened preorders for its first foldable iPhone, the iPhone Duo, at 8AM ET on Friday — and delivery estimates slipped into 2027 within less than an hour. The phone starts at $1,999 for the 256GB model, and every configuration on Apple's website was showing late January delivery dates as of this writing.

The iPhone Duo was unveiled during Apple's "Surprise and shine" event alongside the iPhone 18 Pro and the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 models. It features a 5.4-inch outer screen and unfolds into a 7.6-inch Super Retina XDR display, with Apple Pencil support arriving later this year. Instead of Face ID, the Duo has a Touch ID sensor along its edge, and it carries an IP68 rating, Ceramic Shield 2 on its back, and Ceramic Shield on the front display. It runs on the same A20 Pro chipset as the iPhone 18 Pro and includes the same 48-megapixel fusion camera, although without variable aperture. The foldable comes in Star White and Night Sky and launches in stores on Friday, October 23rd, with unlocked preorders available from Apple and Best Buy.

The rapid delivery slip could be due to high demand for Apple's first foldable, low supply, or a combination of both. Apple typically reserves some units for in-store availability, so while online orders are delayed, shoppers might be able to get one sooner at an Apple Store. The Duo was announced in September and features the shorter passport-style design Samsung introduced earlier this year.

Carrier deals are available alongside Apple's preorder. AT&T is offering new and existing customers up to $1,200 off the iPhone Duo with a qualifying trade-in via 36 monthly bill credits; customers switching to AT&T can receive up to $800 through a reward card to help pay off their existing phone balance, and those activating a new eligible line online can save up to $200 per line. T-Mobile will provide up to $1,000 off with eligible trade-ins on an Experience Beyond or Go5G Next plan, or the same discount without a trade-in when adding a new line and switching from another carrier. Verizon is waiving its $40 activation fee for new customers who preorder, offering the phone at $55.55 per month over 36 months at 0% APR, or a $72.22-per-month Simplicity Pro package that bundles perks like two extra TravelPass days per month and allows upgrades up to two times within a rolling 12-month period.

Preorders remain open on Apple's website, but customers ordering now face a long wait before they get the device. The delivery delays underline both the anticipation for Apple's first foldable — one of the most highly awaited phones in years — and the possibility of supply constraints as the October 23rd launch approaches.
