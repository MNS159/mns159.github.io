Ukraine War

Russian strikes kill at least 5 in Ukraine as US envoys visit

International & World desk

Russian attacks killed at least five people and wounded five others at an industrial facility in the city of Kamianske in Ukraine’s Dnipropetrovsk region on Saturday, according to regional governor Oleksandr Hanzha. The strikes came as US special envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner arrived in Moscow for talks with Russian President Vladimir Putin before a planned visit to Kyiv on Sunday with a proposal to end the war, now in its fifth year.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said the strikes on Kamianske hit a civilian enterprise, and also noted “demonstrative Russian strikes on our airports overnight – in Kyiv and Boryspil.” He said these were “clearly a reaction to the discussions and preparations for the possibility of the US side using an aircraft to travel to Ukraine and land at Kyiv Airport or Boryspil Airport.” Zelenskyy added that Ukraine had “taken note of this Russian move, and next week we will adjust our operation accordingly regarding Russian airspace.” No comment was reported from the US envoys.

The attacks occurred despite announcements of a limited three-day ceasefire. Putin ordered a halt to strikes on Kyiv starting Saturday, and Ukraine’s army was ordered to cease fire for three days beginning at midnight Saturday. However, Russian attacks continued overnight, and Ukraine’s Armed Forces clarified they had not been required to order the temporary ceasefire on the front lines because Russia had not stopped its aggression. A separate local ceasefire was brokered by the International Atomic Energy Agency to enable repairs at the Zaporizhzhia nuclear power plant, which has been without external power since August 20.

In other regions, overnight Russian attacks injured three people in Odesa, including a 17-year-old boy; a 17-year-old girl in Mykolaiv; and two women in the Kyiv region hit by a strike on a residential building in Boryspil. Russian defence ministry said it struck two metallurgical plants it claimed were sites for military production. Separately, a 62-year-old man was wounded in the Nikopol district. In the Kharkiv and Bucha districts, other deadly Russian attacks occurred. The air war has intensified, with Russia increasingly using jet-powered drones that are harder to intercept. Civilian deaths in Ukraine have risen sharply in recent months.

After three hours of talks in Moscow, Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said Putin told the US envoys that Russia was making “real progress” on the battlefield and stressed the need to address “all the root causes of the conflict.” Ushakov said the Americans had “outlined a number of ideas regarding possible avenues for a settlement” but did not specify them. President Donald Trump had suggested the US had a fresh concrete proposal, but did not elaborate. Trump described a “personality problem” between Zelenskyy and Putin, saying they “truly hate each other.” Zelenskyy said Ukraine is prepared to refrain from strikes on Moscow through Monday. The envoys were due to meet Zelenskyy in Kyiv on Sunday, their first visit to Ukraine since Trump returned to power.
