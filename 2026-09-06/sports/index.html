Boxing

Katie Taylor retires after farewell win at Croke Park

Sports desk

Katie Taylor, the undisputed light-welterweight champion, retired from boxing after a unanimous decision victory over Flora Pili at a sold-out Croke Park in Dublin on Saturday. The 40-year-old Irishwoman finished her professional career with 26 wins from 27 fights, three undisputed championship reigns across two divisions, and an Olympic gold medal. Leclerc crashes out of Italian GP; Vettel drives Schumacher's Ferrari

Sports desk

Ferrari driver Charles Leclerc crashed out of the Italian Grand Prix at Monza on Sunday, bringing out a red flag after just two racing laps. College Football

Michigan Wins on Second-Chance Hail Mary After Clock Controversy Against Western Michigan

Sports desk

No. 16 Michigan defeated Western Michigan 13-12 on Saturday night on a Hail Mary touchdown pass from Bryce Underwood to JJ Buchanan as time expired. The win was overshadowed by a significant clock controversy after officials ruled there was one second remaining on the game clock following Underwood's first Hail Mary attempt, which sailed out of the back of the end zone and was touched by Western Michigan safety Micah Davis. Replays showed the NBC broadcast clock at zero when Davis made contact, but the Big Ten later issued a statement and video showing the official game clock still reading 0:01 at the moment of contact. Underwood's second Hail Mary, a rocket thrown as he was being hit, was caught by Buchanan in traffic for the winning score.

College Football

LSU crushes Clemson 51-10, deepening questions about Dabo Swinney's program

Sports desk

LSU opened its season with a dominant 51-10 victory over Clemson on Saturday night in Baton Rouge, handing the Tigers the worst loss of Dabo Swinney's tenure at Clemson. The blowout, which saw LSU outgain Clemson in first downs 38 to 8 and limit the visitors to 40 rushing yards, immediately renewed scrutiny over Swinney's resistance to modern college football trends and the decision to rehire Chad Morris as offensive coordinator. Clemson managed only 83 passing yards from quarterback Christopher Vizzina before being pulled before halftime, and the Tigers' offense finished with negative rushing yards at the break. Freshman Tait Reynolds also struggled, completing 4 of 6 passes for 22 yards and losing nine yards on the ground.
