FIFA President Infantino confirms re-election bid amid opposition

Sports desk

FIFA President Gianni Infantino will stand for re-election in 2027, the world football governing body confirmed on Sunday, pushing ahead with plans to seek a fourth term despite mounting opposition from European and other confederations over his scrapped proposal to sell stakes in FIFA competitions to private investors.

“The FIFA president announced in April that he will stand for re-election in 2027. Of course, nothing has changed. Mr Infantino will be standing for re-election,” a FIFA spokesperson said. The statement came a day after Infantino declined to answer questions about his future at the Women’s Under-20 World Cup in Katowice, Poland, saying, “There will be a lot to say, but there will be a right place and a right time to say this. It's not today, it's not here.”

Infantino, 56, has been FIFA president since 2016. His re-election had appeared a certainty as recently as mid-July, with more than 200 of the 211 member associations reportedly backing him. However, his plan to sell stakes in FIFA competitions—including the World Cup—to private investment firms sparked a global outcry. European football’s governing body, UEFA, threatened to boycott all FIFA events and is preparing a lawsuit in connection with the investor plans on suspicion of breach of fiduciary duty. No charges have been brought; Infantino insists he has not violated any law, and FIFA has accused UEFA of a “smear campaign.”

The Football Associations of England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland are among those to have publicly withdrawn support since the plan was announced. UEFA has abandoned the idea of a vote of no confidence, which would require 159 votes to change FIFA statutes—considered unrealistic. Instead, UEFA is working with other confederations to find a credible alternative candidate. UEFA president Aleksander Ceferin has said he is not available to challenge Infantino. Potential candidates include Concacaf president Victor Montagliani and Asian Football Confederation president Sheikh Salman bin Ibrahim Al Khalifa.

Candidates must receive the backing of at least 106 of the 211 FIFA member associations and register by November 18. The election will take place at the 77th FIFA Congress in Rabat, Morocco, on March 18, 2027. If re-elected, Infantino would serve until 2031.
