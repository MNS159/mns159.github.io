Google's Gemini hits 1 billion monthly users, fastest-growing product ever

Tech / AI desk

Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced today that the Gemini app has reached 1 billion monthly active users (MAUs), making it the 14th Google product to achieve the milestone and the fastest-growing product in the company's history. The figure refers specifically to users who open the Gemini app or visit the Gemini web interface to enter a prompt or access Gemini Live, and does not include users who encounter Gemini through other Google products such as Search, Gmail, or Drive. Google reported 750 million monthly users in February and 950 million on its Q2 2026 earnings call in late July; the company says it reached 1 billion last week before announcing it today.

Gemini's growth mirrors that of OpenAI's ChatGPT, which also recently surpassed 1 billion monthly users. OpenAI disclosed in an August 6 blog post that "more than 1 billion people are putting ChatGPT to work," though external data suggested ChatGPT crossed the threshold as early as June. OpenAI spokesperson Lindsay McCallum said ChatGPT hit 1 billion monthly users "some time ago" and reached 1 billion weekly active users in July. OpenAI had reported 900 million weekly active users in February, meaning ChatGPT's growth has slowed significantly from its earlier pace. Google spokesperson Alex Joseph confirmed Gemini's 1 billion MAU figure, while Anthropic does not disclose user data for its Claude chatbot, with estimates placing it in the tens of millions.

Josh Woodward, Google's vice president overseeing Gemini, provided additional usage details. He said 63 percent of Gemini's active users employ voice input, with a growing number being "voice-only" users. Of those using Gemini Live, 20 percent share their camera feeds and screens with the assistant. On the image-generation front, Gemini now produces more than 150 million images daily, all watermarked with Google's SynthID technology. Woodward noted that businesses use these images for marketing materials, though many are likely memes and jokes. Additionally, 38 percent of school-related requests include an attachment, prompting Google to develop new study tools for release in the coming weeks.

Gemini's user base is heavily skewed toward Android: Woodward posted that Gemini has more than 100 million active users on iOS, implying the vast majority of its 1 billion users are on Android. The Gemini app comes preinstalled on Android phones, a distribution advantage that underscores why OpenAI is reportedly interested in building its own hardware. Google has integrated Gemini across its product ecosystem, including Search's AI Mode and AI Overviews, Workspace tools, and the standalone app. The company continues to roll out new models and features, such as Gemini 3.5 Flash, which it says improves coding and autonomous AI-agent tasks. The announcement comes ahead of Google's Made by Google event, where more Gemini-powered features are expected to launch across Pixel devices.

The AI chatbot race has tightened significantly this year, with models from Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic consistently leapfrogging each other, and competition emerging from Chinese companies. While ChatGPT remains the most popular and well-known AI chatbot brand, its once commanding lead has largely evaporated. With both platforms now past the 1 billion-user mark, the focus shifts to finding ways to monetize those users and develop new use cases for the technology.
