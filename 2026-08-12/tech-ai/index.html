AI Products

Google's Gemini hits 1 billion monthly users, fastest-growing product ever

Tech / AI desk

Google CEO Sundar Pichai announced today that the Gemini app has reached 1 billion monthly active users (MAUs), making it the 14th Google product to achieve the milestone and the fastest-growing product in the company's history. The figure refers specifically to users who open the Gemini app or visit the Gemini web interface to enter a prompt or access Gemini Live, and does not include users who encounter Gemini through other Google products such as Search, Gmail, or Drive. Google reported 750 million monthly users in February and 950 million on its Q2 2026 earnings call in late July; the company says it reached 1 billion last week before announcing it today.

AI Funding

River AI, co-founded by xAI co-founder, raises $1.1B in seed/Series A from General Catalyst and others

Tech / AI desk

River AI, a two-month-old artificial intelligence startup co-founded by xAI co-founder Igor Babuschkin, has raised $1.1 billion in a seed and Series A funding round. The round was led by General Catalyst and AMP PBC, with participation from Nvidia, AMD Ventures, Y Combinator, and Temasek. River came out of stealth in June with a mission to reinvent AI from scratch, focusing on how models are trained in order to create personally trainable assistants rather than systems aimed at replacing human workers.
