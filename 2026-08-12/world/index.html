Disaster

Colombia earthquake death toll reaches 181 as rescuers race to find survivors

International & World desk

Rescuers in Colombia are working against the clock to free survivors trapped under rubble after a magnitude-7.4 earthquake struck western Colombia on Monday, killing at least 181 people and injuring nearly 2,600. President Abelardo de la Espriella, who was sworn in just days before the disaster, declared a state of emergency and said the government is deploying all its capabilities to protect lives and deliver aid. The quake hit at 7:34 a.m. local time on Monday, with its epicenter in the remote Chocó department, and was felt across a vast area including neighboring Ecuador, Venezuela and Panama. More than 70 aftershocks have been recorded since.

Middle East Conflict

US forces fire on ship breaching Iran blockade; Iran sets conditions for reopening Strait of Hormuz

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

The United States military fired two Hellfire missiles from a Navy MH-60 helicopter at a Panama-flagged cargo vessel in the Gulf of Oman on Tuesday, after the ship allegedly attempted to violate the US blockade of Iranian ports. Central Command said the strike disabled the vessel's steering gear and that no casualties were reported among the crew. The attack came as Iran reiterated that the Strait of Hormuz — through which one-fifth of the world's oil and natural gas is shipped during peacetime — will remain closed until the United States meets its demands, including the lifting of sanctions, the release of frozen Iranian assets, and compensation for attacks.

Ukraine War

Zelenskyy says Ukraine has sent proposals to US to end war; Russia uses North Korean missiles

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters, AP

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Tuesday that Kyiv has handed the United States new proposals aimed at ending the four-and-a-half-year war with Russia, while accusing Moscow of using North Korean-supplied ballistic missiles in a deadly strike on the southeastern city of Zaporizhzhia. In his nightly video address, Zelenskyy said, “We have conveyed our proposals to the American side,” but offered no details. He said the US could help strengthen Ukraine’s defenses, particularly air defenses, while increasing pressure on Moscow so that Russia prepares “for an end to the war rather than prolonging it.” US negotiators Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner were last in Moscow in January and have not yet visited Kyiv.

UNESCO: 2.4 million Afghan girls barred from school five years after Taliban takeover

International & World desk

An estimated 2.4 million Afghan girls remain excluded from secondary education five years after the Taliban returned to power, UNESCO said Tuesday, warning that the figure could reach nearly 4 million by 2030 if the ban remains in place. The number has risen by about 200,000 since last year, the U.N. agency reported, and represents a complete reversal of two decades of educational progress in Afghanistan.
