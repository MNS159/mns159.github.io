FDA approves Lilly's Mounjaro to reduce cardiovascular risk in adults with type 2 diabetes

Health & Science desk · anchored on Reuters

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration approved Eli Lilly's Mounjaro (tirzepatide) to reduce the risk of major cardiovascular events—including heart attack, stroke, and cardiovascular death—in adults with type 2 diabetes who are at high risk for these events. The approval makes Mounjaro the first dual GIP and GLP-1 receptor agonist proven to lower such risks, according to the company. The decision was based on results from the SURPASS-CVOT trial, which enrolled more than 13,000 participants across 30 countries over a median follow-up of about four years. In the head-to-head trial against Trulicity (dulaglutide), a GLP-1 drug with an established cardiovascular benefit, Mounjaro showed an 8 percent lower rate of the composite endpoint of cardiovascular death, heart attack, or stroke (MACE-3). The estimated hazard ratio was 0.92 (95.3% CI: 0.83, 1.01), demonstrating non-inferiority; superiority over Trulicity was not established.

AstraZeneca and Ionis Detail Surprise Failure of Heart Disease Silencer Drug, Raising Questions for Alnylam

Health & Science desk

AstraZeneca on Friday detailed the surprise failure of a closely watched Phase 3 clinical trial for its experimental heart disease drug, a silencer developed with Ionis Pharmaceuticals. The study found that the drug offered no additional benefit for patients with the progressive condition ATTR-CM (transthyretin amyloidosis with cardiomyopathy) who were already taking a different type of medicine called a stabilizer. The effect was so pronounced that it tanked the entire trial, according to STAT.

Measles outbreaks expand in Georgia and Pennsylvania; two unvaccinated individuals die in Pennsylvania

Health & Science desk

Measles outbreaks are accelerating in Georgia and Pennsylvania, with the Pennsylvania Department of Health reporting its first two measles-associated deaths in 35 years and Georgia confirming four new cases linked to a metro Atlanta daycare. Pennsylvania has recorded 424 measles cases this year, all in people who were not fully vaccinated, including 73 hospitalizations. Georgia’s total has risen to 11 cases in 2026, the second-highest in the state in the last decade. Nationally, more than 2,700 cases have been reported this year, the highest in recent history, putting the United States at risk of losing its official measles elimination status, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC).

FDA approves updated COVID-19 vaccines targeting XFG variant for fall 2026

Health & Science desk

The US Food and Drug Administration has approved updated COVID-19 vaccines from Moderna, Novavax-Sanofi, and Pfizer-BioNTech for the fall 2026 season, the companies announced Thursday. The vaccines are designed to prevent severe disease caused by the dominant XFG subvariant of the JN.1 coronavirus strain. The FDA approved the shots for adults 65 and older, as well as younger people with one or more chronic conditions that raise their risk of severe illness.
