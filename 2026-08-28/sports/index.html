Watkins apologises for refusing to play as Villa sell him to Al-Hilal; Jackson arrives

Sports desk

Aston Villa manager Unai Emery said Ollie Watkins apologised for refusing to play in Sunday's 4-0 defeat at Brighton as the striker prepares to complete a £51 million move to Saudi Pro League side Al-Hilal. Villa have agreed a deal to sign Chelsea forward Nicolas Jackson for up to £65 million as his replacement, making Jackson the club's record signing. Watkins, 30, is expected in Saudi Arabia on Friday to finalise the transfer, ending a six-year spell at Villa Park in which he scored 108 goals in 278 appearances, including a club-record 91 in the Premier League.

Soccer

Uefa’s US court filings accuse Infantino of ‘criminal mismanagement’ in escalating feud

Sports desk

Uefa has filed court documents in the United States that accuse Fifa president Gianni Infantino of “criminal mismanagement” and a “stunning dereliction of duty” over his now-abandoned plan to sell stakes in competitions to private investors. The papers, lodged to obtain evidence for potential criminal proceedings in Switzerland, describe the Fifa Forward Enterprise (FFE) plan as “a fraudulently off-market price promoted by Infantino for his own benefit” and claim the World Cup was assigned an “absurdly low enterprise value” of about £15bn. The filings request subpoenas against Fifa, venture capital firm Thrive Capital, and former Formula 1 chief Greg Maffei, seeking documents and deposition testimony related to the deal.
