Federal judge rules Pentagon illegally blacklisted Anthropic over AI ethics stance

Tech / AI desk

A federal judge ruled Thursday that the Trump administration illegally blacklisted artificial intelligence company Anthropic by designating it a supply-chain risk to national security in retaliation for the company's refusal to allow its AI technology to be used for lethal autonomous weapons and mass surveillance of Americans. U.S. District Judge Rita Lin of the Northern District of California granted key portions of Anthropic's motion for summary judgment, vacating government directives that barred federal agencies and defense contractors from using Anthropic's products and ordering the administration to rescind the measures.

CIA Uses AI to Generate Intelligence Reports Shared With Local Police

Tech / AI desk

The Central Intelligence Agency has been using artificial intelligence to write open-source intelligence analyses that are then distributed to local police through Department of Homeland Security fusion centers, according to documents obtained by Reason magazine. The reports, created by the CIA's Open Source Enterprise (OSE), were produced daily during this year's soccer World Cup covering security situations in Canada and Mexico. Reason obtained the documents through a Washington Public Records Act request for World Cup–related police bulletins.
