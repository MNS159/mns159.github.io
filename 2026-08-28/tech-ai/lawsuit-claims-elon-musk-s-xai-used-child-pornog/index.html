Lawsuit accuses Elon Musk's xAI of using child pornography to train Grok models

Tech / AI desk

Elon Musk's artificial intelligence company xAI has been accused in a lawsuit of training its Grok chatbot on child sexual abuse material (CSAM), as regulators and courts continue to probe the extent of the problem and some Grok users have been arrested. The complaint, filed Wednesday by a plaintiff identified as Jane Doe, alleges that xAI used CSAM depicting her as a preschool-age victim to train its AI models.

According to the complaint, Doe was repeatedly raped by adult men in the early 2000s to create CSAM that was sold to pedophiles online. Her images have since been hashed by organizations including the National Center for Missing and Exploited Children (NCMEC) and the Canadian Centre for Child Protection (CCCP). For her safety, Doe receives alerts from the U.S. Department of Justice Victim Notification System whenever she may be a victim in a new criminal investigation.

Doe said she was shocked when the CCCP notified her that it had identified AI-generated CSAM on xAI depicting her. The complaint alleges that messages were found on online forums "between offenders chatting about creating AI generated CSAM of Plaintiff and other similarly situated known, legacy, victims of CSAM." This re-traumatized Doe, who now fears that xAI has made it easier to produce more violative images of the most distressing time in her life.

The lawsuit further alleges that xAI has stored the images Grok generates and uses those outputs to further train the model. Doe believes that Grok has been trained on both the initial set of images that have haunted her for more than 20 years and the more recent AI-generated ones. The case adds to mounting legal challenges over deepfake content generated by Grok, as Musk's AI company has also sued some of its users.
