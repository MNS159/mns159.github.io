Report: Nvidia to acquire AI model repository Hugging Face for $12.9 billion

Tech / AI desk

Nvidia is reportedly moving forward to acquire Hugging Face, the cloud repository for open-source AI models, for $12.9 billion, according to a report from The Information citing one person with knowledge of the matter. Another source confirmed to CNBC that talks are underway. The deal is not finalized and could still run into trouble, but both parties appear to be actively pursuing it.

Hugging Face is a platform that hosts open-source AI models, benchmark evaluations, and data sets used for AI training and testing. Developers and researchers search for models, download and run them, and fine-tune them into variants that are then uploaded back to the repository. The company was founded in 2016. Nvidia had invested in Hugging Face in the past, as had Google and Microsoft. Hugging Face reportedly turned down a $500 million investment from Nvidia last year that would have valued it at $7 billion, because it did not want a single dominant investor. The company last raised money in 2023, when a $235 million round led by Salesforce Ventures valued it at $4.5 billion. Salesforce had also been among other companies trying to buy Hugging Face.

An acquisition would give Nvidia a major foothold in open-source AI at a time when open-source developers are increasingly closing the gap with closed systems from companies such as Anthropic and OpenAI. Nvidia and several other tech companies have publicly supported the role of open-weight models as alternatives to proprietary frontier models. Nvidia has released its own family of open models, called Nemotron.

The deal would also help Nvidia protect its dominant position in AI chips, which has been under threat as major tech peers build out their own chips to reduce reliance on Nvidia hardware. Those who download open-source models from Hugging Face need to host and run those models on their own computing infrastructure, which usually involves Nvidia’s GPUs. Hugging Face also uses Nvidia chips to run the paid hosting services it offers developers. An acquisition would mark a return for Nvidia to cloud computing, a business it reportedly scaled back roughly a year ago. Owning Hugging Face could also give Nvidia a way to offload unused cloud capacity from the computing deals it has guaranteed for customers.
