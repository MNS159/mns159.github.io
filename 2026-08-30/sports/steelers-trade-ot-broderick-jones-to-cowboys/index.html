Steelers trade OT Broderick Jones to Cowboys

Sports desk

The Dallas Cowboys have acquired offensive tackle Broderick Jones from the Pittsburgh Steelers in a trade that sends third- and sixth-round picks in the 2027 NFL Draft to Pittsburgh, along with a 2027 fourth-round pick going to Dallas, according to multiple reports. The deal was reported Saturday by ESPN's Adam Schefter and Jeremy Fowler and confirmed by the Cowboys on Sunday, ahead of the NFL's roster cutdown deadline.

Jones, the 14th overall pick in the 2023 NFL Draft out of Georgia, has played in 45 games with 38 starts over three seasons for the Steelers. He started 11 games in 2025 before suffering a neck injury in Week 12 against the Chicago Bears that ended his season. Jones underwent spinal fusion surgery in the offseason and was cleared for training camp, but he struggled in preseason action, including allowing two sacks in an Aug. 21 game against the New York Jets. According to Pro Football Focus, Jones has allowed 22 sacks in his career, fourth-most among tackles since 2023. In 2025, he surrendered six sacks and 23 pressures, per TruMedia, and earned a 57.7 overall PFF grade, ranking 70th among 89 qualified offensive tackles.

The Steelers declined Jones' fifth-year option earlier this offseason, making him an unrestricted free agent after the 2026 season. Pittsburgh also selected tackle Max Iheanachor with the 21st overall pick in the 2026 NFL Draft, further signaling a move away from Jones. The Cowboys, meanwhile, add a 25-year-old tackle who can play both left and right sides. Jones has a base salary of $1.5 million for 2026. In Dallas, he is expected to provide depth behind projected starters Tyler Guyton at left tackle and Terence Steele at right tackle, and could compete for the swing tackle role. Steele has allowed 16 sacks and 88 pressures over the past two seasons.

Cowboys head coach Brian Schottenheimer, when asked Friday about the team's swing tackle situation, said, "I think we feel good about our tackle group. I think I want to see the film, see how Nate did. Nate and Drew have been competing, but I want to see the film. But I thought we had a couple really good days." Cowboys owner and general manager Jerry Jones added, "We've seen some guys that we might make some shifting around if it called for it. But we've got a good plan. We'll see where we go from here, it's not over yet." The Cowboys also released wide receiver Denzel Mims on Saturday, according to Calvin Watkins of the Dallas Morning News.

The trade comes as the Cowboys deal with an injury to outside linebacker Marist Liufau, who suffered a fractured forearm requiring surgery, per Schottenheimer. The NFL roster cutdown deadline is Sunday at 6 p.m. ET. The Cowboys must decide how many offensive tackles to carry on their initial 53-man roster before their season opener Sept. 13 at the New York Giants. Trade grades from CBS Sports gave the Steelers a B and the Cowboys a B, noting the deal is an even swap that gives Pittsburgh future draft capital in a loaded 2027 class while Dallas adds cheap, experienced depth.
