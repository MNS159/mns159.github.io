Boxing

Hrgovic stops Itauma in ninth round to win vacant IBF heavyweight title

Sports desk

Filip Hrgovic, a heavy underdog, scored a ninth-round TKO of previously unbeaten Moses Itauma on Saturday at the O2 Arena in London to claim the vacant IBF heavyweight title. The referee waved off the fight as Itauma's corner threw in the towel. Itauma, 21, was taken to hospital afterward as a precaution, promoter Frank Warren said, adding that the Briton had also injured his leg.

Steelers trade OT Broderick Jones to Cowboys

Sports desk

The Dallas Cowboys have acquired offensive tackle Broderick Jones from the Pittsburgh Steelers in a trade that sends third- and sixth-round picks in the 2027 NFL Draft to Pittsburgh, along with a 2027 fourth-round pick going to Dallas, according to multiple reports. The deal was reported Saturday by ESPN's Adam Schefter and Jeremy Fowler and confirmed by the Cowboys on Sunday, ahead of the NFL's roster cutdown deadline.

MLB

Crow-Armstrong hits three homers, bolsters NL MVP case

Sports desk

Chicago Cubs center fielder Pete Crow-Armstrong delivered a three-homer, six-RBI performance in a 15-5 win over the Cincinnati Reds at Wrigley Field on Saturday, strengthening his case for the National League MVP award. Crow-Armstrong went 4-for-5 with three home runs, becoming the first Cubs player to record at least four hits, three home runs, and six RBI in a game at Wrigley since George Mitterwald in 1974. He had a chance for a fourth home run in the eighth inning against Reds position player Jose Trevino, who was pitching, but grounded out on a 58.1 mph knuckleball.
