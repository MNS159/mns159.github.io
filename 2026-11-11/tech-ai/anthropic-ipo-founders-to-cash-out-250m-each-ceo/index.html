Anthropic Founders to Cash Out $250M Each in IPO; CEO Predicts Cancer Cure in 10 Years

Tech / AI desk

Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company founded by former OpenAI employees, has disclosed in its S-1 filing that each of its seven co-founders plans to sell approximately 3.38 million shares in its upcoming initial public offering. At the midrange of the company's unusually broad initial price range of $70 to $78 per share, each founder would net just shy of $250 million. At the low end, each would pocket just over $236 million; at the high end, just under $264 million. The IPO could value Anthropic at up to $1.4 trillion, with bankers reportedly suggesting the valuation could rise to $1.5 trillion due to excess demand.

The seven co-founders are CEO Dario Amodei; President and Chair of the Board Daniela Amodei; Chief Compute Officer Tom Brown; Head of Public Benefit Jack Clark; Chief Science Officer Jared Kaplan; Chief Architect Sam McCandlish; and Interpretability Research Lead Christopher Olah. After the sale, each will own about 3.2% of the outstanding Class A shares, or roughly 22% collectively. However, other holders, including Amazon and Google, hold nonvoting Class N shares, so the founders' stake in total equity will be considerably smaller. Each founder will also receive one share of Class F stock with supervoting rights, giving the group 51.1% of the votes, with no single founder holding significantly more shares or votes than the others.

The S-1 filing hints at strong demand: board member Reed Hastings has indicated he wants to buy $300 million worth of shares through his donor-advised fund, and board member Christopher Liddell wants to buy $3 million worth. The seven founders left OpenAI in 2021 after disagreements over how AI should be built, forming Anthropic with a stated focus on AI safety. Siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei had held senior research and policy roles at OpenAI, joined by five fellow OpenAI colleagues.

The company has recently released new models, Mythos and Fable 5.2, with Fable built with strong cybersecurity and biology safeguards against misuse, following extensive prerelease safety testing. Anthropic has hired David LaBianca, formerly a managing director and chief cybersecurity architect at Goldman Sachs and later a senior director of security engineering at Google, as a member of technical staff working on security engineering. The hire aligns with Anthropic's historic focus on AI safety.

Meanwhile, the traffic between finance and AI is not entirely one-way: Nataniel Ruiz, a PhD computer scientist and DeepMind researcher focused on generative photo and video models, has left Google to join Citadel's equity quantitative research team. Anthropic, Google, and Citadel declined to comment on the moves. Anthropic's IPO filing marks a significant milestone for the company, which was valued closer to $18 billion in 2024, and employees who joined earlier may now see substantial payouts.
