Pochettino ready to accept Man Utd offer to replace Carrick

Sports desk

Mauricio Pochettino is ready to accept an offer from Manchester United to become their next head coach, with the club having opened talks with the Argentine about replacing Michael Carrick, who is expected to be relieved of his duties in the next 48 hours. Sources have told BBC Sport that USA manager Pochettino wants to take the job once Carrick's future is clarified. US Soccer officials are expecting an official approach from United and have a contingency plan for their manager's departure, including a succession plan. United would be required to pay $10m (£7.5m) to release Pochettino from his contract, which he signed in August.

Carrick's future at United will be decided at a meeting in Monaco on Wednesday attended by chief executive Omar Berrada, director of football Jason Wilcox and co-owner Sir Jim Ratcliffe. Berrada and Wilcox have flown to Monaco for one of their regular, planned meetings to discuss strategy, with the main issue being Carrick's future and what should happen next. It is expected that Berrada and Wilcox's recommendation is for the head coach to go. Carrick, 45, was pictured arriving at the club's Carrington training base on Wednesday. United are 18th in the Premier League, inside the relegation zone, after winning just one of their first 10 games and none since August. It is the club's lowest position at this stage of a top-flight season since 1986-87, when they were 20th.

Pochettino, 54, is understood to view the prospect of managing United as a serious opportunity, with the expectation he would accept the job were it formally offered. United have spoken to the former Argentina defender about their managerial position on three previous occasions: when Jose Mourinho was sacked in 2018, in 2022 before the club appointed Erik ten Hag, and again when Ten Hag was sacked in 2024. Pochettino's first Premier League job was at Southampton from January 2013, followed by a five-year stint at Tottenham from 2014 that included a Champions League final appearance. He managed Paris St-Germain from January 2021 to July 2022 before spending the 2023-24 season in charge of Chelsea. He moved into international football by taking the USA job in September 2024 and led the co-hosts to the last 16 of the 2026 World Cup.

The USA have a two-legged Concacaf Nations League quarter-final against Haiti over the next week. With confidence over a succession plan, it raises the possibility that Pochettino may not take charge of those games. On Thursday, US Soccer cancelled planned media with players, citing a clash with Veterans Day, though some reports claimed it was to stop the squad being asked about their manager. Pochettino wants to remain respectful to the US Soccer Federation and will wait for United to make their approach.

Pochettino and United go back a long way, with his name associated with the club since he was spotted leaving a lunch appointment with Sir Alex Ferguson in 2016. Ferguson was three years into his retirement, while Pochettino was building his reputation at Tottenham. Pochettino called it 'a meeting in London between colleagues, between a genius and an apprentice.' He was mentioned as a contender to succeed Louis van Gaal when he was sacked by United in May 2016, then when Mourinho suffered the same fate in December 2018. Pochettino was the other name on United's shortlist when Ten Hag was appointed in April 2022, then again when the Dutchman was shown the door in October 2024.

While it may seem a touch immoral for Manchester United to be talking to a potential new manager before they have decided if their current one is staying or not, this kind of situation is not uncommon in football. Jose Mourinho had agreed a contract with United in 2016 before Louis van Gaal was sacked, which is how stories came out that the Dutchman would be dismissed as he was celebrating the FA Cup final triumph over Crystal Palace. Going further back, Mark Hughes was replaced immediately by Roberto Mancini at Manchester City in 2009, with Hughes' imminent dismissal reported on the morning of his final game in charge.
