Thai Teenager Kills 8 in School Shooting, Including Grandparents, Police Say

International & World desk

A 14-year-old student killed his grandparents and then opened fire at a high school in Nonthaburi province, outside Bangkok, on Friday, leaving at least eight people dead and more than 20 injured, police said. The gunman died by suicide after the attack, according to authorities, who reported the shooting at the Debsirin Nonthaburi school in the Bang Kruai district.

Police said the teenager fired at least 26 bullets using a gun belonging to his grandfather and was found with 34 additional rounds. The death toll includes the suspected attacker, his two grandparents, and at least five teachers, according to officials. Law enforcement said three teachers and three students were killed; the deputy interior minister later stated that five teachers died. The health department reported 22 people wounded, with nine in critical condition. Emergency workers described treating students with injuries to the back, chest and arms, and finding a male teacher dead on an upper floor and a female teacher with chest and arm wounds.

A witness told reporters that students hid in a classroom after hearing gunshots from another building before police knocked on the door and escorted them to safety. Images from the scene about an hour after the attack showed people outside comforting one another while police and first responders remained inside the school, which appeared to have been secured. The motive remained under investigation, police said. The school, part of the Debsirin network founded by the Thai royal family, had about 3,100 students and 147 teachers in the 2025 academic year.

Thailand has the highest rate of civilian gun ownership in Southeast Asia, and the Small Arms Survey in 2017 ranked it 13th globally for small gun possession. The country has experienced several deadly shootings in recent years, including a 2022 attack in which a former police officer killed 36 people, 22 of them children, at a daycare center. In February 2025, a 17-year-old stole a police gun and opened fire at a high school in southern Thailand, killing one person. Prime Minister Anutin Charnvirakul offered condolences and ordered officials to care for the victims.
