Natural Disasters

7.7-magnitude earthquake kills at least 38 in Indonesia

International & World desk

A powerful 7.7-magnitude earthquake struck off the coast of Indonesia's Flores region early Saturday, killing at least 38 people, collapsing buildings and triggering landslides, the country's disaster mitigation agency said. The shallow quake, with a depth of 10 to 15 kilometers, hit at about 5 a.m. local time (22:00 GMT Friday) about 68 kilometers north-northwest of the city of Ende in East Nusa Tenggara province, according to Indonesian and U.S. geological agencies. Dozens of aftershocks followed, including one of magnitude 6.1. A tsunami warning was issued but lifted about three hours later when no significant sea-level rise was detected, though tsunami waves of less than one meter were recorded in several areas.

Israeli strike kills seven in southern Lebanon in deadliest attack since June

International & World desk · anchored on AP

An Israeli air strike on a family home in the village of Ansar in southern Lebanon early Saturday killed seven people, including two women and three children, and wounded three others, according to Lebanese state media. The strike, which destroyed the home, was the deadliest reported Israeli attack in Lebanon since agreements reached in June led to a significant reduction in hostilities between Israel and Hezbollah.

Ukraine War

Ukraine war: Kyiv advances in southeast, Russia claims settlements; Patriot missile shortage looms

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky announced on Saturday that Kyiv had struck a Russian rocket and space centre in Samara using Flamingo missiles, as well as the Savasleyka military airfield, a base for Russian fighter jets. Zelensky described the strikes as 'new significant results' from deep operations, noting the Samara target was approximately 900 kilometres from Ukraine's border. 'Our plan of long-range sanctions against Russia for this war is being implemented, and it is important that Russia's war potential be reduced,' he said. Local authorities in Samara confirmed that industrial infrastructure had sustained 'localised damage' after what they described as a 'massive attack' that was repelled by air defences.

Ebola outbreak in DR Congo spreads to sixth province, kills 2,000

International & World desk

The Ebola virus is spreading at a record pace in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, with the outbreak reaching a sixth province and killing 2,000 people out of more than 4,300 recorded cases since it was officially declared on May 15, according to health authorities and the World Health Organization. The WHO has warned that the current outbreak, driven by the rare Bundibugyo strain for which no approved vaccine or treatment exists, could surpass the 2014–2016 West African epidemic that killed 11,000 people. The WHO expects the outbreak to peak in six months.
