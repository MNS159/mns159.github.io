UFC 330: Makhachev defends welterweight title against Machado Garry in Philadelphia

Sports desk

Islam Makhachev makes the first defence of his welterweight title against Ian Machado Garry at UFC 330 on Saturday at Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia. The main event is expected to begin around 10:30 p.m. ET, following a four-fight main card that starts at 9 p.m. ET on Paramount+, with prelims at 7 p.m. ET and early prelims at 5:30 p.m. ET. Both fighters weighed 170 lb (12 st 2 lb) for the contest.

Makhachev, 34, holds a professional record of 28 wins and one loss, with five wins by knockout, 13 by submission and 10 by decision. He is on a 16-bout winning streak and is looking to break Anderson Silva's record of 16 consecutive UFC victories. In his most recent bout, he claimed the welterweight championship with a unanimous decision win over Jack Della Maddalena at UFC 322 in December 2025. Makhachev is a three-time Fight of the Night and three-time Performance of the Night recipient, and was the CBS Sports 2023 MMA Fighter of the Year. He suffered his lone defeat to Adriano Martins by knockout at UFC 192 on Oct. 3, 2015.

Machado Garry, 28, from Ireland, is 17-1 overall with seven wins by knockout, one by submission and nine by decision. He has won 10 of his 11 UFC fights since his debut in 2021, with his sole loss coming via unanimous decision to Shavkat Rakhmonov at UFC 310 on Dec. 7, 2024. Since that defeat, he has bounced back with consecutive unanimous-decision wins against Carlos Prates and Belal Muhammad. Machado Garry is a former Cage Warriors welterweight champion and a one-time Performance of the Night winner. At the weigh-ins, he told booing fans they "should be grateful" about watching "two of the biggest fighters ever in this sport."

Makhachev described Machado Garry as his "next target" at the weigh-ins, telling supporters he is "ready to make all people happy" with victory. A wide range of UFC fighters offered predictions for the bout. Among those picking Makhachev are Tom Aspinall, Alexander Volkanovski, Daniel Rodriguez, Brandon Moreno, Jorge Masvidal, Sean Brady and Matt Brown. Picking Machado Garry are Carlos Prates, Sean O'Malley and Mauricio Ruffy. Georges St-Pierre gave a neutral assessment, saying Machado Garry has an edge on the feet but Makhachev has an edge on the ground. Makhachev is listed as a -365 favorite at DraftKings Sportsbook, with Machado Garry at +285.

In the co-main event, Mackenzie Dern defends her strawweight title against Gillian Robertson. The full main card also includes Mansur Abdul-Malik vs. Dustin Stoltzfus and Edson Barboza vs. Esteban Ribovics. The preliminary card features Joel Alvarez vs. Chidi Njokuani, Kaue Fernandes vs. Jalin Turner, and Donte Johnson vs. Eric McConico. Early prelims include Tresean Gore vs. Vicente Luque, Lucas Fernando vs. Rafael Tobias, Ramiz Brahimaj vs. Neil Magny, and Myktybek Orolbai vs. Jeremiah Wells.
