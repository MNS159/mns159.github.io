AI Competition

OpenAI and Anthropic slash prices as Chinese AI rivals gain ground

Tech / AI desk

Leading US AI labs OpenAI and Anthropic have introduced steep price cuts on their newest models as cost-conscious customers increasingly turn to cheaper alternatives from Chinese competitors. OpenAI said it is cutting prices for GPT-5.6 Luna, its fastest and most affordable model, by 80 percent, while Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5 at half the price of its most capable model, Fable 5. According to Silicon Data’s token price index, the overall cost that customers pay for models from top US labs has fallen nearly a quarter since mid-July.

Tech / AI desk

A high-severity vulnerability in macOS that could give attackers full remote control of affected systems is being actively exploited in the wild, Dutch cybersecurity authorities warned this week. Tracked as CVE-2026-65400, the flaw carries a severity rating of 7.1 out of 10 and stems from a bug in the macOS screen sharing feature, which allows a remote party to view the screen and control the keyboard and mouse while the machine is on.

Cybersecurity

US water utilities hit by coordinated hacks; Iran suspected

Tech / AI desk

Since late July, U.S. water utilities have been hit by a wave of cyberattacks that authorities say appears to have affected facilities in around a dozen states. On July 28, Minnesota announced that water treatment plants in more than 30 communities were targeted in coordinated attacks. Two days later, the FBI said water and wastewater utilities in at least seven states reported incidents, and that in some cases the attacks “degraded water operations.” The U.S. has more than 150,000 water systems, many run by local companies that may lack the resources or cybersecurity expertise to defend themselves.
