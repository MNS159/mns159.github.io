International & World Ukraine War

Russia and Ukraine exchange massive aerial attacks, killing at least 9

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Russia and Ukraine launched large-scale aerial attacks against each other overnight, killing at least nine people across both countries, officials said Sunday. In one of Ukraine's largest drone offensives of the war, Russia's Defense Ministry reported destroying 822 Ukrainian drones nationwide. Moscow Mayor Sergei Sobyanin said about 600 drones were detected heading toward the capital, with roughly a third destroyed over the Moscow region. An 83-year-old man was killed when a drone hit a private home in the Ramensky district, local Gov. Andrey Vorobyov said. In Russia's southwestern Rostov region, a drone attack killed five people, damaged homes and a railway station, and sparked a forest fire, Gov. Yury Slyusar said. A woman was also killed in the Belgorod region after a bus was struck, Gov. Vyacheslav Gladkov said.

In Ukraine, Russian strikes killed at least five people and wounded more than 10, officials said. In Kryvyi Rih, President Volodymyr Zelensky's hometown, a 44-year-old man was found dead in rubble and 14 steel plant workers were wounded. Two people in their 60s were killed when a house was destroyed in Zaporizhzhia, and one person was killed in Sumy, Zelensky said. In Kyiv, air-raid sirens sounded four times since Saturday night; at least six people, including a child, were injured, Mayor Vitali Klitschko said. A book market in the city's Pochayna district was damaged, with burnt books scattered on the ground. Vinyl store owner Luba, 42, said she lost 500,000 hryvnias (£8,262) worth of stock, telling the BBC: "This business was my baby — many of the vinyls destroyed were rare and irreplaceable."

North Korea Plans to Send Up to 50,000 More Troops to Russia, Intelligence Says

International & World desk

Intelligence agencies report that North Korea is preparing to send an additional 30,000 to 50,000 troops to Russia, according to Ukrainian and international officials. The deployment would supplement an estimated 14,000 to 15,000 North Korean troops already sent to help defend Russia's Kursk region since late 2024. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy has warned that North Korea's improved combat and weapons experience could pose a security threat to both Ukraine and the Asia-Pacific region.

Israeli airstrikes kill 11 in southern Lebanon; deadliest since June ceasefire

International & World desk

At least 11 people were killed in Israeli airstrikes in southern Lebanon on Saturday, in the deadliest day of attacks since a US-brokered ceasefire framework was announced in June, according to Lebanese authorities. The strikes hit two villages: a dawn raid on the outskirts of Ansar killed seven people, including three children and two women, and a later strike on Deir al-Zahrani killed four others and wounded others, the Lebanese Health Ministry reported.
