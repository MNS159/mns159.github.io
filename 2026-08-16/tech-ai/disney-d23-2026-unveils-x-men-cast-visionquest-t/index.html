Disney D23 2026 unveils X-Men cast, VisionQuest trailer, Ahsoka S2 teaser

Tech / AI desk

Disney's D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event in Anaheim, California, kicked off its first day with major announcements for Marvel and Star Wars, including the cast for the MCU's untitled X-Men film, the first trailer for the Disney+ series VisionQuest, and the first teaser for Ahsoka season 2. The X-Men movie is scheduled for release on May 5, 2028. VisionQuest premieres on October 14, and Ahsoka season 2 debuts on January 20, 2027.

Marvel Studios revealed the cast for its upcoming X-Men film, which will feature Sadie Sink as Jean Grey (previously revealed in Spider-Man: Brand New Day), Kit Connor as Scott Summers (Cyclops), Christopher Abbott as Charles Xavier (Professor X), Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, Maya Boyd as Storm, and Inde Navarrette as Rogue. Adam Driver will play the villain Nathaniel Milbury, AKA Mister Sinister. The film is set to hit theaters on May 5, 2028, though the date may shift.

The first trailer for VisionQuest, completing a trilogy that began with WandaVision and continued with Agatha All Along, was released. Paul Bettany returns as Vision, and James Spader reprises his role as Ultron. The official premise states that Vision, rebooted and having escaped those who sought to weaponize him, has been in hiding. He consults AI personas in his programming, including F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S., and Ultron, and goes on the run with a mysterious boy named Thomas Shepard, who may be Vision's son reincarnate. Ruaridh Mollica has been cast as Tommy/Thomas Shepherd, continuing a storyline from Agatha All Along. Bettany has said the series will explore "intergenerational trauma" with fathers and sons.

For Star Wars, Lucasfilm dropped the first teaser for Ahsoka season 2, showing a darker, witchcraft-filled take on the galaxy, with Grand Admiral Thrawn threatening to thrust the galaxy back into large-scale war after the fall of the Empire. Ryan Gosling also took the stage to tease the upcoming film Star Wars: Starfighter, though the footage shown was exclusive to attendees and has not been officially released online. The film is scheduled for release on May 28, 2027, and stars Gosling alongside Matt Smith, Aaron Pierre, and Mia Goth.

Disney also showed a new trailer for Avengers: Doomsday, featuring Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom, with Kevin Feige, Downey, Chris Evans, and Hayley Atwell present. The film is scheduled for release on December 18. Additionally, Matt Groening hinted that the cult classic game The Simpsons: Hit & Run may be returning in some form, though showrunner Matt Selman quickly added "or not." The original 2003 game is not available on modern consoles or backward compatibility lists, leading to speculation about a possible HD remaster or direct port.

Other announcements included a brief glimpse of the Pixar feature Ghost Market, set for March 2028, which centers on a lunch shop on Oʻahu that serves lost spirits. Disney and Square Enix announced a Kingdom Hearts anime series with an all-new storyline, with game series creator Tetsuya Nomura involved. A teaser for the Bluey Movie showed the series transitioning from 2D to 3D computer animation for its theatrical release.

Sources: Ars Technica·The Verge
