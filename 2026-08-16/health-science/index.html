Common food preservative linked to rising suicide deaths among young people in UK

Health & Science desk

A chemical widely used to preserve food—sodium nitrite—has been linked to a growing number of suicide deaths in the United Kingdom, with young people and men accounting for a disproportionate share of cases, according to a study published August 15, 2026, in BMJ Public Health. Researchers analyzed postmortem samples submitted to the country's primary laboratory for nitrite and nitrate testing between March 2019 and August 2024. Of 164 cases where coroners approved data use, the average age was 28; ages ranged from 14 to 74 for males and 17 to 82 for females.

31 dead, nearly 600 ill from lung disease linked to stone countertop fabrication, California study finds

Health & Science desk

A new public health report from California has identified 592 cases of silicosis — a deadly, incurable lung disease — among workers who fabricated engineered-stone countertops, including 31 deaths and 65 people who required lung transplants. The findings, published Wednesday in NEJM Evidence by the California Department of Public Health, cover cases identified from January 2019 through June 2026 and highlight the severe toll of exposure to crystalline silica in the countertop industry.
