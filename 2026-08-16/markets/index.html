Markets reverse Fed tightening signal, easing to 30-year extreme after Warsh comments

Financial & Markets desk

Wall Street has reversed the tightening in financial conditions that Federal Reserve Chairman Kevin Warsh had credited to markets, with a Bloomberg gauge of US market conditions rising Thursday to its easiest level since 1996. The reversal comes just two weeks after Warsh said markets had done "quite a bit" of the Fed's tightening, and it has pushed the measure to a 30-year extreme even as the Fed's benchmark rate has stayed unchanged at 3.5% to 3.75%.
