Makhachev defeats Garry at UFC 330, breaks UFC win record

Sports desk

Islam Makhachev cemented his status as the pound-for-pound best fighter in the world by defeating Ian Machado Garry via unanimous decision at UFC 330 in Philadelphia on Saturday, defending his welterweight title and breaking Anderson Silva's 13-year record for consecutive UFC wins with his 17th straight victory. The 34-year-old Russian, a former lightweight champion, improved to 29-1 while Machado Garry (17-2) suffered his second career loss. The judges scored the bout 49-46 twice and 48-47.

Makhachev dominated the grappling exchanges, securing seven unanswered takedowns and accumulating over 12 minutes of control time compared to Machado Garry's 34 seconds. A head kick in the second round dropped the Irishman, who survived but could not generate enough offense to sway the judges; Machado Garry landed 29 significant strikes to Makhachev's 22. "Ian is a great fighter, good man and I respect him. He gave me a hard time but I still prove every fight," Makhachev said afterward. Machado Garry apologized to fans in Ireland and vowed to return: "I stand here as a man proud of what I've done, proud of where I came from and proud where this will take me."

Despite the record, CBS Sports reported that UFC CEO Dana White criticized Machado Garry for lacking "even an ounce of killer instinct," noting that the challenger's defensive approach and mid-fight embrace with Makhachev at the start of the final round may have cost him a chance at victory. White said Machado Garry's defense was so strong that had he taken more risks, he might have won. The analysis also noted that Makhachev, while victorious, looked more human than in previous fights, and that the welterweight division may now see potential threats in Carlos Prates and Michael Morales.

In the co-main event, American-Brazilian Mackenzie Dern made the first defense of her women's strawweight title by defeating Canada's Gillian Robertson via unanimous decision, winning the majority of grappling exchanges and securing her fourth straight victory. Dern, 33, came close to finishing Robertson multiple times and showed improved wrestling. "I'm still the champion," Dern said. The victory was seen as a breakthrough performance that solidifies her as a true champion, though a potential return of former champion Weili Zhang looms.

Also announced at UFC 330: Australia's Alexander Volkanovski will defend his featherweight title against Russia's Movsar Evloev at UFC 333 in Abu Dhabi on 24 October, with the co-main event featuring Russia's Petr Yan defending his bantamweight belt against Georgian former champion Merab Dvalishvili. Additional bouts include England's Lone'er Kavanagh vs. Uzbekistan's Ramazan Tamirov in a flyweight contest, and English featherweight Arnold Allen vs. American Aaron Pico. On the preliminary card, Jalin Turner knocked out Kaue Fernandes in 39 seconds, continuing his resurgence after a brief retirement.
