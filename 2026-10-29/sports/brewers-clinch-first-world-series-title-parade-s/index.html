Brewers clinch World Series title; Milwaukee-area businesses offer free food and drink

Sports desk

The Milwaukee Brewers defeated the Tampa Bay Rays 4-1 in Game 5 of the World Series on Wednesday, Oct. 28, 2026, at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg, Florida, clinching the championship. After the final out, the team celebrated in the clubhouse with the Commissioner's Trophy, with players including Jackson Chourio, Jacob Misiorowski, Cooper Pratt, Brandon Woodruff, Sal Frelick, Christian Yelich and Chad Patrick joining the festivities.

Milwaukee took control in the second inning, when Brice Turang doubled off Rays starter Drew Rasmussen and Sal Frelick was hit by a pitch. Tampa Bay third baseman Junior Caminero singled off Brewers pitcher Jacob Misiorowski, who worked the early innings at Tropicana Field, where empty seats were visible during the first inning. The Rays never got closer, and the Brewers closed out the 4-1 victory to end the series.

Back in Wisconsin, Milwaukee-area businesses are joining the celebration. Milwaukee Brat House, at 1013 N. King Drive, promised free bratwursts before the clincher and plans to make good on that offer, though details have not yet been shared. Georgie Porgie's, the fast-casual diner in Oak Creek and Racine, is giving away free junior-size Brew Moon shakes all day Thursday, Oct. 29, while supplies last, with no purchase necessary. Brew Moon is the Brewers' postseason special: blue moon frozen custard topped with whipped cream and blue and yellow sprinkles.

Tally's Tap & Eatery at S31W24661 W. Sunset Drive in Waukesha will host a Free Beer Day on Monday, Nov. 2, offering free Miller Lite draft beer starting at noon while supplies last, fulfilling the promise on its exterior sign: "If the Brewers win it all, free beer for y'all." Thursday is Victory Thursday in Milwaukee, where fans marked the title at an American Family Field watch party during Game 5, and more deals could still be coming.
