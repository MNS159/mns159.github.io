Commanders QB Jayden Daniels Dislocates Left Elbow; Quinn to Provide Update Monday

Sports desk

Washington Commanders quarterback Jayden Daniels suffered a dislocated left elbow during Sunday's game against the Dallas Cowboys, head coach Dan Quinn confirmed after the game. X-rays showed no fracture, and the team plans further imaging once back in Virginia to determine the severity of the injury. Quinn is scheduled to provide an update on Daniels' condition Monday afternoon.

The injury occurred when an offensive lineman stepped on Daniels' foot, causing him to brace himself with his left arm. His elbow folded inward on the play. Daniels was able to throw the ball away but could not leave the field under his own power and was later carted off. It is the same elbow Daniels injured last season.

Daniels, 25, has been frequently sidelined during his NFL career despite playing all 17 games and the playoffs as a rookie in 2024. In 2025, he missed Weeks 3–4 (knee), Week 8 (hamstring), Weeks 10–13 (left elbow), and Weeks 15–18 (left elbow). In 2026, he had appeared in one game, completing 52.9 percent of his passes for 164 yards and two touchdowns before the injury.

Quinn said Sunday evening, 'Man, we're bummed for him. I thought he was, like, really playing his (expletive) off. … I was just disheartened for him. Like, I know what he's gone through.'

NFL Network analyst Michael Robinson, a former Pro Bowl fullback, said Sunday night: 'I think some of it is bad luck. But we know Jayden Daniels, he's a slimmer-bodied individual, alright? He's also a quarterback that will take off. He's also a quarterback that he's an ultimate competitor. If I'm Jayden, I would really look at in the offseason putting on a just a little bit of weight. This is still tackle football, this is still a physical game.'

Daniels, the No. 2 overall pick in the 2024 NFL Draft, won the Heisman Trophy at LSU in 2023. He signed a four-year, $37,746,650 contract with a $24,272,108 signing bonus and carries an $8,578,784 cap figure for 2025. The contract includes a fifth-year option the team can exercise in 2028.
