Radiopharma firms ITM and Telix merge after FDA rejection

Health & Science desk

ITM Isotope Technologies Munich SE and Telix Pharmaceuticals, two leading companies in the radiopharmaceuticals field, are merging in a deal worth at least $1.65 billion, following a surprise FDA rejection for one of the firms. Under the agreement, Telix will acquire all shares of privately held ITM, with the potential for an additional $700 million payment to ITM shareholders if the company’s lead drug meets regulatory and sales milestones.

The merger creates another well-resourced company in the radiopharmaceutical sector, which is currently dominated by Novartis. These medicines work by delivering radioactive isotopes directly to cancer cells. Novartis supercharged the field with the launch of its two such treatments in 2018 and 2022.

The deal comes after one of the companies received an unexpected rejection from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, though the source material does not specify which firm or which drug was affected.

Sources: STAT
