Ukraine War

Russia and Ukraine trade deadly strikes as Zelenskyy prepares to meet Trump at UN

International & World desk · anchored on AP

Russia and Ukraine exchanged heavy aerial attacks on Tuesday, killing at least four people in Ukraine and one in Russia, as Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy arrived in New York for a high-stakes meeting with U.S. President Donald Trump on the sidelines of the United Nations General Assembly. Trump is expected to press Zelenskyy to agree to an energy truce with Russia, according to CNBC, and the two leaders are set to discuss further air defense support for Kyiv, Ukrainian drone strikes on Russian oil refineries, and how to end the more than 4½-year conflict.

Russia’s overnight barrage hit at least four Ukrainian regions with missiles and drones, killing four people in the central city of Dnipro and a woman in the northern Sumy region, officials said. Ukraine’s air force reported the attack included 212 drones, four cruise missiles, and an unspecified number of Oniks anti-ship and ballistic missiles. The strikes damaged energy infrastructure, leaving nearly 100,000 consumers without power in the Chernihiv region, and hit industrial sites in Dnipro, Pavlohrad, Kryvyi Rih, and Poltava. The Russian Defense Ministry said it struck metallurgical and chemical industries, fuel and energy facilities, and port infrastructure, including a plant in Kyiv producing components for long-range cruise missiles and a tobacco factory in Poltava used to make small arms ammunition.

Ukraine’s special forces said they struck the Kuibyshev oil refinery in Russia’s Samara region overnight, sparking a large fire at the facility owned by Rosneft. Regional Governor Vyacheslav Fedorishchev said one person was killed and four wounded in the attack. The Russian Defense Ministry said its air defense units intercepted and destroyed 297 Ukrainian drones over 14 regions and the illegally annexed Crimean Peninsula. The strikes came as Ukraine launched what is thought to have been its largest drone attack on the Moscow region on Sunday, damaging a facility at an oil refinery on the city’s outskirts and killing three people.

Zelenskyy, who arrived in New York on Monday evening and met with French President Emmanuel Macron, said his government remained in talks with the Trump administration and that there were ideas “on the table for bringing peace closer, starting with strong de-escalation steps.” He added: “Ukraine’s energy sector, critical infrastructure, and our food exports must stop being targets for Russia, and this will lead to matching de-escalation steps on our part. Solutions are possible.” Macron said Tuesday that France would provide Ukraine with more radars to help protect its power grid and that he discussed with Trump the possibility of a truce on strikes on energy infrastructure and a “moratorium” on Black Sea attacks.

Trump on Monday posted on Truth Social that “a large number” of Russian diesel oil refineries had been “blown up” and were out of commission, writing: “This ridiculous and never ending War with Ukraine must be ended. The whole World suffers as 25,000 people, mostly soldiers, are being killed each month. What a shame!” Trump has previously called on Zelenskyy to stop targeting Russian oil refineries, saying the strikes are “hurting the world” as fuel supply disruptions from the Ukraine and Iran wars have pushed U.S. retail diesel prices above $6.50 per gallon for the first time, according to AAA. Ukraine has insisted that Russian energy sites are legitimate military targets.

The war has had global repercussions, including on food supplies. Russia and Ukraine have in recent years accounted for about 30% of the world’s wheat exports and 12% of corn exports, most shipped through the Black Sea, but attacks last month slashed both countries’ agricultural exports by two-thirds, according to a report Tuesday by Thomas de Waal of Carnegie Europe. “The lack of that grain in world markets puts several African countries at risk of famine, while world cereal prices, already pushed up by the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, could rise even higher,” the report said. Zelenskyy also plans to press Trump for more U.S.-made Patriot air defense systems and to discuss de-escalation proposals he briefed U.S. senators on Monday. Russian President Vladimir Putin is not due to attend the UN General Assembly.
