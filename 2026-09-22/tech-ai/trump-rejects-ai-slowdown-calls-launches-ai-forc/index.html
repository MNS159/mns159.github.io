Trump Rejects AI Slowdown Calls, Launches 'AI Force' and Polls for New AI Name

Tech / AI desk
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

President Donald Trump has rejected calls to slow the development of artificial intelligence, announcing plans to appoint an AI "Tsar" and create an "AI Force" while dismissing safety concerns as a "hoax." The announcement, made on his social media platform Truth Social, comes as leading industry figures have warned that humans could lose control over the rapidly advancing technology without a slowdown.

Trump wrote that he will announce the AI "Tsar" in the near future, adding, "Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!" He described AI as "the next Industrial Revolution, or Internet, but will be even larger and more impactful, possibly as much as 25% of our Country's GDP." The president asserted that the U.S. is leading China and the rest of the world in AI development and that he intends to keep it that way.

Trump characterized the backlash to AI and the proliferation of data centers as one of a series of "hoaxes" created by the Left, along with "Russiagate," climate change, and "Transgender for Everyone." He stated, "It all began with an attack on our Data Centers, until people realized how wealthy and prestigious they were for the Communities in which they were built."

The president made clear he will not impose regulation on the industry. "We will not in any way hinder or stifle the Growth of this incredible Industry. Rather, we will cherish it, help it, and watch over it, as it grows!" he wrote. The announcement comes amid a growing popular backlash to AI, but Trump has dismissed safety concerns and instead embraced the technology's expansion.
