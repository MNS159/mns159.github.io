Jeanie Buss opposes family sale of Lakers stake, setting up ownership fight

Sports desk

Jeanie Buss is contesting her family’s plan to sell its remaining 17.8% stake in the Los Angeles Lakers to new majority owners Bob Iger and Josh Kushner, according to a letter from her attorney obtained by CNBC. The dispute pits Buss against her five siblings — Joey, Jesse, Jim, Johnny and Janie — who have all reportedly agreed to sell the shares as part of a larger transaction that values the franchise at $12.5 billion. The Buss family has held an ownership interest in the Lakers since Dr. Jerry Buss bought the team in 1979.

MLB owners unanimously approve sale of Padres to José E. Feliciano and Kwanza Jones

Sports desk

Major League Baseball owners on Monday unanimously approved billionaire investors José E. Feliciano and Kwanza Jones as the new majority owners of the San Diego Padres, concluding a process that began in April when the husband-and-wife duo agreed to purchase a controlling interest from the Seidler family at a record franchise valuation of $3.9 billion. The vote was conducted by conference call. The sale is expected to close near the end of this week, and the Padres plan to introduce Feliciano and Jones at a Petco Park news conference on Aug. 24.
