Kane confirms Bayern Munich contract extension talks set to begin this week

Sports desk

Bayern Munich striker Harry Kane has confirmed that negotiations to extend his contract with the Bundesliga champions beyond 2027 are set to begin this week or next week. Speaking at a media day in Munich on Monday, the 33-year-old England captain said the discussions had been delayed until after he returned from a post-World Cup holiday but would start soon.

"I'm not sure exactly when, but they'll start this week or next week," Kane said. "We're calm, they're calm with the situation. Obviously now's the time to start talking, because with only a year left it's important. But both sides are relaxed and I'm pretty sure there'll be talks this week or next week."

Kane joined Bayern on a four-year deal in 2023 and has since scored 146 goals in 147 appearances across all competitions. During his time at the club, he has won two Bundesliga titles and a German Cup, while reaching the Champions League semi-finals in two of his three seasons. Bayern have repeatedly stated their desire to extend Kane's contract, calling him their "best transfer ever" at the end of last season. German media have reported that a new deal would run until at least 2029.

On Tuesday, German World Cup winner Lothar Matthaeus said Kane should be the favorite for the Ballon d'Or, which will be awarded in October. Matthaeus, who won the award in 1991, wrote in a column for Sky Germany that Kane "deserves" the award more than any other player after a stellar season in which he scored 61 goals in 51 games. "(Kane) delivered outstanding performances and, in my opinion, didn't have a single weak spell," Matthaeus wrote.

Defending Bundesliga champions Bayern are next in action on Saturday, when they play Borussia Dortmund in the season-opening Supercup.
