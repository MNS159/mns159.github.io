AI video startup Higgsfield raises $400M at $5.4B valuation, quadrupling in 8 months

Tech / AI desk

Higgsfield, an AI video and image generation startup founded by former Snap executive Alex Mashrabov, announced Monday that it has raised a $400 million Series B round at a $5.4 billion valuation. The raise comes just eight months after the company reached a $1.3 billion valuation, quadrupling its worth in that period.

The company, launched in 2023, offers tools such as Cinema Studio for directing AI-generated films and Marketing Studio for advertising and marketing teams. It gained attention over the past year for premiering AI-generated movies at the Cannes Film Festival and in New York. According to a company release, Higgsfield has 30 million users across 200 countries and has achieved $700 million in annualized revenue.

Enterprise adoption has become a growing area for the company. Higgsfield said it is now working with 390 of the Fortune 500. Mashrabov told TechCrunch that the company expects “enterprise adoption of video AI to become much more deeply embedded in everyday marketing and creative workflows.”

The fresh capital will be used for typical growth expenses such as hiring and product development, but also to secure compute capacity. “Video is one of the most compute-intensive domains in AI,” Mashrabov explained, noting that one minute of video requires processing equivalent to roughly 60,000 words. The company sees reliable compute access as a necessary expense to remain competitive with rivals including Synthesia and Runway.

The Series B was led by DST Global, with additional participation from Goldman Sachs Alternatives, Valor Capital, and Tribe Capital.

Sources: TechCrunch
