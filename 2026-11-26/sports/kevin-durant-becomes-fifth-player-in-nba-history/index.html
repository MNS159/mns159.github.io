Kevin Durant becomes fifth player in NBA history to reach 33,000 points

Sports desk

Kevin Durant made NBA history Wednesday night, becoming the fifth player in league history to reach 33,000 career points. Durant, playing for the Phoenix Suns, entered the game against the Houston Rockets needing 14 points and crossed the milestone with a three-pointer with 46.3 seconds remaining in the second quarter. He finished with 20 points in the Suns' 116-104 NBA Cup loss in Houston.

Durant joins Kobe Bryant, Karl Malone, Kareem Abdul-Jabbar, and LeBron James in the exclusive 33,000-point club. "It's cool," Durant told reporters after the game. "I try not to make it about myself in there. I understand that it's rare to hit these type of accomplishments and I don't ever want to undersell it, but we're playing a team game and it's not about me most of the time." He added that he appreciates teammates who take time to celebrate individual achievements, noting that the Suns also celebrated Rockets center Alperen Sengun's triple-double that night.

The milestone came amid a heated exchange between Durant and Rockets guard Dillon Brooks in the final minute of the game. With roughly 30 seconds left in the fourth quarter, the two players stopped playing and went nose-to-nose near halfcourt while play continued around them. Both players received double technical fouls, and Brooks was ejected with 23.8 seconds remaining after picking up his second technical.

Durant addressed the exchange in his postgame comments. "I just love basketball. That's simple. I love to play ball. I love everything about the game, all the details that add up to winning," he said. "I don't mind if somebody got a grievance with me. I don't mind addressing it right there in the moment. I'm sure I said some things that he probably didn't like and he addressed it right there. And I can respect that as a man. And it didn't get farther than that. But if it does get farther than that, I'll be ready for that, too."

Durant and Brooks have a history of on-court clashes, which intensified after they were swapped for one another in a blockbuster trade between Phoenix and Houston last year. Earlier this month, tensions flared again when Brooks took subtle shots at Durant's former tenure in Phoenix following an online back-and-forth between Durant and Suns owner Mat Ishbia.
