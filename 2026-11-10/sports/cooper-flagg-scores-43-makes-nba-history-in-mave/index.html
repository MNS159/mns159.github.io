Cooper Flagg scores 43, makes NBA history in Mavericks win

Sports desk

Dallas Mavericks second-year star Cooper Flagg delivered a historic performance Monday night, scoring a season-high 43 points along with 10 rebounds, six assists, three steals and three 3-pointers to lead Dallas to a 125-122 victory over the Indiana Pacers.

Flagg, who turns 20 next month, became only the second teenager in NBA history to record at least 40 points, 10 rebounds and five assists in a single game, according to the NBA's official X account. The only other player to reach those milestones as a teenager was future Hall of Famer Kevin Durant, who posted 42 points, 13 rebounds and six assists in the final game of his rookie season with the Seattle SuperSonics in 2007-08.

Selected first overall in the 2025 NBA draft out of Duke, Flagg was named NBA Rookie of the Year last season after averaging 21.0 points, 6.7 rebounds and 4.5 assists per game. In his sophomore campaign, he has elevated his play, averaging 25.9 points, 6.9 rebounds, 6.9 assists and 2.2 steals per contest through the first 10 games.

Monday's performance also marked the 14th game of Flagg's career in which he scored at least 30 points, putting him second all-time among players who have recorded 30-point games before turning 20. The only player ahead of him is LeBron James, who accomplished the feat 20 times as a teenager. With his 20th birthday still a month away, Flagg has time to add to that total.

The Mavericks, who missed the playoffs each of the previous two seasons and won only 26 games during Flagg's rookie campaign, currently sit ninth in the Western Conference with a 7-4 record, placing them firmly in playoff contention.
