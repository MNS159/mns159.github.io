NFL trade deadline: Jaguars acquire Jordan Davis; Vikings explore QB market; Bengals deal falls through

Sports desk

The Jacksonville Jaguars made a series of defensive acquisitions Monday, headlining the day with a blockbuster trade for Philadelphia Eagles defensive tackle Jordan Davis. According to multiple reports, the Jaguars sent multiple draft picks, including a first-round pick in the 2027 draft, to the Eagles for Davis. The Jaguars also added Pro Bowl safety Jessie Bates III from the Atlanta Falcons in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick, and acquired defensive tackle Davon Godchaux from the New Orleans Saints for a 2028 sixth-round pick. The moves come as Jacksonville sits at 6-2 and positions itself as a Super Bowl contender.

The Minnesota Vikings, coming off a Monday night loss to the Buffalo Bills in which quarterback Carson Wentz struggled, are actively exploring the quarterback trade market. According to Yahoo Sports, the Vikings have inquired with the Las Vegas Raiders about a reunion with Kirk Cousins, who played for Minnesota from 2018 to 2023 and made three Pro Bowls. Cousins has thrown for 2,340 yards, 20 touchdowns and eight interceptions in nine games this season for the 4-5 Raiders. The Vikings have also shown interest in Indianapolis Colts quarterback Daniel Jones, who has 1,722 passing yards, six touchdowns and 10 interceptions in nine games. Jones played for Minnesota in 2024 after being released by the New York Giants.

A reported trade between the Cincinnati Bengals and Philadelphia Eagles for edge rusher Arnold Ebiketie fell through. The deal, which would have sent Ebiketie to Cincinnati for a 2027 sixth-round draft pick, was initially reported but later fell apart. Bengals head coach Zac Taylor said Monday, "Nothing. We're good," when asked about the team's plans at the trade deadline. Ebiketie, 27, signed a one-year deal with the Eagles in March and has 23 total tackles and three tackles for loss in nine games this season.

Other trades included the Detroit Lions acquiring safety Andre Cisco from the New York Jets for a 2028 sixth-round pick, according to ESPN's Adam Schefter. Cisco, 26, has 23 tackles, a pass defended and a forced fumble in nine games this season. The Jaguars also confirmed that wide receiver Brian Thomas Jr. is not being traded, per ESPN's Jeremy Fowler, despite speculation. Dallas Cowboys owner Jerry Jones told 105.3 The Fan that the team does not expect to make a "significant" trade.

Several notable injuries were reported Monday. Los Angeles Rams All-Pro receiver Puka Nacua is expected to be placed on injured reserve with MCL and PCL sprains in his right knee and turf toe, head coach Sean McVay announced. Green Bay Packers running back Josh Jacobs will undergo surgery for a fractured finger and is expected to miss up to three weeks, per CBS Sports' Matt Zenitz. Arizona Cardinals quarterback Gardner Minshew is feared to have a torn UCL in his throwing arm and could be out for the season, according to NFL Network's Ian Rapoport. The Cardinals signed quarterback Josh Johnson off the Cleveland Browns' practice squad and will turn to rookie Carson Beck as starter. Cardinals wide receiver Michael Wilson will miss the rest of the season with a neck injury, head coach Mike LaFleur said. Baltimore Ravens head coach Jesse Minter said Jacoby Brissett is expected to start in Week 10 after the team acquired him from the Cardinals last week.

Indianapolis Colts running back Jonathan Taylor, who has 656 rushing yards and eight touchdowns this season, remains a potential trade target. The New England Patriots have been suggested as a logical suitor, especially after Rhamondre Stevenson's knee injury. Taylor thanked fans after Sunday's loss, saying, "This place helped shape me into the man I am today." The NFL trade deadline is Tuesday at 4 p.m. ET.
