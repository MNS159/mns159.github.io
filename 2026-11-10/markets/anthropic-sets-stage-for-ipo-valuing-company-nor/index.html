Anthropic sets IPO range valuing AI company at up to $1.3 trillion

Financial & Markets desk

Anthropic, the artificial intelligence company behind the Claude chatbot, has set the stage for an initial public offering that could value the firm at between $1.1 trillion and $1.3 trillion. The company plans to offer shares at $70 to $78 each on New York's Nasdaq exchange, aiming to raise up to $64 billion.

The valuation range, while enormous, falls short of the roughly $2 trillion that some backers had predicted earlier this year, as reported by the Financial Times. At the top end, Anthropic would be valued at about $1.3 trillion, below SpaceX, Elon Musk's rocket and AI company, which listed its shares in June at nearly $1.8 trillion.

Anthropic is floating at a volatile time for AI firms. After last week's U.S. midterm elections, the Nasdaq fell sharply on concerns that a Democrat-led Congress could slow the development of AI and the buildout of data centers. Broader fears persist over whether the billions of dollars being poured into AI will ultimately pay off.

In its most recent financial results for the six months ending June 30, Anthropic reported a surge in revenue to $16.2 billion, up from $1.1 billion in the same period a year earlier. However, its pre-tax loss ballooned to $80.7 billion from $12.9 billion.

Fiona Cincotta, a senior market analyst at City Index, described the $64 billion target as 'fairly bullish' given 'the volatility [and] concerns towards AI that we've seen in sort of particularly last week.' She told Reuters: 'It's showing that they are still sort of upbeat about the outlook as far as AI is concerned and that obviously that they think that the demand is going to be there.'

Anthropic is just five years old. It was co-founded in 2021 by brother and sister Dario and Daniela Amodei along with five others, all of whom previously worked at OpenAI, the rival AI giant behind ChatGPT.
