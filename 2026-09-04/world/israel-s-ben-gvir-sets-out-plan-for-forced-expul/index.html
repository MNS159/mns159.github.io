Israel's Ben-Gvir sets out plan for forced expulsion of all Palestinians from Gaza

International & World desk
LiberalConservative
Coverage is balanced across the spectrum

Israeli National Security Minister Itamar Ben-Gvir has proposed a plan for the forced expulsion of all Palestinians from the Gaza Strip, according to a report from Al Jazeera published on 4 September 2026. Defence Minister Israel Katz stated that the plan still requires approval from the United States.

The report, by Al Jazeera’s Mohammad Saleh, describes the plan as an “illegal forced expulsion.” No further details on the mechanics, timeline, or scope of the proposal were provided in the source material. Ben-Gvir, a far-right member of Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu’s coalition, has previously called for the reestablishment of Israeli settlements in Gaza and the encouragement of Palestinian emigration.

Katz’s comment that the plan needs US approval suggests that the Israeli government is not moving unilaterally on the proposal. There was no immediate reaction from the Palestinian Authority, Hamas, or the US government reported in the source.

Sources: Al Jazeera
