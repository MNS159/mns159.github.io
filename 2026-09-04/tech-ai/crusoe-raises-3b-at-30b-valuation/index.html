Crusoe Raises $3B at $30B Valuation, Plans Potential IPO

Tech / AI desk

Data center developer Crusoe, which counts Meta, Microsoft, and OpenAI among its customers, has raised a new $3 billion funding round at a $30 billion valuation, according to a Bloomberg report. The deal is co-led by Atreides Management and Valor Equity Partners, with participation from Mubadala Capital, the asset management subsidiary of Abu Dhabi’s sovereign wealth fund Mubadala.

The fresh capital comes just 10 months after Crusoe raised a $1.38 billion round at a $10 billion valuation last October, reflecting a tripling of its valuation in less than a year. The company recently signed a massive $13 billion, five-year cloud contract to supply quantitative trading firm Jane Street with GPUs and AI infrastructure, Bloomberg reported.

Launched in 2018 as a crypto mining operation powered by flared natural gas, Crusoe has since pivoted into a major AI infrastructure and cloud provider best known for developing hyperscale data center campuses for clients like Oracle and OpenAI. The company's rapid growth and customer base underscore its shift from energy-intensive crypto mining to powering the AI boom.

Crusoe recently met with investment bankers, including Goldman Sachs and Morgan Stanley, to discuss a potential near-term IPO, Axios reported last month. The new $3 billion round and the discussions with bankers signal that the company is preparing for a public offering, though no timeline has been confirmed.
