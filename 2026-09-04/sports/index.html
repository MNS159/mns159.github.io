NBA imposes historic penalties on Clippers over Kawhi Leonard salary-cap scheme

Sports desk

The NBA on Wednesday levied an unprecedented set of punishments against the Los Angeles Clippers, stripping the team of five first-round draft picks, banning owner Steve Ballmer from the arena for one year, and suspending team president of business operations Gillian Zucker for a year. The penalties stem from a league investigation that confirmed the Clippers orchestrated a secret $28 million deal with star forward Kawhi Leonard through a no-show endorsement arrangement with the financial firm Aspiration, a scheme designed to circumvent the NBA's salary cap.
