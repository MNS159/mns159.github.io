Ben Simmons agrees to 1-year, $3.5M deal with Kings

Sports desk

Three-time All-Star Ben Simmons has agreed to a one-year, $3.5 million contract with the Sacramento Kings, his agents Max Wiepking, Sean Tribe and Ryan Arney told ESPN's Shams Charania and Marc J. Spears. The deal marks Simmons' return to the NBA after he sat out the entire 2025-26 season.

Simmons, the No. 1 overall pick in the 2016 NBA Draft, traveled to Sacramento on Monday for in-depth medical exams, an on-court workout and meetings with coach Doug Christie and other staffers, according to Charania. He also worked out for Kings general manager Scott Perry in late August and met with Perry for an hour. The agreement came after Simmons completed medical examinations.

The 6-foot-10 guard/forward has not played in the NBA since the 2024-25 season, when he appeared in 51 games — his most since 2020-21 — and averaged 5.0 points, 5.6 assists and 4.7 rebounds across stints with the Brooklyn Nets and Los Angeles Clippers. Simmons won Rookie of the Year in 2018 and made the All-NBA Third Team in 2020, but injuries have limited him to 60 or fewer games every season since 2020-21, and he missed the entire 2021-22 season.

Simmons told The Athletic's Sam Jane in December that he did not want to return until he felt 'bulletproof.' In June, he told Men's Health he was working toward a comeback, mentioning Miami and Philadelphia as possible landing spots. 'I plan on getting as strong as I can physically, getting my ass on the court, and then the team realizing that my abilities will be needed,' he said.

In Sacramento, Simmons is expected to back up rookie Darius Acuff Jr., whom the organization views as a potential franchise cornerstone. The Kings have been seeking floor generals since trading De'Aaron Fox to the San Antonio Spurs. Simmons provides veteran depth, elite playmaking and defensive versatility at an affordable price, though it remains unclear whether the addition makes the Kings a contender. If Domantas Sabonis is eventually traded, Simmons could potentially work his way into the starting lineup with strong performances.
