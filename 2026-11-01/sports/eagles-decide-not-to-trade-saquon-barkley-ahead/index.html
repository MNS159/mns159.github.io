Eagles decide not to trade Saquon Barkley ahead of NFL deadline despite struggles

Sports desk

The Philadelphia Eagles have decided not to trade star running back Saquon Barkley before the NFL's Nov. 10 trade deadline, according to reports from ESPN and NFL Network, ending speculation that the former Offensive Player of the Year could be moved as the team's season spirals with a 2-5 record and a five-game losing streak.

Barkley, 29, has two years and approximately $35 million remaining on his contract and is under team control through the 2028 season. Despite interest from other clubs, general manager Howie Roseman has made it clear to potential suitors that Barkley is off the market, per NFL Network insiders Mike Garafolo and Ian Rapoport. The organization reportedly still views itself as able to contend in the future with its current offensive nucleus and wants to keep Barkley as part of its longer-term plans.

Barkley has been limited by injuries this season, appearing in five games and recording 244 rushing yards and one touchdown on 43 carries — a steep drop from his 2,005-yard season two years ago. He missed two games with a hamstring injury and suffered a back issue during Philadelphia's 38-21 loss to Dallas on Oct. 26, playing only nine snaps in that game. Barkley did not practice Wednesday or Thursday but returned Friday and is off the injury report for Sunday night's game against the Washington Commanders.

"One day I'm not going to be playing football," Barkley told reporters Friday when asked about playing through injuries. "Whenever that is, I don't want to look back and be like, 'I could've gave it a chance and I didn't.' I like playing football. So if I have an opportunity to play, I'm going to try my best to play."

The Eagles' decision to retain Barkley comes amid a season derailed by injuries. Quarterback Jalen Hurts will miss the remainder of the season after doctors found multiple blood clots on his lungs. Top wide receiver DeVonta Smith has been limited to four games and will miss another Sunday with a hamstring injury. With Tanner McKee starting at quarterback, Philadelphia ranks near the bottom of the NFL in scoring and total offense.

While Barkley is staying put, other Eagles veterans could still be dealt before the deadline. Tight end Dallas Goedert, linebacker Zack Baun and defensive lineman Moro Ojomo have been mentioned as possible trade candidates. The Eagles already made one trade this season, sending center Cam Jurgens to Baltimore earlier this month. Philadelphia hosts the New York Giants in Week 9 before its bye the following week.
