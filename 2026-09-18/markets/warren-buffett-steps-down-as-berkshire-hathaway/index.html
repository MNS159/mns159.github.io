Warren Buffett steps down as Berkshire Hathaway chairman; son Howard named successor

Financial & Markets desk

Warren Buffett, the 96-year-old legendary investor who built Berkshire Hathaway into a $1 trillion conglomerate, stepped down as chairman on Friday, effective immediately, and was named chairman emeritus while remaining a director. His son, Howard Buffett, succeeded him as chairman under a long-standing succession plan, the company announced. Susan Decker will continue as lead independent director.

In a letter to shareholders, Buffett wrote, 'Father Time always wins. He has, however, been generous with me. He has given me the opportunity to see Berkshire reach a point where I am more confident than ever about what lies ahead.' Greg Abel, who took over as CEO in May 2025, said in a company release: 'The culture Warren built and the values he championed will remain at the heart of Berkshire, and Howard will be their guardian.'

Buffett took over a failed New England textiles mill in 1965 at age 34 and over six decades transformed it into a financial and industrial juggernaut with $44.5 billion in operating earnings last year and nearly 400,000 employees. Under his tenure, Berkshire posted a 19.7% compounded annual return to shareholders, nearly double the return of the S&P 500.

Berkshire shares have struggled in 2026, up just 1% while the S&P 500 has rallied more than 11%, partly due to rising oil prices and investors' preference for higher-growth sectors. Abel has begun using more of Berkshire's $365.5 billion cash hoard to buy back shares, stepping up repurchases to $4.5 billion in the second quarter.

Buffett praised Abel in his letter: 'My expectations for him were sky high from the start, and he has exceeded them. The company is in excellent hands, and I look forward to remaining a shareholder alongside you.' Abel told CNBC: 'He gave me an extraordinary responsibility — the best job in American business — and then the latitude to lead in a manner consistent with Berkshire's culture and values. I look forward to continuing to work alongside Warren, with Howard serving as Chairman and Sue as Lead Independent Director.'
