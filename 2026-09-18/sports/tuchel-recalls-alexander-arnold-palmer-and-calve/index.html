Tuchel recalls Alexander-Arnold, Palmer, and Calvert-Lewin for England squad

Sports desk

Thomas Tuchel has recalled Trent Alexander-Arnold, Cole Palmer, and Dominic Calvert-Lewin for England’s upcoming Nations League matches, warning that players must “perform” to keep their places. The squad will assemble for four games during the September international window, beginning with a home fixture against World Cup winners Spain at Wembley on 26 September, followed by trips to the Czech Republic (29 September) and Croatia (3 October), before hosting the Czechs on 6 October.

Alexander-Arnold and Palmer were omitted from Tuchel’s squad for the 2026 World Cup but return after strong starts to the season. Alexander-Arnold, 27, last played for England in June 2025 and has made five appearances for Real Madrid this term, registering one assist. Palmer, 24, scored four goals and provided two assists in six games for Chelsea; his last England cap came in a March friendly defeat to Japan. “They had a good start to the season,” Tuchel said. “They never did something wrong. We just chose different players. So now it is a new competition… Cole has his numbers back – he has his assists back, he has decisive goals back. I think he has a point to prove, and the same goes for Trent.”

Tuchel confirmed Alexander-Arnold will be used as a right-back despite playing midfield for Real Madrid. “He had a good start in two positions for Real Madrid and he comes as a right-back for us,” he said. On what he expects from the full-back: “Perform, that’s just what it is. It is a competition… it is now time to fight for his place.” Of Palmer, Tuchel added: “Cole needs to step up when he comes and he needs to show these kind of numbers to get his place, keep his shirt and justify the nomination.”

Dominic Calvert-Lewin earned his recall after a 15-goal debut campaign at Leeds United. Leeds manager Daniel Farke called it “fully deserved”. “Perfect start to the new season. He has scored goals, has delivered performances, plays with confidence and he's in a good rhythm,” Farke said. “He has developed… and right now you could see that he is way more in his rhythm, way more settled, and obviously takes a leadership role in our group.”

The squad includes Bournemouth’s Alex Scott, Liverpool winger Rio Ngumoha, and Newcastle left-back Lewis Hall, among others. Notable absentees due to injury include Dean Henderson, Jordan Henderson, Reece James, Djed Spence, and John Stones. Phil Foden, Morgan Gibbs-White, Adam Wharton, and Harry Maguire were also omitted. The Nations League matches are England’s first since the World Cup, where they lost 2-1 to Argentina in the semi-finals before beating France 6-4 in the bronze-medal match. Tuchel reflected on that tournament, saying he may need to “embrace the chaos” and adjust his style to suit England’s strengths.
