AMD acquires World Labs AI startup for $8.2 billion, upping ante against Nvidia

Tech / AI desk

Chipmaker AMD announced it will acquire World Labs, an AI company specializing in world models, in a deal valued at $8.2 billion. The transaction is expected to close by the end of the year, subject to regulatory approval. World Labs was founded in 2024 by computer vision scientist Fei-Fei Li along with researchers Justin Johnson, Christoph Lassner, and Ben Mildenhall, and had raised $230 million in funding—some of which came from AMD.

World Labs develops world models, a class of AI systems designed to produce predictive simulations of the physical world. The company's first publicly available tool, Marble, generates small 3D spaces using Gaussian splats that can be exported as assets for film production, game development, and other applications. World Labs has since introduced additional models, such as Atlas, tailored for different use cases.

A key application area for world models is the generation of synthetic data for training robots, a field where Nvidia holds a strong lead. AMD has previously released its own world model, Micro-World, an open-source project, but has lacked a comprehensive stack to rival Nvidia's Cosmos and related products.
