Joseph scores record-equalling five tries as England thrash Japan 78-15 in Nations Championship

Sports desk

Will Joseph marked his return to international rugby after a four-year absence by scoring a record-equalling five tries as England thrashed Japan 78-15 at Twickenham in the Nations Championship. The hosts ran in 13 tries in total, with Joseph's haul matching the England record for most tries in a single match.

The comprehensive victory saw England dominate from start to finish, overwhelming Japan with a relentless attacking display. Joseph made a stunning comeback by crossing the line five times, a feat that places him alongside the greatest try-scorers in England's history.

Japan, who struggled to contain England's pace and power, were ultimately outclassed. The 78-15 scoreline reflects England's superiority in all facets of the game, with the home side converting their opportunities with clinical efficiency.

The match was part of the Nations Championship. For England, the result provides a significant boost as they build towards future international fixtures, while Japan will look to regroup after a heavy defeat on the road.
