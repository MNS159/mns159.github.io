FDA rejects Summit's ivonescimab lung cancer drug after trial misses survival endpoint

Health & Science desk · anchored on Reuters

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has issued a complete response letter to Summit Therapeutics, declining to approve its experimental lung cancer drug ivonescimab for patients with previously treated, EGFR-mutated non-small cell lung cancer, Summit announced on Sunday. The decision blocks the immediate U.S. launch of the PD-1xVEGF bispecific antibody, which had been approved in China since May 2024 for the same indication through Summit's partner Akeso.
