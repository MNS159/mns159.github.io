Panathinaikos owner confirms 'serious' offer to Nikola Jokic; agent says claim is true

Sports desk

Panathinaikos AKTOR Athens owner Dimitris Giannakopoulos made a "very serious proposal" this year to buy out the final season of Denver Nuggets star Nikola Jokic's NBA contract, an offer that was rejected by the Nuggets within seconds, Giannakopoulos said on the Euro Insiders podcast. Jokic's longtime agent, Misko Raznatovic, confirmed the claim to Athletiko, stating: "What Giannakopoulos said is true."

Giannakopoulos, the billionaire founder of Greek pharmaceutical company Vianex, said he presented the offer both to Jokic and to the Nuggets. "I did make a very serious proposal this year, both to his team and to him," Giannakopoulos said. "I got a rejection. I know that he has one more year of a contract." He described the Nuggets' response: "It took a few seconds to say, 'Who? Greece? Where? What? Bye.'" Jokic is under contract for a $59 million salary next season and holds a $63.8 million player option for the 2027-28 season.

Giannakopoulos vowed to pursue Jokic again next summer. "There is one player who is a dream of mine, whom I haven't signed yet, but I will sign next year," he said. "It's Jokic, the best player in the world!" When hosts laughed, he insisted: "Don't laugh at all. If I don't sign Jokic, I'm not going anywhere." He dismissed the notion that he would need to match a potential five-year, $360 million max extension from the Nuggets, arguing the relevant figure to beat is Jokic's $64 million player option for 2027-28.

Jokic, the three-time NBA MVP, informed the Nuggets this offseason that he would not sign a contract extension. He was eligible for a four-year, $278 million deal but can add another year and roughly $80 million more by waiting until next summer. Jokic has said he plans to play for the Nuggets for the remainder of his career, but his decision not to extend has drawn league-wide attention. NBA insider Marc Stein told the DNVR Nuggets Podcast: "There are a lot of eyeballs on the Denver Nuggets right now league-wide. There is a lot of curiosity about where this team is going."

This is not Giannakopoulos's first attempt to lure a Nuggets player. Last year, he tried to buy out the final two years and $20 million remaining on center Jonas Valanciunas's contract after Denver acquired him in a trade. Valanciunas chose to play for Denver, but the Nuggets later waived him, and he signed with Lithuanian club Zalgiris Kaunas. Panathinaikos finished second in the Greek Basketball League last season at 19-5 and seventh in EuroLeague with a 22-16 record. The highest-paid player in European basketball last season made $5.6 million, while Panathinaikos paid Kendrick Nunn $5.3 million.

Jokic, who led the NBA in rebounds (12.9) and assists (10.7) last season while averaging 27.7 points, is also set to represent Serbia in upcoming FIBA World Cup qualifying windows. The Nuggets face financial constraints after matching a $12 million offer sheet to Spencer Jones, entering the second apron, and facing a $32 million increase in luxury taxes, which could complicate re-signing restricted free agent Peyton Watson.
