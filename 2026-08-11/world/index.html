Syrian court sentences ex-President Assad to death in absentia

International & World desk

A Syrian criminal court in Damascus has sentenced ousted President Bashar al-Assad to death in absentia after convicting him of premeditated murder, torture, war crimes and crimes against humanity during the country's nearly 14-year civil war. It was the first conviction against the former leader, who fled to Moscow with his family after his regime was overthrown in December 2024, ending the Assad family's five-decade grip on power.

At Least 13 Killed in Ukrainian Drone Strike on Russian Oil Refinery

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

At least 13 people were killed and 75 others wounded in a Ukrainian drone strike on the city of Nizhnekamsk in the Russian republic of Tatarstan, local officials said Monday. The strike hit the Taneko oil refinery, one of Russia's largest, more than 1,100 kilometers (680 miles) from the Ukrainian border. A child was among the dead, according to the head of Tatarstan, Rustam Minnikhanov, who declared a day of mourning.
