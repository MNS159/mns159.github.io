At Least 13 Killed in Ukrainian Drone Strike on Russian Oil Refinery

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

At least 13 people were killed and 75 others wounded in a Ukrainian drone strike on the city of Nizhnekamsk in the Russian republic of Tatarstan, local officials said Monday. The strike hit the Taneko oil refinery, one of Russia's largest, more than 1,100 kilometers (680 miles) from the Ukrainian border. A child was among the dead, according to the head of Tatarstan, Rustam Minnikhanov, who declared a day of mourning.

Nine of the victims were in a hostel at the time of the attack, the Russian news agency RIA reported, citing the regional governor's office. The Uzbek consulate in Kazan said seven of those killed were citizens of Uzbekistan. Russia's Investigative Committee said civilian individuals not involved in military operations were present where the drones struck. The attack lasted several hours overnight and into the morning, with verified images on social media showing large plumes of smoke and flames.

Ukraine's military confirmed it hit the Taneko refinery, as part of its campaign to raise the cost of Russia's war. Andriy Kovalenko, head of Ukraine's Center for Countering Disinformation, said the facilities targeted are used to carry out attacks against Ukraine and support Russia's war effort. He added that every civilian death in Russia is on President Vladimir Putin and that without Putin's aggression, those people would still be alive.

In other overnight attacks, Russian authorities reported one person killed and two wounded in the Voronezh region, and a fire at an industrial facility in the Orenburg region after Ukraine said it struck one of the country's largest oil refineries there. Fires also broke out in Siberia's Tyumen region after falling drones hit an industrial facility, local officials said. The Russian online retailer Wildberries said its facilities were attacked but goods were mostly unaffected. Moscow said more than 450 Ukrainian drones were shot down overnight across Russia.

Ukraine itself was hit by Russian strikes that killed at least nine people, including six in Zaporizhzhia, where the regional governor said rockets and guided aerial bombs struck the area. Explosions were reported in Kyiv, where a children's hospital was struck but patients and staff were sheltering. President Volodymyr Zelensky said North Korean ballistic missiles were used in the attacks, describing it as a vicious attack. The drone strike in Tatarstan is one of the deadliest single attacks on Russia since the start of the full-scale war in 2022, as Kyiv has stepped up long-range strikes on energy and industrial facilities that contribute to Russia's war logistics.
