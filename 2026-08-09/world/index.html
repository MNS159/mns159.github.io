Russia-Ukraine War

Overnight Strikes Kill Seven in Ukraine and Russia; Drone Incident in Bulgaria Raises Tensions

International & World desk · anchored on AP

At least seven people were killed in overnight attacks across Ukraine and Russia, officials reported Saturday. In Ukraine, a Russian missile strike near Kyiv killed three people, including a child, and wounded three others, according to local authorities. Meanwhile, Ukrainian drone strikes hit two Russian oil refineries—the Ilsky and Syzran facilities—causing fires, the Ukrainian general staff said. The attacks come as Russia intensifies its aerial campaign, aware that Ukraine's stock of Western-donated interceptor missiles is running low.

Middle East

Iran sets six sweeping conditions for Strait of Hormuz reopening; Houthis claim attack on Saudi Aramco facility

International & World desk

Iran’s Supreme National Security Council has issued six new conditions the United States must meet before the Strait of Hormuz can fully reopen, broadening the terms beyond the June memorandum of understanding, as Iranian President Masoud Pezeshkian called for an end to a state of “neither war nor peace” with Washington. The council’s secretary Mohammad Bagher Zolghadr, as reported by state broadcaster IRIB, said the strait would remain closed until the U.S. “corrects its behaviour.” The conditions include an end to U.S. threats and insults to Iran’s national and religious values; a permanent halt to attacks on Iran and its allies in Lebanon, Palestine, Yemen and Iraq; lifting the U.S. naval blockade and withdrawal of U.S. naval and air forces from around Iran; compensation for damage from what Zolghadr called two “imposed wars”; lifting of sanctions; and unconditional release of frozen Iranian assets.
