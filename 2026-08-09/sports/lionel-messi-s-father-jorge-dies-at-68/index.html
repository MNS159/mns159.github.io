Lionel Messi's father Jorge dies at 68

Sports desk

Lionel Messi missed Inter Miami's Leagues Cup defeat to Monterrey on Saturday night after the death of his father, Jorge Messi, at the age of 68. Jorge, who had been the Argentina forward's agent since Messi was 14, died on Saturday in a hospital in his hometown of Rosario, Argentina, following a long illness. Miami paid tribute before kickoff with a minute's silence, black armbands, and Jorge's name displayed on the big screens at Nu Stadium, while the club added a black ribbon to its social media profiles and said: "Our hearts are with our captain, Leo, and the entire Messi family."

Messi's Argentina teammate Rodrigo de Paul celebrated scoring the opening goal in the 2-1 defeat by removing his shirt to reveal a Messi shirt underneath, and fans held handmade signs offering support. Miami striker Luis Suarez, suspended for the match, wrote on Instagram that Jorge "will continue to watch over" his son "with pride, as he always did." Messi's former club Barcelona observed a moment of silence before their 1-0 friendly win against Nottingham Forest in Udine, Italy, and thanked Jorge "for his commitment to our club, for entrusting us with the beginnings and the most glorious years of his son Leo's football career."

Jorge Messi played a key role in persuading Barcelona to sign his 13-year-old son in 2000, when he accompanied Lionel to Catalonia. Fans left flags and flowers outside Messi's childhood home in Rosario, and Argentine clubs Boca Juniors and River Plate both held periods of silence before their matches. The Argentine Football Association said it "deeply mourns with sadness and sorrow the passing of Jorge Messi, father of our captain and emblem, Lionel Messi," adding: "From here, we stand in solidarity with the entire family during this difficult time and send the most heartfelt, warm and affectionate embrace." Paris St-Germain, where Messi played from 2021 to 2023, said: "The club sends its full support to Leo, his family and loved ones in this painful ordeal. May he rest in peace."

During the 2026 World Cup, there were unconfirmed reports about Jorge's health, and the family later announced he was undergoing "a health situation" during the summer. After scoring in Argentina's opening game against Algeria, Messi told the media his tears had "nothing to do with soccer" as he was going "through some difficult, complicated days," adding: "I am thankful for the support I have received from my teammates and the staff during this time." Jorge was unable to travel to the United States for the World Cup, and Lionel traveled to Rosario to be with him immediately after the final on July 19 before returning to Inter Miami, where he scored twice in a Leagues Cup match against San Luis.

Jorge is survived by his wife, Celia, and his four children: Lionel, his two older brothers Rodrigo and Matias, and younger sister Maria Sol. Newell's Old Boys, Messi's first club, said in a statement: "Newell's Old Boys mourn with profound pain and sorrow the passing of Jorge Messi. He was a renowned [Newell's] supporter, businessman and the father of the captain of the Argentina national team, Lionel Messi. Jorge was the support and the person who, together with his wife, Celia, strengthened the career of the greatest player of all time with vision, rigour and affection." Real Madrid also expressed condolences, saying: "Our club wishes to express its condolences to Leo, his family, and all his loved ones. May he rest in peace."
