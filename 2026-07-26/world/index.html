Berlin Pride ramming suspect shot dead by police after manhunt

Ballout, a German citizen of Lebanese heritage, was located at an allotment garden complex in the Spandau district and rushed at officers with a bladed weapon, prompting police to open fire, authorities said. Tens of thousands evacuated as wildfires rage across Europe and North Africa

International & World desk

Tens of thousands of people have been evacuated from their homes as wildfires continue to rage across parts of Europe and North Africa, with France and Spain among the hardest-hit countries. In France, approximately 55,000 people were evacuated overnight into Sunday from villages south of Bordeaux in the Gironde region, bringing the total number of evacuees in the country's southwest to more than 220,000, according to Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez. In Spain, more than 90,000 people have been evacuated from regions around Madrid and Ávila, with additional evacuations near Valencia. Across both countries, more than 330,000 people have been forced to flee.
