US-Iran Conflict

US and Iran pause strikes for second day as diplomatic efforts continue

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

The United States and Iran have paused military strikes for a second consecutive day, marking the first sustained halt in hostilities since the US began a nightly bombing campaign against Iran nearly two weeks ago. No US attacks on Iran were reported on Saturday or Sunday, and Iran’s army spokesman, Mohammad Akraminia, confirmed that Tehran had also halted its retaliatory operations. “Our strategy has essentially been retaliatory. We have also halted our retaliatory operations,” Akraminia said, according to the AFP news agency. The pause follows 13 nights of US airstrikes on Iranian coastal areas and infrastructure, which Iran had met with counterstrikes on countries in the region hosting US military forces.

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Berlin Pride attack suspect Abdul Ballout shot dead by police

International & World desk

Berlin police shot and killed Abdul Ballout, the 21-year-old German citizen of Lebanese heritage suspected of carrying out a van-ramming and stabbing attack at a Berlin Pride event on Saturday evening, ending a 24-hour manhunt. Officers opened fire after Ballout rushed at them with a knife at an allotment garden complex in the western borough of Spandau on Sunday evening, police said. Despite immediate resuscitation efforts by the Berlin Fire Department, he died at the scene.

Wildfires

Wildfires force mass evacuations in France and Spain, disrupt Tour de France

International & World desk

Wildfires raging across southwestern France and parts of Spain have forced more than 300,000 people to evacuate their homes, authorities said Sunday, as the blazes disrupted the final stage of the Tour de France and caused widespread damage. In France, some 55,000 people were evacuated overnight into Sunday in the Gironde region near Bordeaux, bringing the total number of evacuees in the country’s southwest to more than 220,000, according to Interior Minister Laurent Nuñez. In Spain, more than 90,000 people have been evacuated from regions around Madrid and Ávila, with over 60,000 evacuated near the capital alone, the Interior Ministry reported.
