Ukraine-Iran Conflict

Iran warns Ukraine attack on Caspian vessel 'cannot go unanswered'

International & World desk · anchored on Reuters

Iran has accused Ukraine of attacking an Iranian commercial vessel in the Caspian Sea, an explosion that killed one sailor and wounded another, and warned the attack "cannot go unanswered." Tehran summoned Ukraine’s chargé d’affaires to protest what it called a "hostile and criminal" attack, according to state news agency IRNA.

Iran’s Foreign Ministry claimed the attack had been acknowledged by Ukrainian leadership and said it reflected Kyiv’s continued "irrational and hostile approach" toward Iran. It described the incident as a violation of Article 2(4) of the UN Charter and warned it could further inflame and expand the Russia-Ukraine war. Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi discussed the matter with EU foreign policy chief Kaja Kallas, calling for a "firm response" from the UN Security Council, the EU, and the international community, and for the perpetrators to be held accountable.

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy announced on X that Kyiv had achieved "very strong results" with long-range strikes in the Caspian Sea, hitting "vessels used in military cargo shipments involving Iran, as well as a warship." He did not identify the commercial vessel named by Tehran as a target. Zelenskyy separately alleged that Russia had been passing satellite imagery of Gulf states and US military sites to Iran, covering airbases in Bahrain, Jordan, and Kuwait on July 19 and 20.

Iran and Ukraine have been at odds since 2022, when Iran sold Russia the Shahed drones that Moscow has since used extensively against Ukrainian cities. Kyiv says it has shot down more than 44,000 of the Iranian-designed drones since Russia’s invasion. Ukraine has also offered its drone interceptors and expertise to Gulf governments that have come under Iranian attack during the US-Iran war, deploying anti-drone specialists to Qatar, the UAE, Saudi Arabia, and Kuwait.

In its condemnation, Iran’s Foreign Ministry reiterated that Tehran has never intervened in the Russia-Ukraine conflict. The attack on Iran’s northern coast, said Al Jazeera’s Tohid Asadi reporting from Tehran, is "putting extra pressure" on Iran’s maritime trade as it also deals with a US blockade on its southern coast.
