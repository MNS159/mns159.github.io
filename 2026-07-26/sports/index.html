49ers coach Kyle Shanahan injured in car accident, will begin training camp in limited role

Sports desk

San Francisco 49ers head coach Kyle Shanahan was involved in a car accident last week and suffered non-life-threatening injuries, the team announced Saturday. Shanahan, 46, will participate in team activities on a limited basis at the start of training camp while he recovers. Assistant head coach Chris Foerster and the team's coordinators will handle the bulk of head coach duties during this period.

PGA

Rookie Koivun holds off Scheffler for maiden PGA win

Sports desk

American rookie Jackson Koivun held his nerve to beat world number one Scottie Scheffler by three strokes and claim his first PGA Tour victory at the 3M Open on Sunday. Playing in just his third tournament since turning professional, the 21-year-old former world amateur number one closed with a bogey-free five-under 66 to finish at 25-under 259, a tournament record. Scheffler fired an eight-under 63 but settled for second at 22 under.

Brewers' Misiorowski sets franchise strikeout record

Sports desk

Milwaukee Brewers right-hander Jacob Misiorowski set a franchise record for strikeouts in a game Sunday, becoming the first pitcher since 1920 to record 11 strikeouts and allow no hits through four innings. Misiorowski finished with 12 strikeouts and allowed just one run on a solo home run by Cole Carrigg in the fifth inning, after not allowing a hit through the first four innings.
