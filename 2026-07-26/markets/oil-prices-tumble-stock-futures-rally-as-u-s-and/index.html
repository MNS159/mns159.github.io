Oil prices tumble, stock futures rally as U.S. and Iran pause attacks

Financial & Markets desk

U.S. stock-index futures rallied and oil prices tumbled on Sunday after the United States and Iran took a pause from fighting, according to multiple market reports. The moves come as Wall Street prepares for a busy week that includes the Federal Reserve’s policy meeting and key earnings reports from Big Tech companies.

The pause in hostilities between the U.S. and Iran drove a sharp reversal in energy markets, with crude oil prices sinking sharply in early trading. Meanwhile, equity futures climbed, reflecting investor relief that the immediate risk of further escalation had subsided.

Market participants are now turning their attention to the Federal Reserve’s meeting, which will be closely watched for any signals on interest rate policy. Additionally, a slate of earnings reports from major technology firms is expected to provide further direction for stock markets in the days ahead.

The combination of easing geopolitical tensions and a packed economic and corporate calendar is likely to keep volatility elevated. Analysts noted that the temporary lull in fighting between the U.S. and Iran has removed a key source of uncertainty, but the broader outlook for oil prices and equities remains tied to the Fed’s decisions and earnings results.
