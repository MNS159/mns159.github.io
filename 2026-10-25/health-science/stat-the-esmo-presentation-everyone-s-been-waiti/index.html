ESMO Day 2: Nearly 40,000 Attendees in Madrid; STAT+ Subscribers Get Exclusive Daily Coverage

Health & Science desk

The European Society for Medical Oncology's annual meeting is entering its second day in Madrid, with nearly 40,000 people in attendance, according to a STAT newsletter report from the conference.

STAT's "ESMO in 30 Seconds" newsletter, which provided the update, said it will publish one more edition wrapping up the conference on Monday afternoon. On Thursday, STAT will host a live recap and question-and-answer session covering all the ESMO news.

The article is exclusive to STAT+ subscribers, who also receive daily coverage and analysis of the biotech sector.
