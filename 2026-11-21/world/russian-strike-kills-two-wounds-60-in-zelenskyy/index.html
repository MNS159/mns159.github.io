Ukraine War

Russian strike kills two, wounds 60 in Zelenskyy's hometown Kryvyi Rih

International & World desk

A Russian ballistic missile strike on the central Ukrainian city of Kryvyi Rih — President Volodymyr Zelenskyy's hometown — killed two people and wounded at least 60 others on Friday evening, local officials said Saturday. The dead included a seven-year-old boy and a 20-year-old woman, while six children were among the wounded. Oleksandr Vilkul, head of the city administration, reported that about 25 apartment buildings, a hospital, schools, and an industrial facility were damaged. He added that Russian forces launched further missiles after emergency workers arrived, striking rescuers, medical personnel, and utility crews. Footage from Ukraine's emergency services appeared to show additional strikes during the rescue operation.

The attack followed Russian President Vladimir Putin's vow of a 'painful' response to Ukraine's strikes this week on the Kerch Bridge linking Russian-occupied Crimea with Russia, a vital supply route for Russian forces. Moscow confirmed a further strike on Kryvyi Rih on Friday night, saying a thermal power plant was hit along with a power substation in the Rivne region, both of which it claimed supply electricity to Ukraine's military industry.

The violence came as two Russian missiles and a suspected drone landed in southeastern Poland, a NATO member, on Friday. Polish Prime Minister Donald Tusk described the incident as a 'deliberate provocation' and questioned its timing alongside military exercises involving European countries. NATO Secretary-General Mark Rutte condemned the airspace violation on Saturday and pledged that the alliance would defend Polish territory.

Elsewhere in Ukraine, Russian attacks killed two people in the southern Zaporizhia region, struck a cargo vessel near Odesa, wounded 11 people including two children in Kharkiv, and wounded one person in Kyiv. In Russia, local officials said Ukrainian drone attacks wounded three people in the Bryansk and Belgorod border regions. Finland reported a drone attack on a Russian cargo ship in the eastern Baltic Sea on Saturday morning, with Prime Minister Petteri Orpo warning that the war is 'spilling over into our immediate vicinity.'

Russian authorities also reported six deaths, including a child, from a Ukrainian attack on a residential area in Russian-controlled Luhansk in eastern Ukraine. On the diplomatic front, U.S. President Donald Trump said after talks with Putin in China that the Russian leader wanted to end the war diplomatically. Zelenskyy told reporters in Kyiv on Saturday he remained less convinced that Putin was prepared to compromise, noting Moscow's demand for Ukrainian withdrawal from the entire Donbas region, which Kyiv refuses. Zelenskyy reaffirmed Ukraine's ambition to join NATO, while Washington has discussed offering security guarantees resembling the alliance's collective-defense commitments.
