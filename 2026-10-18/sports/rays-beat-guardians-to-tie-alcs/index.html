Rays beat Guardians to tie ALCS

Sports desk

The Tampa Bay Rays evened the American League Championship Series at two games apiece on Saturday, defeating the Cleveland Guardians 7-3 at Progressive Field in a game delayed a day by heavy rain. The victory sets up a best-of-three finish for a spot in the World Series against the Milwaukee Brewers, with Game 5 scheduled for 5 p.m. ET Sunday. The rain postponement erased the series' second travel day, meaning Games 4 through 7 would be played on four consecutive days if necessary.

Tampa Bay's offense, which had scored as many as four runs only twice in eight postseason games entering Saturday, broke out against a Guardians bullpen anchored by Foster Griffin. The Rays tied the game in the top of the third when Yandy Díaz hit a two-out double and Jonathan Aranda drove Díaz in two pitches later. Junior Caminero, who had been quiet in the postseason, gave the Rays a 2-1 lead in the sixth inning with an RBI double. Tampa Bay added three insurance runs in the seventh, and Díaz sealed the margin with a two-run homer in the top of the ninth.

Caminero nearly homered in the fourth inning, launching a fly ball with an exit velocity of 110.7 mph and a 29-degree launch angle — a combination that had produced a home run on every one of 180 previous similar occasions at the ballpark. But the mid-40s temperature in Cleveland kept the ball in play, and Guardians center fielder Petey Halpin caught it at the wall. Caminero, who tied for the AL lead with 42 homers during the regular season, remained homer-less in the postseason but delivered the go-ahead double two innings later.

The Guardians rallied in the bottom of the seventh, stringing together four straight two-out hits to score two runs and cut the Rays' lead to 5-3. The inning ended when Steven Kwan grounded out to first base with the tying run on base, sliding in headfirst and busting his lip in the process; he remained in the game with a bandage. Rays manager Kevin Cash said after the game, "Overall, just happy that Junior squared some balls up, Aranda came through in a big way for us and then Yandy there at the end with a two-run, add-on homer."

The series now shifts to a best-of-three format. Game 5 will feature Drew Rasmussen starting for the Rays and Parker Messick for the Guardians. The winner of the ALCS will face the Milwaukee Brewers, who have not played since Thursday, in the Fall Classic.
