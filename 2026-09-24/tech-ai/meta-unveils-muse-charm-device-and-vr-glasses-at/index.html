Meta unveils Muse Charm device and VR glasses at Connect 2026

Tech / AI desk

Meta held its annual Meta Connect showcase on Wednesday, unveiling a slate of new wearables including the Muse Charm, a standalone AI gadget, and the Meta VR Glasses, a lightweight VR device in a glasses form factor. The company also announced camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio smart glasses, refreshed Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 glasses, and updates to its in-house glasses line, along with expanded availability for existing products in multiple countries.

The Muse Charm, teased by CEO Mark Zuckerberg at the end of the keynote, is a handheld device housing Meta’s Muse AI agent. According to a Bloomberg report, it features a two-inch OLED touchscreen, a built-in 5G modem for offline AI interaction, a fingerprint sensor, cameras for photos and video, speakers, microphones, and a USB-C charging port. The device can recognize and interact with other nearby Charms. It will run a new operating system and can be set up via Meta’s Muse app on iOS or Android. Zuckerberg said the company plans to ship the Charm “in time for the holidays in December” but did not confirm pricing; sources told Bloomberg it will likely cost as much as a smartwatch, with mobile carrier fees for 5G access still undetermined. Zuckerberg noted the company has “only built a few of these so far” and needs to finalize “the finishing material,” describing a translucent exterior with an “elevated hacker aesthetic.”

The Meta VR Glasses offer full virtual reality in a smart glasses-style frame weighing 100 grams—described as “five times lighter than Meta Quest 3.” They are tethered to a pocket-sized puck containing a Qualcomm Snapdragon Reality Elite processor, 128GB of storage, 12GB of RAM, and a battery offering up to three hours of media playback. The glasses feature a 5K micro OLED “Infinite Display” with 2412 x 2288 pixels per eye and a refresh rate up to 120Hz. They have access to the full Meta Quest catalog of apps and games, support Xbox Cloud Gaming, and can connect to a Mac or Windows laptop as a virtual external display with an optional virtual keyboard and touchpad. Meta says the glasses are “the first IMAX Enhanced certified VR device ever” and include a “hologram” feature that creates a “photorealistic digital version of your face” for use in calls. The Meta VR Glasses will launch in spring 2027 at a price of $1,299.

Meta also introduced the Ray-Ban Meta Audio, camera-free smart glasses weighing 43 grams with up to 12 hours of battery life (48 hours with the charging case). They launch October 13th starting at $349 with 23 color and lens combinations. The refreshed Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 glasses offer up to nine hours of battery life, a customizable action button for instant access to Meta AI, and a six-mic array that cuts “more than 90% of background noise.” They start at $449 with 27 color and lens combinations, including new Zena and Aviator frame styles. A lower-cost version of the Meta Adventurer glasses will start at $249, launching October 23rd. Meta also announced a “Mix & Match” customization program, new EssilorLuxottica styles (Capri and Nova), a new ivory colorway for the Kylie Jenner Starfire frames, and a collaboration collection with Lisa of Blackpink.

Meta Glasses are expanding to Singapore, South Korea, Mexico, and the United Arab Emirates starting today, with Brazil “coming soon.” The Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses are now on sale in the UK and Canada, and will be available in France, Italy, and Germany starting October 13th. Meta is launching a new Explore app for Display glasses and adding features including photo editing, an improved calendar, and mirrored phone notifications. The “hologram” feature from the VR Glasses will roll out in Early Access on WhatsApp to Meta Ray-Ban Display users in the US later this fall. Meta said it is “working on” bringing the Muse AI assistant to its smart glasses, where it will be able to guide workouts, log meals, book appointments, and help purchase products. New Muse features include video calls with a customizable live avatar and dedicated email addresses for the AI agent.

The Muse AI agent, less than a month old, is capable of taking actions on a user’s behalf, including computer use on Macs and control over an email address. Zuckerberg dedicated part of the keynote to new capabilities for the agent. The Muse Charm is Meta’s entry into the experimental AI hardware space, with the closest analog being the Rabbit R1. Zuckerberg promised to share more details about the Charm “soon.”
