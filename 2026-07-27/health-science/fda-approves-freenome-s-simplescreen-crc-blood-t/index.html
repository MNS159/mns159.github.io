FDA approves Freenome's SimpleScreen CRC blood test for colorectal cancer screening

Health & Science desk · anchored on Reuters

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration has approved Freenome's SimpleScreen CRC blood test for colorectal cancer screening in average-risk adults, according to multiple news reports.

The test is a blood-based screening tool intended for individuals at average risk for colorectal cancer. The approval marks a new option in noninvasive cancer screening, as the test requires only a blood draw rather than a colonoscopy or stool-based test.

Freenome, the company behind SimpleScreen CRC, developed the test to detect colorectal cancer signals from blood samples. The FDA's decision follows clinical data supporting the test's performance in the intended population.

Coverage of the approval was provided by Reuters, HCP Live, OncLive, and Patient Care Online, each highlighting the test's potential to increase screening rates among average-risk adults who might otherwise avoid traditional screening methods.
