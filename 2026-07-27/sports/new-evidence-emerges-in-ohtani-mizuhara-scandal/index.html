New evidence emerges in Ohtani-Mizuhara scandal

Sports desk

New evidence has emerged in the sports gambling scandal involving Los Angeles Dodgers star Shohei Ohtani and his former interpreter, Ippei Mizuhara, according to a recent ESPN podcast investigation. The two-part series, titled "The Betrayal of Shohei Ohtani," was released Monday and includes a preview clip from Saturday in which investigative reporter Tisha Thompson describes a paper trail linking Ohtani directly to bookmaker Matthew Bowyer.

In the clip, Thompson recounts meeting a source who showed her screenshots on an encrypted app, each image disappearing after 30 seconds. "According to the screenshots I just saw, Shohei Ohtani’s bank account sent wire transfers to Matt Boyer’s illegal bookmaking operation. I saw two of them, totaling $1 million," Thompson says. She adds that before she could process the information, another source reached out with new details and a new name: Ippei Mizuhara, Ohtani’s interpreter of six years. That source said Mizuhara was prepared to claim he was the one gambling and that Ohtani was paying his debts.

The scandal first broke in 2024 when Mizuhara was convicted of bank fraud for impersonating Ohtani to pay off gambling debts owed to Bowyer. The conviction allowed Major League Baseball to avoid the embarrassment of having an all-time great linked to gambling in the same way as Reds legend Pete Rose, whose career and legacy were overshadowed by his own betting. However, many fans and pundits have remained convinced that Ohtani played a larger role than officially indicated.

ESPN also shared a recording of Mizuhara impersonating Ohtani during a call to authorize a transfer of funds to Bowyer’s account. The league offices likely breathed a collective sigh of relief when the episodes aired, as the recording appears to confirm Ohtani was a victim betrayed by someone he trusted. Nonetheless, the story continues to generate speculation and shows no signs of fading, despite MLB’s apparent desire to move on.

The first two episodes of "The Betrayal of Shohei Ohtani" are available wherever podcasts are streamed.
