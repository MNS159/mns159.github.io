Boxing greats pay tribute to Katie Taylor ahead of final fight

Sports desk

Katie Taylor will retire from boxing on Saturday after her fight with Flora Pili at Croke Park in Dublin, and as she prepares for her final bout, some of the biggest names in women's boxing have paid tribute to her career. Among them is Deirdre Gogarty, the woman who inspired Taylor as a child and who became Ireland's first female world boxing champion in 1997.

Gogarty wrote an open letter to Taylor, reflecting on how far the sport has come since she first walked into a boxing gym in Drogheda 39 years ago. 'Back then, I was told women would never be accepted in boxing. That even if we were allowed to compete, nobody would want to watch,' Gogarty wrote. 'And now, tens of thousands of people will fill one of Ireland's most iconic sporting venues to watch two women box... wow!!!'

Gogarty's letter was a reply to a note Taylor sent her in 1996, when Taylor was 10 or 11 years old. In that letter, Taylor wrote: 'I can't see boxing for girls taking off over here in the near future... Hopefully things will change over the next few years but maybe one day they'll even let us box in the Olympics.' Taylor also mentioned that she trained two nights a week at her father's club and was struggling to find fights, while her brother Peter was a champion.

Gogarty praised Taylor for transforming women's boxing beyond championships, medals, and records. 'You didn't simply succeed in the sport - you helped transform how the world sees it, so that another generation of girls never has to question whether they belong,' she wrote. 'When I sit among that enormous crowd at Croke Park, I'll be thinking about all the women who fought when nobody was watching.'

Other women's boxing greats, including Claressa Shields and Amanda Serrano, have also paid tribute to Taylor as she prepares to retire. Taylor, 40, faces Pili on Saturday, 5 September at Croke Park. Coverage will be on BBC Radio 5 Sports Extra from 19:00 BST, then 5 Live from 22:00, with ringwalks for the main event expected between 22:00 and 22:30.
