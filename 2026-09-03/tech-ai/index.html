AI M&A

Nvidia acquires Hugging Face for $12.93 billion, vows to keep AI platform open

Tech / AI desk

Nvidia announced Thursday it will acquire Hugging Face, the leading open-source AI model repository, for $12.93 billion, cementing the chipmaker's role in the broader AI ecosystem. The deal, confirmed after weeks of speculation, includes $11.9 billion paid to shareholders and an additional $1 billion in equity to retain Hugging Face employees. The acquisition is expected to close in the first half of 2027, according to a securities filing.

Antitrust

Judge orders Google to adjust ad business but spares breakup

Tech / AI desk

A federal judge ruled Wednesday that Google will not be forced to sell its online advertising exchange, rejecting the Justice Department’s most aggressive remedy in the long-running ad-tech antitrust case. In a sealed order, Judge Leonie M. Brinkema of the Eastern District of Virginia instead required the company to adjust its business practices to favor competitors, according to TechCrunch and Ars Technica. The full ruling will remain under seal for 14 days to allow the parties to request redactions, meaning the specific changes Google must make are not yet public.

Data Breach

FBI probes breach exposing millions of driver’s license photos from ID verification service

Tech / AI desk

The FBI is investigating a data breach that exposed more than 150 million driver’s licenses, passports and other identity documents, after an identity-theft service called Nexus began selling the records on the dark web this week. According to a report by security journalist Brian Krebs, whose own license was among the stolen documents, the trove includes high-resolution scans showing both the front and back of IDs, as well as infrared and ultraviolet images that could be used to create counterfeit documents that pass hologram tests. The stolen records belong to individuals in the United States and Canada.
