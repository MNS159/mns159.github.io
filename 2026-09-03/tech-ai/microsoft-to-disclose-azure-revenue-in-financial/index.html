Microsoft to Disclose Azure Revenue in Financial Reporting Overhaul

Tech / AI desk

Microsoft announced it will overhaul its financial reporting structure, splitting its businesses into two segments and disclosing quarterly revenue for its Azure cloud business for the first time. The changes, which take effect with the company's first-quarter fiscal 2027 earnings report in late October, are designed to reflect the impact of artificial intelligence on Microsoft's operations and business models.

The new structure consolidates Microsoft's previous three reporting segments—Productivity and Business Processes, Intelligent Cloud, and More Personal Computing—into two: Agents and Infra, and Devices and Consumer. Agents and Infra will encompass Azure, Microsoft 365 Cloud, Microsoft 365 commercial and consumer products, server products, industry solutions, and enterprise and partner services. Devices and Consumer will include search and advertising revenues from LinkedIn and search advertising, as well as revenue from the Xbox division and Windows OEM and devices.

For the first time, Microsoft will provide quarterly revenue figures for Azure. The company had previously only disclosed year-over-year growth rates for the business, and began reporting Azure revenue annually last year. Under the new structure, Azure revenue will be more narrowly defined, excluding GitHub cloud, other developer cloud services, Security Copilot, and Healthcare and Life Sciences cloud revenues. 'Under this reporting structure, Azure becomes more purely our consumption-based platform and infrastructure business,' said Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella.

Nadella framed the changes as a response to the transformative effect of AI on the company. 'There's no question AI represents a profound shift in both technology and business,' he said. 'It is changing what we build and how we operate, and it is blurring the boundaries between our products and reshaping our business models.' The new segments are intended to give investors 'full transparency of quarterly revenue across each of our key businesses, including Azure, M365 Cloud, industry solutions, and ads,' Nadella added.

The reporting overhaul marks a significant shift in how Microsoft communicates its financial performance, providing investors with more granular data on its cloud and AI-driven businesses. The company will begin using the new structure when it reports earnings for the first quarter of fiscal 2027.
