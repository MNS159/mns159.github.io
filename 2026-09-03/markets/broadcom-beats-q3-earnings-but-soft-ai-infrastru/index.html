Broadcom beats Q3 earnings but soft AI infrastructure guidance disappoints investors

Financial & Markets desk

Broadcom (AVGO) reported fiscal third-quarter earnings that topped analyst estimates, but a softer-than-expected current-quarter revenue forecast and margin compression sent shares down 6% to $346.49. The chipmaker posted revenue of $29.6 billion, up 86% year over year, and adjusted earnings of $3.32 per share, beating the Zacks Consensus Estimate of $3.22 per share by 3.11%. AI semiconductor revenue surged 221% year over year to $16.7 billion, now representing 56% of Broadcom’s total revenue.

The disappointment centered on the company’s outlook for the fiscal fourth quarter. Broadcom guided for revenue of $34.8 billion, below consensus expectations, while gross margin compressed to 73% from 78% a year ago. The guidance miss and margin pressure overshadowed the triple-digit AI growth, leading to a company-specific selloff rather than a broad sector decline. The iShares Semiconductor ETF (SOXX) fell just 1%, reinforcing that the reaction was tied to Broadcom’s own numbers.

Among AI peers, NVIDIA (NVDA) rose 1% to $227.69 as Broadcom’s custom-silicon commentary was seen as confirming the AI compute cycle. Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) slipped 2% to $448.68, and Marvell Technology (MRVL) fell 0.6% to $205.20, both declining on read-across concerns about custom-accelerator margins. Analysts noted that Broadcom’s management may be taking a conservative stance on its potential, with one analyst saying the company perhaps came up short of true 'market expectations.'

Broadcom’s stock has added about 6.8% since the start of the year, underperforming the S&P 500’s 11.5% gain. The company has now beaten consensus earnings estimates in each of the last four quarters. For the current fiscal year, the consensus EPS estimate stands at $11.74 on revenue of $106.07 billion, with the next quarter expected to deliver $3.83 per share on $35.16 billion in revenue. Based on mixed estimate revisions ahead of the report, Zacks ranks Broadcom a #3 (Hold), indicating shares are expected to perform in line with the market in the near term.

The earnings report and guidance underscore a split narrative: Broadcom’s AI business continues to expand rapidly, but investors are focused on the broader financial picture, including margin trends and the pace of infrastructure spending. With a $350 billion two-year hyperscaler book cited as confirmation of AI demand, the market’s reaction suggests that near-term execution and profitability are now under greater scrutiny.
