Record cyclosporiasis outbreak tests agencies hit by Trump-era cuts

Health & Science desk

The U.S. is facing a record outbreak of cyclosporiasis, a foodborne illness, and the response is testing federal agencies that oversee such outbreaks — agencies that were hit by funding cuts last year under the Trump administration, NPR reports.

The reductions in funding are now being put to the test as those agencies confront the outbreak, which has reached record levels. The situation highlights the potential strain on federal oversight of foodborne illness during a period of budget constraints.
