Google DeepMind Faces Restructuring, Potential Layoffs as Hassabis Pushes for Independent AI Safety Body

Tech / AI desk

Google DeepMind (GDM) is undergoing a major restructuring that will shift its focus away from developing cutting-edge, flagship AI models toward smaller, more cost-efficient Flash models, according to exclusive reporting from the Chinese outlet ifanr, republished by 36Kr. The reorganization is expected to result in large-scale layoffs, potentially affecting one-third or more of the team's 7,000 to 8,000 employees. People familiar with the matter said the cuts are intended to streamline redundant personnel, including algorithm hires who do not perform algorithm work. Employees may transfer internally to avoid layoffs, and some teams have already been merged into other Google business units.

Databricks raises $5B at $190B valuation, up from initial $1B target

Tech / AI desk

Databricks announced Thursday that it has raised $5 billion in a new funding round at a $190 billion valuation, far exceeding the $1 billion the company initially intended to raise. CEO and co-founder Ali Ghodsi told TechCrunch that the fundraising snowballed after The Information published an article about a potential raise during a company conference in June, triggering a flood of investor interest totaling $15 billion. To avoid alienating long-term backers, Databricks issued more stock and closed the round at a valuation that rose from the $188 billion disclosed in July.
