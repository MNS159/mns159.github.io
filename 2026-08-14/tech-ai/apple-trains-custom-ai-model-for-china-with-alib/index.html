Apple trains custom AI model for China with Alibaba's support

Tech / AI desk · anchored on Reuters

Apple has trained a custom artificial intelligence model for the Chinese market in partnership with domestic tech giant Alibaba, according to three unnamed people familiar with the matter. The rare U.S.-China collaboration comes as Apple prepares to roll out its on-device generative AI service, Apple Intelligence, in China, and marks a departure from the company’s previous strategy of using third-party Chinese models for its devices sold there.

The China-focused large language model was developed with Alibaba’s support, Reuters reported. By building its own model, Apple gains more control over its products in the competitive Chinese smartphone market, rather than relying on domestic models as it has historically done. U.S. models such as OpenAI’s ChatGPT are not available in China.

Apple officially registered the on-device generative AI service with China’s cyberspace regulator last month, clearing a major regulatory hurdle. Under Chinese law, AI models must be registered with and cleared by the government before public release. Sources told Reuters the rollout should happen “in the coming months after an update to its iOS operating system.”

The partnership gives Apple a leg up navigating Beijing’s difficult regulatory landscape, particularly amid escalating U.S.-China tensions surrounding AI development. If approved, Apple would become the first U.S. company permitted to offer a proprietary AI model in China, according to Reuters. Apple did not immediately respond to a request for comment from The Verge.
