FDA advisers vote on Grail's multi-cancer blood test

Health & Science desk

A key advisory committee to the Food and Drug Administration is meeting today to vote on whether the agency should approve the first multi-cancer blood test, made by Grail. The Galleri test, which costs $949, claims to detect up to 50 forms of cancer, many of which are not routinely screened for today. The panel of independent experts will hear from Grail, FDA officials and the public before making a recommendation; the FDA often follows such advice but is not required to.
