Trump says AI will be renamed 'super intelligence' in all U.S. documents

Tech / AI desk
LiberalConservative
Coverage tilts slightly center-left

President Donald Trump announced during his keynote speech at the United Nations General Assembly that he is renaming artificial intelligence to 'Super Intelligence' in all U.S. government documents. 'The use of the word artificial makes intelligence fake... It is not fake. It's actually amazing,' Trump said, according to CNBC.

The rebranding effort comes as Trump prepares to host Chinese President Xi Jinping in Washington, D.C., later this week, with AI safety and competition expected to be a key topic. The president has dismissed warnings about the technology's rapid development, calling them a 'hoax' and 'scam.'

Later Wednesday, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, and Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue are scheduled to address the United Nations Security Council to debate AI safety and regulation. The appearance follows growing calls to slow the pace of frontier AI models.

In a related development, OpenAI and Anthropic released new, lower-cost AI models on Tuesday. OpenAI introduced GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, cutting API prices by 50% compared with GPT-5.6 promotional pricing, with Sol for complex tasks and Luna for high-volume work. Anthropic unveiled Claude Opus 5.5, its latest offering in the Claude 5.5 family.

Investors responded positively, with technology stocks rallying and the Nasdaq hitting a new all-time intraday high and record close on Tuesday. Futures were flat in early Wednesday trading amid a broadly positive handover from Asia.
