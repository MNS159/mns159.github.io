Knicks raise championship banner, face 76ers on NBA Opening Night

Sports desk

The New York Knicks will raise their first championship banner in 53 years on Tuesday night at Madison Square Garden, celebrating the franchise’s 2026 NBA title before tipping off the 2026-27 season against the Philadelphia 76ers. The banner-raising and ring ceremony begin at approximately 6:30 p.m. ET, with tip-off scheduled for 7 p.m. ET. The game airs on NBC and streams on Peacock.

The Knicks swept the 76ers in the Eastern Conference semifinals last season en route to the championship, their first since the 1972-73 campaign. New York enters the season with its top nine scorers returning, including Jalen Brunson, Mikal Bridges and Josh Hart — the first trio of teammates to win both an NCAA and NBA title together (Villanova). The Knicks replaced center Mitchell Robinson with Andre Drummond in the offseason.

Philadelphia underwent a dramatic overhaul, trading for Jaylen Brown and signing LeBron James in free agency. James, a multi-time NBA MVP, joins Joel Embiid, Tyrese Maxey and V.J. Edgecombe to form a loaded starting five. The 76ers have not reached the conference finals in 15 consecutive playoff appearances, tied for the third-longest such streak in league history. The franchise’s last championship came in 1983, when Moses Malone led the team to the title in his first season after joining as a multi-time MVP.

The Knicks enter as 4.5-point favorites at home, according to DraftKings, with the over/under set at 232.5 points. The SportsLine Projection Model forecasts a 116-109 Knicks victory. LeBron James is projected for 20.3 points, 6.8 rebounds and 6.3 assists; his points prop is set at 15.5. Jaylen Brown’s over/under is 20.5 points in his Sixers debut, with the model projecting 24.3. Joel Embiid is at 22.5 (model: 22.9) and Tyrese Maxey at 21.5 (model: 24.4). No NBA team has ever had four players average 20 or more points per game in a season.

Nationally, the 76ers have drawn the heaviest betting action to win the title, with 37.6% of handle and 30.9% of bets at BetMGM, making them the book’s biggest liability. The Thunder and Spurs are +260 co-favorites, with Philadelphia at +850. The Knicks are +1000 to repeat. Tickets for the game are among the most expensive in NBA regular-season history. The NBA has had eight different champions in eight seasons, the first such stretch in league history; no team has won back-to-back titles since the 2017-18 Warriors.

Tuesday’s game is part of an NBA Opening Night tripleheader that also includes the Boston Celtics at Detroit Pistons (3 p.m. ET) and the Oklahoma City Thunder at San Antonio Spurs (9:30 p.m. ET). The 2026-27 season marks the return of NBA coverage to NBC and Peacock, with 100 regular-season games airing across three marquee nights: Peacock NBA Monday, Coast 2 Coast Tuesday, and Sunday Night Basketball.
